Once a day it asks each company with a key which models it may use and reads two public price lists, LiteLLM’s and OpenRouter’s, taking a price when they agree. New models then appear here and new prices are counted, and admins are told on Telegram when a model in use is going or its price moves. No model call, and nothing about the family is sent. Off, the prices built into this version are used.
