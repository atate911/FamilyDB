Google only lets an app into a calendar through a “client” you make in Google Cloud. It is free, and you do it once. Sign in as the Google account that owns the family calendar throughout.

  1. Make a project: name it {{ site_title }} and press Create. Wait for it to finish, and check that it is the project chosen at the top of the page.
  2. Turn on the Google Calendar API: press Enable.
  3. Set up the sign-in screen: press Get started. App name: {{ site_title }}; support email: yours. Audience: External. Contact email: yours. Agree, and Create.
  4. Publish it: press Publish app and confirm. Do not skip this: left in “Testing”, Google cuts the connection off after seven days.
  5. Make the client: Create client, application type Desktop app, name {{ site_title }}, Create. Then press Download JSON, open the file it saves, and copy everything in it.
