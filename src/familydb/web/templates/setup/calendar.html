1. Give {{ site_title }} its own account at Google

Google lets an app into a calendar through a “service account”: a robot account with an address of its own, which you then share the calendar with. It is free, it never asks anybody to sign in, and it does not expire. Sign in to Google Cloud as any account you like.

  1. Make a project: name it {{ site_title }} and press Create. Wait for it to finish, and check that it is the project chosen at the top of the page.

  2. Turn on the Google Calendar API: press Enable.

  3. Make the service account: Create service account, name it {{ site_title }}, press Create and continue, then Done: it needs no role.

  4. Open the account you just made, then Keys, Add key, Create new key, JSON. A file is saved, ending in .json: keep it for the last part.

2. Share the family calendar with it

  1. In Google Calendar’s settings, choose the family calendar under Settings for my calendars.

  2. Under Share with specific people, add the service account’s address: it is the client_email line in the file, ending iam.gserviceaccount.com. Set its permission to Make changes to events.

  3. Further down, under Integrate calendar, copy the Calendar ID.

3. Give both to {{ site_title }}

{{ site_title }} tries them on the calendar before it keeps them, so if something is not right it says what.
