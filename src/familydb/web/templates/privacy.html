Who runs it

This is a private copy of FamilyDB, run by a family for itself on a computer the family controls. There is no company behind this copy, no advertising, and nothing is sold. Only people the family's admin has added can use it. Questions about your information go to that admin, who can see and remove it.

What it keeps

Everything is kept in one database on the family's own computer, and in its backups:

  • The family list: each person's name, their role (admin, parent or kid), their Telegram id if they use Telegram, and, if an admin adds them, a birthday and, for a kid, whether she is a girl or a boy. A password is kept only as a one-way hash.
  • The conversation: messages sent to it and its replies, on Telegram and on this page, and a record of each step it took to answer (which tool it used, with what, and what came back).
  • What the family saves: ideas, plans, things to do and their reminders, how outings went, the kids' wish lists, and short notes it remembers about the family.
  • Where someone is, only when they share it: a position shared on Telegram, or sent from this page with "Send where I am" ticked, is used for three hours and deleted after a day.
  • Voice notes and photos: the words written down from them are kept; the recording and the picture are not.
  • Costs: how much each request to an AI company was estimated to cost.
  • Someone not on the list who writes to its Telegram bot: their Telegram name and id, for 30 days, so an admin can choose to add them.

Signing in to this page sets two cookies: one keeps you signed in, and one remembers that this browser has signed in before, so that guessing at passwords elsewhere does not lock you out. An unsent message may be kept in your browser's storage while the tab is open. There is no analytics and no tracking.

Where it goes

To answer, {{ site_title }} uses a few outside services. Each gets only what it needs:

  • An AI company (today {{ company }}; the family may choose OpenAI, Anthropic or Google, and another it has a key for may answer when the first cannot) receives each message with what is needed to answer it: the family's names and roles, the saved ideas, the notes it remembers, the recent conversation, a kid's age, a shared position, and calendar events it looked up. Voice notes and photos go to it to be written down. Looking an idea up on the web goes through the AI company's web search. The family pays for this directly under that company's terms for its API.
  • Google Calendar, if the family connects it (see below).
  • Telegram carries the messages of anyone who talks to the bot there, under Telegram's own terms.
  • Open-Meteo receives the home area's position for the weather forecast, and OpenStreetMap's Nominatim receives place names to find, and a shared position to name.

Nothing is shared with anyone else, sold, or used for advertising.

Google Calendar

If the family connects a Google account, {{ site_title }} asks Google for two permissions: to see and change events (calendar.events), and to see the list of calendars (calendar.readonly). It uses them only to:

  • read what is on the family calendar, so it does not suggest something when the family is busy, and to show what is coming up on this page;
  • add, move and cancel the family's plans on the calendar the family chose, when they ask;
  • list the account's calendars once, so the family can choose which one to use.

Calendar events it reads may be sent to the AI company above only to answer the family's own question, such as "are we free on Saturday?". They are not used to train AI models, not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared for any other purpose. The access Google grants is stored on the family's computer and can be taken back at any time from the Google account's Third-party apps and services page.

{{ site_title }}'s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Kids

A kid's account is made by a parent or admin, who can read her conversation with {{ site_title }} and her wish lists. Of her details, only her age and whether she is a girl or a boy reach the AI company, never her birthday.

How long, and how to remove it

What is kept stays until it is removed, apart from shared positions (a day) and a stranger's name and id (30 days). To have something removed, ask the admin. A note it remembers can be forgotten on the What she remembers page. Taking a person off the family list removes what was theirs alone (their name, sign-in, Telegram link, where they last were, and what it remembered about them) and keeps what they said with no name on it. The admin can delete anything else from the database on the family's computer, and taking {{ site_title }} off that computer removes everything.
