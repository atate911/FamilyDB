# Outside services

FamilyDB reaches four kinds of outside service: model companies, Telegram, Google Calendar, and public weather, map and price lists. What each one receives is on [What leaves the house](/wiki/security/what-leaves-the-house).

| Service | What FamilyDB uses it for | Required? | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Model companies (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) | Understanding sentences, wording replies, looking ideas up, hearing voice notes and reading photos | Yes, a key for at least one | [Model companies](/wiki/boundaries/model-companies) |
| Telegram | The family's chat | No; the web page's Chat works without it | [Telegram as a service](/wiki/boundaries/telegram) |
| Google Calendar | A shared family calendar that confirmed plans are copied to | No; without it the page shows the saved plans | [Google Calendar](/wiki/boundaries/google-calendar) |
| Weather, maps and price lists | The forecast, the map lookup of your town, and current model prices | No account or key | [Weather, maps and price lists](/wiki/boundaries/weather-and-maps) |
