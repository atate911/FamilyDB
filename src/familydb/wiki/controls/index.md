# Using FamilyDB

Everything the family does day to day is on one of these pages or in Telegram. The first nine are the family's, in one frame: the box to tell Vera something first, the page, and a row of destinations at the foot (on a wide screen, down the side). The rest are the back office, behind your picture; Family, Settings and Setup are for admins. What a page shows depends on the person's role: [Roles and permissions](/wiki/model/family-and-roles#roles-and-permissions) says who may do what.

| Page | What it is for | Who |
|---|---|---|
| [Now](/wiki/controls/home-and-chat) | The box to tell Vera something, what is waiting on you, her picks for right now, today and tomorrow | Everyone |
| [What about](/wiki/controls/home-and-chat#her-picks) | All of her picks, for the next few hours and the weekend, made ahead | Everyone |
| [The board](/wiki/controls/home-and-chat#on-a-big-screen-and-the-kitchen-tablet) | Now for a tablet on the kitchen wall, with a face for each person on the box | Everyone |
| [Chat](/wiki/controls/home-and-chat) | The conversation behind the box; every message is paid for | Anyone who may chat |
| [Eat](/wiki/controls/ideas) | Where should we eat? A pick for the next meal, then every saved restaurant with the one fact that decides | Everyone reads; parents and admins change |
| [Do](/wiki/controls/ideas) | What could we do? What fits the next free stretch on top, then the whole pile; open one for its hours, drive and booking link | Everyone reads; parents and admins change |
| [Week](/wiki/controls/plans-and-tasks) | This week, a day at a time: what is on, the free time, the reminders due, and what is on your plate | Everyone; a kid sees her own things |
| [Kids](/wiki/controls/wish-lists) | Each kid's asks and lists; a kid's own is My list | Parents answer; kids keep |
| [Soon](/wiki/controls/plans-and-tasks#soon) | What is on near home in the weeks ahead, by day, with the free time | Parents and admins |
| [Lists](/wiki/controls/plans-and-tasks#lists) | The shopping list and the family's other lists, with big ticks | Parents and admins |
| [Did](/wiki/controls/plans-and-tasks#did) | What we did, with how each outing went; what is still to rate first | Everyone |
| [What Vera knows](/wiki/controls/memory) | What the assistant remembers about the family, with a way to forget any of it | Parents and admins |
| [Status](/wiki/controls/status) | What is connected, what the models have cost, what needs a look, recent activity | Parents and admins |
| [Guide](/wiki) | This guide | Parents and admins |
| [Settings](/wiki/controls/settings) | Every setting that can change from the web page | Admins |
| [Family](/wiki/controls/family) | Who the assistant talks to, who signs in, and each person's password | Admins |
| [Setup](/wiki/controls/setup) | A new install, one step at a time | Admins |
| [Look and your password](/wiki/controls/look-and-password) | How the page looks for you, and your own password | Everyone signed in |

Telegram is the other way in: say what you want in your own words, use the commands in its menu, or tap the buttons under a reminder. [Telegram: commands and buttons](/wiki/controls/telegram) has them all.
