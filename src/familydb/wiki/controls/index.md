# Using FamilyDB

Everything the family does day to day is on one of these pages or in Telegram; Family, Settings and Setup are for admins. What a page shows depends on the person's role: [Roles and permissions](/wiki/model/family-and-roles#roles-and-permissions) says who may do what.

| Page | What it is for | Who |
|---|---|---|
| [Home](/wiki/controls/home-and-chat) | The box to ask Vera something, with what is coming up, what is left to do and what was added lately | Everyone |
| [Chat](/wiki/controls/home-and-chat) | The conversation itself; every message is paid for | Anyone who may chat |
| [Ideas](/wiki/controls/ideas) | Search, add and fix ideas; open one for its hours, travel time and booking link | Everyone reads; parents and admins change |
| [Plans](/wiki/controls/plans-and-tasks) | The family calendar as a list or a month, read from Google | Everyone reads; parents and admins change |
| [To do](/wiki/controls/plans-and-tasks) | Things to do and their reminders; a tick marks one done | Everyone; a kid sees only their own |
| [Wish lists](/wiki/controls/wish-lists) | A kid keeps their own wishes; a parent answers them | Kids keep; parents answer |
| [What Vera knows](/wiki/controls/memory) | What the assistant remembers about the family, with a way to forget any of it | Parents and admins |
| [Status](/wiki/controls/status) | What is connected, what the models have cost, what needs a look, recent activity | Parents and admins |
| [Guide](/wiki) | This guide | Parents and admins |
| [Settings](/wiki/controls/settings) | Every setting that can change from the web page | Admins |
| [Family](/wiki/controls/family) | Who the assistant talks to, who signs in, and each person's password | Admins |
| [Setup](/wiki/controls/setup) | A new install, one step at a time | Admins |
| [Look and your password](/wiki/controls/look-and-password) | How the page looks for you, and your own password | Everyone signed in |

Telegram is the other way in: say what you want in your own words, use the commands in its menu, or tap the buttons under a reminder. [Telegram: commands and buttons](/wiki/controls/telegram) has them all.
