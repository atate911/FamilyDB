# Wish lists

Each kid keeps what they would like on lists of their own, and a parent answers when they choose. A parent or admin opens **Kids**; a kid opens **My list**, which holds only their own. A kid's ask, and a request flagged Not OK, come up as her question on Now too, with the same Yes and Not this time. The rules behind both are in [Wish-list rules](/wiki/model/wishes).

## What each person sees

| Who | What they can do |
|---|---|
| A kid, on **My list** | Tell the assistant what they would like, in a box at the top. Reorder (Top, Up, Down, or drag where scripts run), move a wish to another list, take one off. See **Answers** (the last 30 days) and **Not on your list**. A line says which parents can see the list |
| A parent or admin, on **Kids** | See each kid's top three on each list, and open a kid for their full lists. Add, reorder, move and take off for them, and answer. **Flagged** shows what the assistant turned away, **Answers** what was answered; both show the last 30 days |

Each kid has three lists: **Every day**, **Christmas** and **Birthday**. A kid never sees another kid's list. Kids switched off on [Family](/wiki/controls/family) are left out. A **to decide** badge counts what waits on a parent. A parent can read a kid's conversation on Chat.

## Ask for something as a kid

1. Write in the box on any page, Now or **My list** say: "I'd like roller skates."
2. The assistant sorts it. A want of their own goes on a list. Something the family could do together becomes an idea or a [thing to do](/wiki/reference/glossary#thing-to-do), and "sushi for my birthday" is both a birthday wish and an idea.
3. The assistant answers at once, briefly, at the kid's age.

Adding goes through the assistant, so it counts as one of the kid's messages. Moving and taking off cost nothing and still work when a limit is reached. If a wish is turned away, **Not on your list** says what to do next, and for a fair ask there is an **Ask a parent** button, once per ask. How the assistant guides wording and what it turns away are in [Wish-list rules](/wiki/model/wishes#how-the-assistant-sorts-an-ask).

## Answer a wish as a parent

1. Answer on Now, where her ask waits as a question, or open **Kids** and choose a kid, or follow the **to decide** count on Kids in the row. It counts kid asks and "Not OK" requests from the last 30 days that no parent has answered.
2. Tap a wish's line to open it, add an optional note (up to 500 characters), and choose **Yes!** or **Not this time**. On a kid's own page, an ask they sent you and a request flagged "Not OK" both show under **Flagged** with the same two buttons.
3. The kid is told in their own conversation, in the assistant's words with your note and, for no, the day they may ask again. The wish moves to **Answers** for 30 days.

A kid sees neither these buttons nor "Not OK".

Only two things reach a parent's phone, each sent to every parent and admin with a Telegram id: an inappropriate request, sent straight away, and a kid's Ask a parent. Each carries **Yes!**, **Not this time** and **Later**. A tap answers as the person who tapped, with no model call; Later leaves it on the page. A new wish sends nothing, so look when you choose.

## Add a wish for a kid

1. Open **Kids** and choose a kid (each kid has an address of their own, such as `/kids/maya`).
2. Type the wish in **Add something for <name>**, choose a list, and choose **Add**.

This makes no model call and costs nothing. It follows the same rules as the kid's own asks, except the lock and the daily count: [Wish-list rules](/wiki/model/wishes#the-rules-code-holds) has them.

## Change the limits

The daily count, list length, how often a kid may ask a parent and the wording nudge are set by an admin on [Spending](/wiki/controls/settings/spending#the-kids-wish-lists). The lockout after a "not this time" is fixed in code: [the ladder](/wiki/model/wishes#the-lockout-ladder).

## Presents

A kid's list is not secret: the parents can see it, and the page says so. A present for a kid is not a wish. Add it as a gift idea on [Do](/wiki/controls/ideas), where it is kept from every kid ([Presents](/wiki/model/ideas-and-places#presents)).

## Taking a kid off the list

Taking a kid off the family list for good deletes their wish lists, and switching them off keeps them ([Family](/wiki/controls/family#switch-off-or-take-off-for-good)).
