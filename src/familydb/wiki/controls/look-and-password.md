# Look and your password

Two pages belong to each person alone: **Look**, where you choose how the web page looks for you, and **Your password**, where you choose the password you sign in with. Everybody signed in as themselves has both, a kid included. Neither is a family setting, and the assistant cannot change either.

## Look

Open **Look** from your account menu, or from the menu on a phone. It has two parts: **Day and night**, then a grid of looks, each drawn as a small sample page. Pick one of each and press **Use this look**.

A look changes color and nothing else. The layout, the words, what each page holds and what each button does stay the same. Red means late or broken in every look, and Vera's dark screen keeps its own colors in all of them.

### Day and night

| Choice | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Match my device** | Day when the phone or computer is set to day, night when it is set to night. Everybody starts here |
| **Always day** | The light version, whatever the device says |
| **Always night** | The dark version, whatever the device says |

Phosphor and Afterglow are green-screen looks, so they are always night whatever you choose, and their samples say "Night only".

### The looks

| Look | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen Table** | The default. Cream paper by day, charcoal at night, deep green for what you act on, each person in their own color |
| **Phosphor** | The page as first drawn: charcoal with a breath of green, like an old green screen |
| **Afterglow** | Kitchen Table as charcoal glass with faint scanlines, and the assistant the one thing that glows |
| **Midnight** | A blue-black page and soft-white ink |
| **Home Computer** | An early-80s family machine: putty, brown-black and one orange key for "this one" |
| **Ink** | Almost no color: a white page and black ink |
| **Enamel** | Old enamel signs: warm gray plaster and a deep petrol panel |
| **Rail yellow** | Station signage: a deep rail-blue panel and one signal yellow for what you act on next |
| **Fjord** | A pale northern sky over deep slate water, with fjord blue for what you act on |

### Where your choice is kept

- **Signed in as yourself:** it is kept with your name, so it follows you to every phone and computer you sign in on. Everybody else keeps their own.
- **Before anybody signs in:** the sign-in page wears the look this browser last used. Signing in sets that browser's look from your saved choice, or clears it if you have not chosen one.
- **While the family still shares one password:** nobody in particular is signed in, so the choice stays in this browser for 365 days, and each screen can have its own. The page says which of the two applies.

There is no household default: each person picks, and anything unrecognized falls back to Kitchen Table.

## Your password

Open **Your password** from your account menu, which shows it only when you are signed in as yourself. While the family shares one password, an admin reaches it from [Family](/wiki/controls/family) or Setup step 2.

### Choose your own password

1. Type **the password you use now**, unless you signed in with a starting password. A wrong one is refused, and too many wrong tries make you wait 15 minutes.
2. Type **a new password** of 12 to 200 characters. A short sentence is easy to remember and hard to guess, like *pancakes on sunday mornings*.
3. Type **the same again**, which must match.

Saving it replaces the old one at once, and you stay signed in on this browser. Every other phone and computer signed in as you is signed out on its next click.

### Sign in with a starting password

An admin makes a [starting password](/wiki/reference/glossary#starting-password) for you on [Family](/wiki/controls/family#passwords). It is made up, shown once, and works only to get you in.

1. Sign in with your name and the starting password.
2. You land on a page headed **Choose your own password**. Nothing else opens until you have.
3. Save a password of your own. The starting one stops working, and you go to Home (an admin with setup unfinished lands on Setup).

### Being signed out elsewhere

A phone or computer is signed out, and asks for a password on its next click, when:

- you choose a new password, or an admin makes you a new starting one;
- an admin takes your password away or switches you off;
- an admin uses **Sign everyone out**;
- it goes unused for longer than the days a sign-in lasts, 30 days unless an admin changed it.

### While the family still shares one password

Until an admin has a password of their own, everybody signs in with the [shared password](/wiki/reference/glossary#shared-password), and this page is for an admin to choose theirs. It asks which admin you are when there is more than one, and does not ask for a current password.

> **Choosing it ends the shared password for everyone, unless you restore an older backup.** It stops opening anything and everybody signed in with it is signed out. From then on each person signs in as themselves, with a starting password from you. Choose yours first, then make the starting passwords on Family.

### If you forget it

A kid asks a parent, and anybody else asks an admin, who makes a new starting password. The only admin follows [Recovery](/wiki/operations/recovery).
