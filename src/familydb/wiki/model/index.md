# The pieces

FamilyDB keeps seven kinds of thing: ideas, plans, things to do, memories, wish lists, the family and Vera. Each has a page with its rules and a link to the page where you change it.

| Piece | In one line | Rules | Where you use it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ideas | A possibility, such as a restaurant or a day trip | [Ideas and places](/wiki/model/ideas-and-places) | [Ideas](/wiki/controls/ideas) |
| Plans | An idea tied to an event on the calendar | [Plans and the calendar](/wiki/model/plans-and-calendar) | [Plans and To do](/wiki/controls/plans-and-tasks) |
| Things to do | An obligation with a reminder | [Things to do and reminders](/wiki/model/tasks-and-reminders) | [Plans and To do](/wiki/controls/plans-and-tasks) |
| Memories | Short facts the family told the assistant about itself | [How memory works](/wiki/model/memory) | [What Vera knows](/wiki/controls/memory) |
| Wish lists | What each kid asks for, answered by the parents | [Wish-list rules](/wiki/model/wishes) | [Wish lists](/wiki/controls/wish-lists) |
| The family | Who the assistant talks to, who signs in, and what each role may do | [The family and roles](/wiki/model/family-and-roles) | [Family](/wiki/controls/family) |
| Vera | The name, character and wording of everything said unasked | [Vera](/wiki/model/vera) | [Personality and family](/wiki/controls/settings/personality) |
| Shared location | Where a phone says a person is, kept briefly | [Shared location](/wiki/model/location) | [Now and Chat](/wiki/controls/home-and-chat#share-where-i-am) |
| State and the database | The one file that holds all of it | [State and the database](/wiki/model/state-and-database) | [Backup and restore](/wiki/operations/backup-and-restore) |
