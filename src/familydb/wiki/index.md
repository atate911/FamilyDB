# Start here

FamilyDB is your family's planning assistant. Vera (that is her name unless you rename her) lives in Telegram and on the web page: she keeps your ideas, plans and reminders and suggests what to do this weekend. This guide is for the parents and admins who use her, and goes deepest for the admin who runs the server. Kids never see it.

This guide is served by your own FamilyDB, so it needs a sign-in and always matches the version you run. If nobody can sign in, read `/opt/familydb/RUNBOOK.md` on the server, or [Recovery](/wiki/operations/recovery).

The badge at the top of each page says who it is for (For admins, or For parents and admins) and how deep it goes: Overview, How-to or Deep dive. The search box at the top of every page reads the whole guide, so a setting's name, an error message or a term finds its page.
