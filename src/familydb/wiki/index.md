# Start here

This is the guide to how FamilyDB works and how to look after it. It is written
for the parents and admins who use it, and goes deepest for the admins who run
it. Kids never see it, by the family's own decision: how the bot works, or why
it doesn't, is not theirs to worry about.

**This guide is new.** It is being
written one section at a time. Each section's first page says what it covers
and what is still to come, and until a page exists the developer documents in
`docs/` are the full account (the [map](/wiki/reference/dev-docs) says which).

## Ways in

**Understand how it works**

- [The big picture](/wiki/overview): what FamilyDB is, and the shape of it
- [The pieces](/wiki/model): ideas, plans, things to do, what she remembers, the family
- [What it does on its own](/wiki/behavior): the jobs that run without being asked
- [What it touches](/wiki/boundaries): the model companies, Telegram, Google, the weather, and what leaves the house

**Run it, or put it right**

- [How you control it](/wiki/controls): every page, Telegram's commands and the command line
- [Running it over time](/wiki/operations): install, back up, upgrade, look after the server
- [Security and trust](/wiki/security): who may do what, and what is guarded

**Look something up**

- [Reference](/wiki/reference): the [glossary](/wiki/reference/glossary) and a [map to the developer docs](/wiki/reference/dev-docs)
- The search box at the top of every page reads the whole guide, so a setting's name, an error message or a term will find its page.

## If you cannot sign in

This guide sits behind the same sign-in as the rest of the page, so it cannot
help you from the outside. Putting an admin's access right is done on the
server: see [Running it over time](/wiki/operations#if-an-admin-is-locked-out).

## How the pages are laid out

Each page opens general and goes down into specifics, so you can stop at the
overview or read on for the detail and the reason. The badge at the top says who
a page is for ("For admins", "For parents and admins") and how deep it goes
(Overview, How-to, Deep dive). The guide summarizes and links; the exact
commands and the code-level detail stay in the developer documents, which are
the source of truth where the two overlap.
