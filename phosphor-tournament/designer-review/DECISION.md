# Decision

The owner chose **Kitchen Table (green)** (`fresh/a1`, from scratch, description only) as the look for FamilyDB, 4 October 2026. Phosphor is not abandoned as a design system; this is a verdict on this app.

For: three blind judges ranked it 2nd, 3rd and 1st of the designs they saw (marks 8, 8 and 9), and it was praised for its wording ("6 days late" on each late to-do, "Not you? Switch" for a shared laptop, a Settings page that explains its tags).

Caveats that go with it: every judge and designer is the same kind of AI, and cream paper with a soft serif and a tomato accent is a look such systems tend to produce. It is a mockup of seven pages, not the app, and nothing under `src/` has changed. See `viewer/` (Modes compared) for the full comparison.

## Update: the final design

Kitchen Table (green) was made final in `final/`: twenty-four pages, a written standard and a brand. The brand brings back FamilyDB's own smiling-monitor mark and gives Vera a small screen of her own, with phosphor green kept for FamilyDB, Vera and what is live, and warm paper for everything the family keeps. Blind checks at each step chose the final over the original (4 of 4) and the refined brand over the plain final (4 of 4). See `final/README.md`.
