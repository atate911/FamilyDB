# Tomato Table

**Idea:** the same kitchen table in late summer: warm cream and brown ink, one ripe tomato for everything the family does, and Vera as its green sprout, a small lit screen on warm charcoal glass.

## What changed from Kitchen Table, and why

- **Tomato is the family's accent, used only as paint.** It fills primary buttons, Send, today's date tile and calendar disc, the current nav row and tab, ticks on hover and checkboxes, and it underlines every link. It replaces Kitchen Table green and brings back the reference's warmth. Link words stay ink: tomato never colours text.
- **Late is brick words with a filled clock.** Red *text* means only late or broken. Late is deep brick (#8E2A16, 15 L* darker than the tomato). It is always written out ("6 days late"), always led by a filled clock that nothing else uses, and the row keeps its 4 px brick rule. Tomato marks something you press. Brick marks a sentence with a clock. At night, late is coral text and tomato is a dark fill.
- **Vera stays undrawn.** The reference's little tomato is gone. Her screen keeps its phosphor green, now on warmer charcoal glass (#1A1310). On a warm page, the one cool green is how you spot her. The mark's square and the favicons are warmed to match.
- **The Ask card** is warm charcoal glass with Vera's screen and a tomato Send: her pane, the family's button.
- **Dark mode is warm:** charcoal-brown in place of green-black, cream ink, a tomato rim on the Ask card, and people's tints warmed.
- **Look:** brown-black ink, soft Fraunces terminals on card titles too, slightly rounder corners, and a warm card shadow.

Pages, words and states are unchanged. Contrast is in STANDARD §7.

## Where it is weaker

- **Red is used twice.** Lightness, form and the clock keep brick and tomato apart, but a tomato button beside a late row still shows two reds. Kitchen Table's green against red had no such tension.
- Theo's orange (slot 4) is close to the tomato (ΔE 23). It's softened by his initial, but not removed.
- Tomato passes contrast with little margin: white on tomato is 4.8:1, and at night tomato on a card is 3.4:1.
- By day the Ask card is a dark brown block: warm, but heavier than the reference's white box.
