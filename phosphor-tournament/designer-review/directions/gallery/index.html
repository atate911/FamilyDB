Which to build

Kitchen Table, green is the one with evidence behind it, and the safest pick.

Tomato Table is the warmest. Its weak spot is two reds side by side: the tomato button and a late to-do in brick.

Afterglow is the most distinctive and the most like FamilyDB’s own mark. It costs warmth and daylight reading, and its light theme needs a setting the app does not have yet.
