# Three directions

The finished Kitchen Table design (`../final/design`) in three looks, each across the same twenty-four pages, day and night, desktop and phone.

- **Kitchen Table, green** (`../final`): the final. Warm paper, a deep green card for Vera with a sun-yellow Send, small panes of phosphor glass for FamilyDB and Vera. Chosen blind over the original 4 of 4, and with its brand 4 of 4.
- **Tomato Table** (`tomato/`): the same table in late summer: cream, brown ink, one tomato for everything the family does, Vera as a small lit screen on warm charcoal. Its ancestor scored highest of twenty blind. Weakness: a tomato button beside a late to-do (brick) shows two reds.
- **Afterglow** (`afterglow/`): phosphor first, a calm green screen day and night, with a light theme (`data-theme="light"`). Most distinctive; costs warmth and daylight readability, and its light theme needs a per-person setting the app does not have.

Each direction's `BRIEF.md` is what its designer was given, `DIRECTION.md` what they decided and why, `reply.md` their report. `compare/index.html` puts the three side by side for seven screens (the images are in `compare/img/`). Neither new direction has been judged blind.
