Three directions for FamilyDB

The same finished design (twenty-four pages, the same content, phone layout, kids' pages and rules) in three looks. Kitchen Table, green is the final; the other two take the strongest alternatives from the blind judging and carry them into the finished system. Pick a screen, then compare.

Which to build

Kitchen Table, green is the one with evidence behind it: chosen by the family, preferred to the original by four of four blind judges, and with its brand by four of four again. It is the safest pick.

Tomato Table is the warmest and has the best score of any look in the blind field, but in this system it has one real weakness: a tomato button beside a late to-do shows two reds, told apart only by shade, the clock icon and the words.

Afterglow is the Phosphor Interface grown up: calm, distinctive, and the most "FamilyDB". It costs warmth and daylight readability, the pixel titles are a taste call, and its light theme needs a per-person setting the app doesn't have yet.

None of the two new directions has been judged blind yet; that can be done the same way as before if one of them tempts you.
