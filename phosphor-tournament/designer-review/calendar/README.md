# Kitchen Table, as a calendar

The family said the Kitchen Table interface is nearly perfect, but it reads like a recipe or gardening site; they want it to feel like a home calendar or house-management app. Two designers restyled the finished design (`../final/design`) with every page, word and layout kept:

- **Kitchen Wall** (`wall/`): the family calendar on the kitchen wall. A bright, ruled grid; today is green and every other colour is a person; one-person plans are solid blocks in that person's colour. Headings, dates, times and money in Manrope.
- **Today Line** (`app/`): a calendar app. A cool, gridded surface, one green that means "now", events marked with a colour bar and tint. Headings, dates, times and money in Inter; words stay Atkinson.

Each has its `BRIEF.md`, `DIRECTION.md` (what changed and where it is weaker), `reply.md` and an updated `STANDARD.md`. `compare/index.html` shows both beside Kitchen Table as it is. Neither has been judged blind.
