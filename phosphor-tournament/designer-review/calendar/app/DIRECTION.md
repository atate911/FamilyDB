# Today Line

**The idea:** Kitchen Table's pages, set like the family's calendar app: dates and times in a precise grotesk on a cool, gridded surface, with one green that means "now".

## What changed, and why

**Type, above all.** Fraunces is gone; a soft serif at 42 px was the recipe-site tell. Headings, dates, clock times, money and the wordmark are now **Inter** (one 43 KB variable file, Latin, OFL). It's the open cousin of the grotesks phone and desktop calendars use, so "Sun 4" and "12:30 pm" look like dates anywhere else on the family's screens. Its figures were drawn for interfaces: tabular where they line up, a centred colon, an open zero. `cv05` and `cv08` (l with a tail, I with serifs) repeat Atkinson's disambiguation. Atkinson keeps every word. The rule: **words are Atkinson, time is Inter.**

**Surface.** Cream, beige lines and warm fills became a cool grey page, white cards and hairlines, with no resting shadows. Radii went from 18/12 to 12/8, and buttons are rounded rectangles. Round is kept for people and Vera's pill.

**Colour.** Sun yellow is gone. Green stays because it's the only hue nobody owns (eight people, red for late, amber for setup), now a cooler signal green used like a calendar's "now" line: today's date, today's cell (a disc and a 2 px top line), the next plan's tile band, the current page, links and the primary button. The Ask card is charcoal glass with a lit Send, like a command bar. The setup card is white with an amber rule. Events wear people's colours the calendar way: a 3 px bar and a 16 % tint.

**Calendar forms.** Date tiles are calendar leaves; weekday heads are tracked Inter capitals; numbers are tabular. Nothing new is shown.

**Brand.** The mark and Vera's screen are unchanged; on grey they read as an app icon. The wordmark is Inter 700, set tight, and keeps its cursor.

## Where it is weaker

- Less distinctive: Inter on grey is many apps' house style. The warmth now lives only in Atkinson, the people's colours and Vera.
- One green for the app and for Vera blurs them by day; phosphor and glass separate them only at night.
- Atkinson's quirks beside Inter's regularity can look mismatched at row-title sizes.
- Yellow "set this up" banners remain the warmest thing on some pages.
