# The calendar, with joy

The family liked the calendar look but found it sterile: "like an AI-designed app, functional but plain, no real soul; the typography fine but uninspired. Can we make it a little more joyous to use?" Two designers each took one calendar take (`../calendar/`) and gave it a soul, keeping every page, word and rule:

- **Kitchen Wall, Felt Tip** (`wall/`, from Kitchen Wall): the wall calendar filled in by everyone with their own felt-tip pen; today circled in green, people's plans as bright stickers, things crossed off with a drawn line.
- **Fridge Door** (`app/`, from Today Line): cards like stickers on a 3 px lip, tear-off date leaves, a die-cut tile per idea kind, and whoever is signed in wearing their own colour.

Both chose Bricolage Grotesque for headings, dates and money (Atkinson still sets every word) and a lilac planner page with a faint dot grid. Each folder has its `BRIEF.md`, `DIRECTION.md`, `reply.md` and updated `STANDARD.md`; `screens/index.html` shows them in full. Neither has been judged blind.
