# One blind judge, all sixteen

One fresh Opus session, run outside the repo, given only "You are an expert creative graphic and interface designer", a three-sentence description of the app, and the sixteen designs as pictures under shuffled codes (X01 to X16): an overview of the seven pages on desktop and phone, plus every page full length. It had no names, no notes, no Phosphor concept, and was not told which were built on the real app and which were mockups, or that one of them is today's page. `BRIEF.md` is exactly what it was given; `review.md` is its full answer; `key.json` maps codes to designs.

## What to make of it

- The judge's top seven are the six from-scratch mockups plus Signal, the one real-app design that ranked well. Every other real-app design sits at 6.5 or below, and today's page is 15th of 16.
- Read that with care. A mockup is a hand-built set of seven finished pages. A real-app build has to dress the app's own markup, so its lower pages (Status especially) fall back to raw tables, native date and select controls and so on. The judge named exactly those as the common weaknesses. Some of the gap is the design and some is the medium.
- The best dark designs it found were from scratch and told the Phosphor idea (Afterglow scanlines 8, Kitchen Terminal 7.5, Afterglow smiling terminal 7), and the warm light designs from scratch did best of all (8.5, 8, 7). The one it called odd: thirteen of sixteen are green on black, which it thought an unusual default for a family app with children.
- Its three families: warm light cards with a sidebar (the three Kitchen Tables); dark green terminal with a sidebar and pixel type (the from-scratch Afterglows and Kitchen Terminal); dark with a top bar and a glowing next-up screen (ten of the sixteen, including every sketch and team entry).
- Things it liked across designs: "6 days late" on each late to-do, "Leave by 12:30" and a countdown on the next plan, the radar or line of ideas by drive time, the summary sentence under the greeting, one-tap "How did it go?" answers.
- Its first change to its own pick (Kitchen Table, tomato): show "N days late" in red on each late to-do on Home, as the green Kitchen Table does.
- One judge, one run, one shuffle. The order of the codes can sway a judge; a second run with a different shuffle would show how much.
