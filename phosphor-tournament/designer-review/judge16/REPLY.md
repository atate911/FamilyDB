**Main findings**
- **Only three real design ideas:**
  - warm cream cards with a sidebar (X02, X05, X07);
  - dark green "old computer screen" designs with a sidebar and pixel-style type (X03, X11, X14);
  - dark top-nav designs with a glowing screen showing the next plan. Ten of the sixteen are this one.
- **Thirteen of the sixteen are green on black.** That's an odd default for a family app with kids.
- **Top three:**
  - **X02** is the most consistent, and even its Status page is properly designed.
  - **X07** has the most helpful wording, such as "say it the way you'd say it at the table" and "6 days late" on every late to-do.
  - **X14** is the best of the dark designs. It tells the family when to leave ("Leave by 12:30") and shows ideas on a map by drive time from home.
- **Bottom three:**
  - **X04** has an unfinished Plans calendar on desktop and a broken one on the phone.
  - **X15** buries the overdue to-dos at the bottom of Home.
  - **X12** has a phone Status page that runs off the screen, and most of its inner pages look unfinished.
- **Bugs that would probably show in a real browser too:** X05's phone Settings squeezes one row to one word per line, and X12's phone Status overflows the screen.
- **If the family could have only one:** X02. The first fix is to show "N days late" in red on each late to-do on Home. Right now only a small "3 overdue" badge says anything is wrong.

**Where I'm least sure:** the pictures can't show motion, such as the radar sweeps, blinking cursors and scanlines. In a real browser, a few of the dark designs (X06, X08, X09, X16) could gain about half a point. X11 could lose half a point if its glow blurs small text. X09's nearly invisible checkboxes may just be how the screenshot came out.
