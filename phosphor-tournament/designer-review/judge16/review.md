# FamilyDB: review of sixteen designs (X01 to X16)

I looked at both overviews for all sixteen and at the full desktop Home for every design. Where I wanted to check something, I also opened full pages: X01 Home; X02 Status; X03 Ideas; X04 Plans; X06 Status; X07 phone Home; X09 Status; X11 Plans; X12 phone Status; X14 Ideas. Anything I say about other pages comes from the overview images, which show only the top part of each page at half size.

---

## Part 1: one review per design

### X01
**What it is**
A near-black terminal look in phosphor green, with a top nav. Its Home is built around a glowing round countdown dial.

**What works**
- The Home hero does two jobs at once. The composer sits on the left. On the right, a dial reading "NEXT IN 1 DAY" is joined by a green line that runs down into the "Coming up" timeline and its NOW node, so the next plan and the list of plans read as one thing.
- On To do, late items carry an orange left rule and large date numbers ("27 SEP SUN"), with a NOW divider before "Renew library cards". You can see at a glance what is overdue.
- "Lately added" puts the kind labels (Activity, Restaurant, Outing…) on the left of a vertical rail. It is a tidy way to show a mixed list.
- Ideas has "The line from home", a strip that plots each idea by drive time. It is a genuinely useful idea.

**What doesn't**
- Status and Settings use very small grey-on-black type in three dense columns. They are hard to read.
- On the phone, the "line from home" numbers run into each other ("7106281"). In the bottom tab bar, "Wishes" is cut off at the right edge.
- In the phone Home the dial drops below the composer, so the line that joined it to the timeline no longer means anything.

**What's beautiful**
Home: the countdown dial with its glowing tick ring, and the green line that falls from it into the Coming up timeline.

**What's ugly**
Status page: the three columns of tiny grey bullet text ("Who answers / Keys / Connected to") at roughly 11 px.

**Mark**
6.5/10. The Home has the best single idea in the dark group, but the inner pages don't carry it through and are hard to read.

### X02
**What it is**
A warm cream app with white rounded cards and tomato-red accents. It uses serif headings, a left sidebar, and a smiling tomato as the mascot.

**What works**
- It is the most complete and even design of the sixteen. Every page uses the same card language, spacing and type.
- On Home, the next plan sits beside a tear-off calendar badge ("OCT 4 Sunday") with the kids' avatars, "Open plan" and "Move it". It is the clearest next-plan card in the set.
- On Home, "Finish setting up" is a dark brown card with a progress bar. It is the only dark block on the page, so it stands out without shouting.
- Status (full page checked) is excellent. It opens with a plain "Vera is working." summary, then shows spend with a bar, a 30-day chart, a "Who answers what" table with "No backup" pills, and five connection tiles, each with its own action button.
- Chat has a conversation list (Family / Maya and Vera / Theo and Vera). Vera's confirmation arrives as an inline chip: "Silver Falls hike · On the calendar".

**What doesn't**
- The Home To do doesn't show lateness on each row. "Was due Sun 27 Sep" is in grey, and only the "3 overdue" badge in the card title says anything is wrong.
- The 30-day chart on Status uses saturated blue bars. That colour appears nowhere else in the app.
- On the phone, Wishes, Status and Settings sit behind a hamburger menu. Only five tabs show.
- The tomato mascot is a bit cute for a parent's tool, though it suits the family.

**What's beautiful**
Home: the "Next plan · Tomorrow" card, with its red-topped calendar badge, serif "Oaks Park roller rink", and avatar pair.

**What's ugly**
Status: the blue bars in the "Last 30 days" chart, which break an otherwise disciplined red, cream and brown palette.

**Mark**
8.5/10. Calm, legible and consistent on every page, and the one I'd trust a non-technical family with.

### X03
**What it is**
A green CRT terminal with a left sidebar. It uses a pixel display font ("Hello, Sam█") and puts nearly everything in a bordered card.

**What works**
- The amber "Finish setup" card has a segmented progress bar. It is the right warning colour against the green.
- Ideas (full page checked) has an "Around home" polar map, which plots each idea by drive time and compass direction. Beside it is a "Free weekend?" card with "Ask Vera for a plan".
- Plans has a "How was it?" panel with Loved it / OK / Not again buttons for past outings.
- The sidebar footer always shows "Working · $0.00 of $2.00 today".

**What doesn't**
- There are boxes inside boxes everywhere. Each to-do row is a bordered card inside a bordered card inside the page, so the screen feels busy.
- Body text and labels are monospace too ("for Maya & Theo", "Done · 29 Sep"), which tires the eye over a long list.
- The pastel pink and lavender avatar circles don't belong to the green and amber palette.

**What's beautiful**
Ideas: the "Around home" map, with faint rings, an N/E/S/W cross, green dots for places and amber dots for restaurants.

**What's ugly**
Home: the To do column, with four separately boxed rows, each holding a boxed checkbox and a pastel avatar, all inside another box.

**Mark**
7/10. A committed, characterful theme with real ideas in it, weighed down by too many borders.

### X04
**What it is**
A dark charcoal and green layout with teletext page codes ("P100 · Home"). Vera's composer is drawn inside a bezelled CRT monitor.

**What works**
- The CRT monitor composer on Home is a memorable object. Its "NEXT UP" inverse label and "> 3 more coming█" fit the theme.
- To do shows its tally as ASCII bars ("With a date ###########… 3").
- The footer legend explains the colours: "Green: the screen, and what to do · Amber: late, or needs a look". That is good practice.

**What doesn't**
- The Plans page is broken (full page checked). The month table has uneven column widths: Monday and Wednesday are narrow, Friday and Saturday wide. Events are plain underlined links. The Earlier and Later buttons are different heights. The day list below has no padding and sits flush against the box edge.
- On the phone, the Plans calendar wraps event names one word per line ("Mount / St. / Helens / day / trip").
- The theme stops after Home and To do. Status and Settings are plain unstyled lists.
- On Home, the CRT column ends at about 700 px, leaving a tall empty area under it.

**What's beautiful**
Home: the bezelled CRT composer, with its scanline glass and the radar blip beside "Oaks Park roller rink".

**What's ugly**
Plans: the unstyled month table, with mismatched column widths and the bare list of underlined dates beneath it.

**Mark**
3/10. One good object on Home, and a Plans page that looks unfinished on both desktop and phone.

### X05
**What it is**
A cream and deep-teal sibling of X02, with serif headings, a sidebar, and a small illustrated roller skate on the next-plan card.

**What works**
- Home opens with a summary sentence: "Tomorrow is the **roller rink**. **Three to-dos are overdue**, and three setup steps are left." The key phrases are underlined or coloured red. It is the best opening line in the set.
- The teal next-plan card has a line-drawn roller skate and a dashed circle. It is charming without being childish.
- Late to-dos get a red warning line on each row ("⚠ Was due Sun 27 Sep · 6 days late").
- Chat attaches place chips (Silver Falls hike, Pho Oregon, Oaks Park roller rink) under Vera's reply.

**What doesn't**
- Phone Settings is broken. The "Sign-in and security" row squeezes its text into a column one word wide ("Sign- / in / and / security / Still / the / password…"), and the row runs a full screen tall.
- On the phone Plans calendar, chips shrink to "Oa… Park rol… rink".
- Times are set with slashed zeros ("09:00", "$2.00") inside a proportional font, which looks like a font fallback.

**What's beautiful**
Home: the teal "Next plan · Tomorrow at 13:00" card, with its calendar tile and the drawn skate on two orange wheels.

**What's ugly**
Phone Settings: the one-word-per-line "Sign-in and security" row.

**Mark**
7/10. Lovely on desktop, but one obvious phone bug on a page parents will need.

### X06
**What it is**
The most disciplined version of the dark top-nav terminal design, with CRT screens used sparingly as accents.

**What works**
- Home's prompt rows explain themselves: "What should we do today? — lands in Plans", "Remind me to… — lands in To do".
- The CRT "Next up" card has a four-week mini timeline (ticks at 4, 9, 17 and 24 against now / 1 wk / 2 wk / 3 wk / 4 wk). It shows the shape of the month in one line.
- "Finish setting up" is a single amber-ruled bar with three step dots. It is present but doesn't take over the page.
- Status (full page checked) has a CRT speedometer for spend ($0 to $2.00). Below it, Who answers / Keys / Connected to are aligned tables with "WORKING" and "OFF" tags and counts ("3 of 4").

**What doesn't**
- The lower half of Status (Waiting, Worth a look) is plain stacked text.
- On the phone, the To do filter control floats to the right instead of lining up with the page.
- The text inside the phone CRT for "line from home" is very small.

**What's beautiful**
Status: the CRT gauge, with its sweeping tick scale, needle dot, and "0% OF THE $2.00 LIMIT".

**What's ugly**
Status: the "Calls that ended oddly" list at the bottom, which is six plain lines of "gpt-6-luna — end_turn / 27 Sep, 19:51".

**Mark**
7/10. A restrained, well-edited version of the dark theme, with one really good chart.

### X07
**What it is**
A cream, deep-teal and mustard-yellow sibling of X02 and X05. It has serif headings and the most helpful copy of the light designs.

**What works**
- On Home, the teal "Ask Vera" panel says "say it the way you'd say it at the table" and has a yellow Send button. It is the friendliest invitation to type.
- The sidebar carries counts (Ideas 12, To do 3, Settings 1) and a "Not you? Switch" link for a shared family laptop.
- Late to-dos say "⏱ 6 days late" in red on every row.
- Settings opens with a red banner that has one clear action, "Choose a password". It also explains its own tags ("Reading this list: Needs a look / Could be better / No tag means it's fine").
- On the phone, the header shows "✓ Vera is on". The Plans calendar shrinks events to coloured bars instead of truncating text, which is the right call.

**What doesn't**
- It uses the same slashed-zero times as X05 ("13:00").
- On phone Settings, the row chevrons drop onto a line of their own under the text.
- On the phone calendar, today's cell reads "3 TOD".

**What's beautiful**
Home: the "Ask Vera" teal panel, with its large soft circle in the corner and the yellow Send button.

**What's ugly**
Phone Settings: chevrons stranded on their own line under "General" and "AI model".

**Mark**
8/10. Warm, clear and the most thoughtful about a real family using it, with only small phone flaws.

### X08
**What it is**
A dark terminal Home with a CRT radar for the next plan and a stat strip across the page.

**What works**
- The stat strip ("TODAY ● Saturday 3 October | PLANS 4 | TO DO 4 | ⚠ LATE 3 | IDEAS 12") gives the whole household's state in one line. LATE is in amber.
- The Plans page pairs a CRT agenda ("OCTOBER 2026 · 5 busy days") with the month grid. On the phone the agenda replaces the grid.
- To do has a CRT "TALLY" bar chart ending in "> first due 2026-09-27 19:56 Call the dentist about Theo█".
- Status has a gauge whose top end is shaded amber.

**What doesn't**
- The "who is asking?" and Kind/Status/For dropdowns are native browser selects with system arrows, on both desktop and phone.
- Settings is one narrow column with the right half of the screen empty.
- Status puts three small-type columns under the gauge.

**What's beautiful**
Plans: the green CRT agenda box listing "Thu 01 10:00 Silver Falls hike…" beside the calendar.

**What's ugly**
Home: the native grey `<select>` for "who is asking?" sitting in the styled composer.

**Mark**
6/10. Good information design in the strip and tally, but the finish is uneven.

### X09
**What it is**
A dark terminal design with the strongest headline type in the group, "Tell Vera. Let's plan.", in a tight, heavy grotesque.

**What works**
- The headline is confident and well set.
- Home's prompt tiles name their destination ("Remind me to… / lands in To do").
- To do has a CRT "WHO HAS WHAT" bar chart by person (Sam 1, Alex 2, Household 1). It is a useful way to share out chores.
- The Status top row (full page checked) is three neat boxed columns.

**What doesn't**
- On Home, the checkboxes for the three late to-dos are nearly invisible: dark squares on a dark background. Lateness isn't coloured either; "was due 6 days ago" is grey.
- "The kids' wishes" is a full-width box above Coming up and To do, yet it holds only "Maya 0 open / Theo 0 open". The order of priority is wrong.
- Below its first row, Status is raw markup: centred "By kind of call" captions over bare tables, "Waiting" tags jammed together, and a plain list for "Calls that ended oddly".

**What's beautiful**
Home: "Tell Vera. Let's plan." in large tight grotesque, with "plan." in glowing green.

**What's ugly**
Home: the To do column, where the three late items have no visible checkbox.

**Mark**
5.5/10. Great type at the top, but the order of priority and the finish fall apart below it.

### X10
**What it is**
A dark terminal design with the hero in a large rounded panel and a stat strip set as a glowing green CRT band.

**What works**
- The CRT stat band (TODAY / PLANS / TO DO / LATE / IDEAS in glowing mono digits) is a nice twist on X08's strip.
- "Finish setting up" is one amber-ruled line: "1 Next: Choose your own password… 2 3".
- Settings is a tidy 3 × 3 card grid. The one card that needs a look has an amber outline.

**What doesn't**
- The desktop top nav has no "Home" item. Only the logo leads home.
- On the To do page, a CRT "TO DO / OPEN" tally floats at the top right and pushes the page title down, so the header looks misaligned.
- On the phone To do, a title collides with its Edit button ("Silver FallsEdit").
- The kids' wishes box is wide and empty at the bottom of Home.

**What's beautiful**
Home: the CRT stat band, a single glowing teletext line across the page.

**What's ugly**
To do: the floating CRT tally that knocks "Things to do 4" half a screen down.

**Mark**
5.5/10. Competent and fairly consistent, with layout slips on the phone and on To do.

### X11
**What it is**
The most atmospheric CRT design: a green-tinted background with scanlines and glow, pixel type, and a sidebar. It is a richer cousin of X03.

**What works**
- Vera speaks first in the composer: "Tomorrow is the roller rink at 13:00. Anything you'd like me to remember, plan or look up?" It is the best expression of the assistant in any design.
- Plans (full page checked) has a "How did it go?" panel with ":D Loved it / :| Fine / :( Not again" buttons. It is witty and on-theme.
- On Home, Wish lists show "empty_" with a blinking-cursor underscore. It is a lovely empty state.
- The next-plan card has a line-drawn roller skate and "Tomorrow 13:00" in pixel type.

**What doesn't**
- The amber "3 steps left" setup panel is the largest thing on Home and pushes the next plan below it.
- The small avatar circles crop their letters ("A", "S" clipped at the edge).
- In the Plans calendar, the multi-day Cannon Beach bar has a jagged notch where it crosses Saturday into Sunday.
- The glow and scanlines soften small text.

**What's beautiful**
Plans: the "How did it go?" emoticon buttons beside the green calendar.

**What's ugly**
Plans: the notched Cannon Beach weekend bar spilling across 24 and 25.

**Mark**
7.5/10. It has the most personality and the warmest Vera, at some cost to legibility and calm.

### X12
**What it is**
The most minimal of the dark terminal designs. Its Home is compact and ends before the fold.

**What works**
- The CRT "Next up" agenda uses dotted leaders ("Mount St. Helens day trip ········ Fri 9 Oct"), like a railway timetable. It is very elegant.
- "● NOW" is a green rule that fades out across the top of the three Home columns.
- An "Also" column gathers small things (Something to eat · 2 to try, Maya's wishes · 0 open) with leaders.

**What doesn't**

- Desktop Status is unstyled bold-and-plain text with no cards.
- Ideas and Plans are faint and sparse, and the calendar cells barely separate.

**What's beautiful**
Home: the dotted-leader agenda inside the CRT, ending "> All plans█".

**What's ugly**
Phone Status: the overflowing page, with its shrunken layout and stray tab bar.

**Mark**
4.5/10. The Home is beautifully restrained, but much of the rest is unfinished.

### X13

A dark terminal design close to X06, with a mixed-font headline ("Give Vera something to **plan.**", the last word in mono).

**What's ugly**

### X14
**What it is**
The most refined of the sidebar CRT designs (X03, X11, X14). It has pixel headings, a heartbeat logo and "Vera is listening".

**What works**

- The sidebar shows status in place of plain counts: "Plans 4 ahead", "To do 3 late" (amber) and "Status OK".
- Ideas (full page checked) has a "How far from home" radar with numbered dots and a legend. Bright rings mark planned ideas, and a note says two ideas aren't mapped yet. The list beside it carries drive time with a direction arrow and a status pill ("Planned · Sun 4 Oct", "Not looked up yet" in amber).

**What doesn't**

**What's ugly**
Phone Plans: the event chips truncated to "Moun / t St…" and "Nutcr / ack…".

**Mark**
8/10. The richest and most useful dark design, with real product thinking behind the look.

No design earns a 9 or 10. None is free of a visible flaw, and none goes much beyond a very good, conventional app. Nothing is so bad it deserves a 1 or 2, because every one shows all of the content.

### 2. Top three
- **X02 (8.5).** It is the most even piece of work. Every page, including the usually neglected Status page, is designed rather than dumped. The warm palette, serif headings and generous cards suit a family. The next-plan calendar badge and the dark setup card show good judgement about emphasis.
- **X07 (8).** It is as polished as X02 and better at what it says. "Say it the way you'd say it at the table", red "6 days late" on every row, "Not you? Switch" for a shared laptop, and a Settings page that explains its own tags. On the phone, the calendar shows coloured bars instead of truncated text.
- **X14 (8).** It is the dark design with the most product thinking: "Leave by 12:30", "IN 1 DAY", the "How far from home" radar with a legend, "How did it go?", and status words in the sidebar ("4 ahead", "3 late", "OK"). The theme serves the content instead of decorating it.

### 3. Bottom three
- **X04 (3).** Its Plans page is visibly unfinished on desktop (an unstyled table with uneven columns, a flush-left list) and broken on the phone (one word per line). Only Home and To do carry the CRT idea; the rest are raw lists.
- **X15 (4.5).** It has no unique failure in the parts, but it puts the overdue to-dos, the most urgent thing on the page, at the very bottom. Meanwhile an empty wishes box and a large setup panel take the top. The composer's Send button is stranded.
- **X12 (4.5).** Its Home is lovely, but its phone Status page overflows the screen. Desktop Status is plain text, and Ideas and Plans are faint and thin. It reads as a design that stopped after page one.

### 4. How many real ideas?
There are really **three** design ideas, and one of them accounts for ten of the sixteen.

- **Family A: warm light cards with a sidebar** (X02, X05, X07). They have a cream background, white rounded cards, serif headings, one strong accent (tomato red or deep teal), coloured avatar initials, and a "Good morning, Sam." greeting. They share almost the same layout; they differ in accent colour, mascot, and the details of the copy.
- **Family B: CRT terminal with a sidebar and pixel type** (X03, X11, X14). They have a dark green background, a pixel or VT323-style display font for headings, a left sidebar with a live status footer, everything in bordered cards, and amber for warnings. They differ in intensity: X03 is boxy, X11 adds scanlines and glow, and X14 is the most edited.
- **Family C: dark terminal with a top nav and a CRT "next up" monitor** (X01, X04, X06, X08, X09, X10, X12, X13, X15, X16). They have a near-black background, a sans-serif UI with mono accents, a composer on the left and a bezelled CRT screen on the right (a radar, an agenda or a dial), and green-for-now, amber-for-late. Inside it there are sub-clusters:
  - X01 swaps the CRT for a countdown dial.
  - X10, X15 and X16 share a no-Home nav, a 3 × 3 Settings grid and the same Status page.
  - X06, X08, X09, X12 and X13 vary the stat strip, gauge and tally.

What separates A from the dark families is the whole register: light, warm and human against technical and nocturnal. What separates B from C is mainly structure, a sidebar with cards and pixel headings against a top nav with open sections and a sans headline.

### 5. If the family could have only one
**X02.** It is the one both parents and the kids could read in daylight on a phone, and it has no broken page.

**What I'd change first:** make lateness visible on each row in the Home To do. At the moment "Was due Sun 27 Sep" is grey and only the "3 overdue" badge signals trouble. It should say "6 days late" in the accent red on each row, as X07 does. Right behind that: recolour the blue Status chart in the house terracotta.

### 6. What I'd take from the others and add to X02
- **X05, Home:** the summary sentence under the greeting, with linked key phrases ("Tomorrow is the **roller rink**. **Three to-dos are overdue**…").
- **X14, Home next-plan card:** "Leave by 12:30" and the "IN 1 DAY" countdown.
- **X14, Ideas:** the "How far from home" radar with numbered dots and a legend, redrawn in cream and terracotta.
- **X11, Plans:** the "How did it go?" buttons, ":D Loved it / :| Fine / :( Not again" (X02's Plans already has a "How did it go?" card; these make it one tap).
- **X11, Home composer:** Vera speaking first ("Tomorrow is the roller rink at 13:00. Anything you'd like me to remember…?").
- **X06, Status:** the spend gauge in place of a thin bar.
- **X07:** the phone header's "✓ Vera is on" status, the sidebar's "Not you? Switch", and Settings' "Reading this list" legend.
- **X07, phone Plans:** coloured event bars in place of truncated text.
- **X16, To do page:** the big open count with a vertical filter list.
- **X11, Home Wish lists:** the "empty_" blinking-cursor empty state, adapted to the serif voice.

### 7. What most of them share
- **The same information architecture and copy.** Every design shows "Finish setting up" with the same three steps, "The kids' wishes" at 0 open, "Google Calendar is not connected, so these are the plans the bot made", and the footer "A little less planning. A little more together." The differences are almost entirely visual.
- **Green on black.** Thirteen of the sixteen are dark with phosphor green. The retro-terminal look is the default here, not a choice that stands out, and it is an odd default for a family app with children.
- **Good: green for now, amber for late.** Almost all of them use a consistent pair, and many draw a NOW marker into the to-do list or timeline. That is a shared strength.
- **Good: the drive-time map of ideas.** About half have a radar or a line from home. It is the best recurring idea in the set.
- **Bad: the Status page bottom.** Below the first section, most dark designs drop into raw tables with centred captions and a plain list of "gpt-6-luna — end_turn" lines.
- **Bad: native controls.** Native date inputs (mm/dd/yyyy) and, in several designs, native selects break the styling.
- **Bad: the setup prompt.** It often gets more space on Home than the family's actual plans.
- **Bad: phone calendars.** They mostly truncate event names into fragments. Only X07 and X11 (bars) and X06, X08, X12 and X16 (lists) avoid it.

### 8. How sure am I
I'm fairly sure of the ranking at the top and bottom. I'm less sure in the 5 to 7 middle, where the dark terminal designs differ in small ways.

Things the pictures can't show:
- **Motion.** Many dark designs clearly mean to animate: radar sweeps, blinking cursors, the X01 dial, the X11 scanlines. In a browser, X06, X08, X09 and X16 could each gain half a point if the CRT pieces move well.
- **Glow and scanlines at real sharpness.** X11 might drop half a point if its glow makes small text as soft live as it looks in the JPEG. X03 and X14 carry the same risk to a lesser degree.
- **Hover, focus and contrast.** The invisible checkboxes in X09 may be a rendering artefact. If they show clearly live, X09 goes up to about 6.
- **Phone bugs.** The X05 phone Settings bug and the X12 phone Status overflow look like real layout failures, not screenshot artefacts. I'd expect them to reproduce, so I wouldn't change those marks.
- **X04's Plans table.** It looks broken whatever the browser.
- **Light versus dark.** X02 and X07 would gain from being seen on an actual phone in daylight, where most of the dark designs would lose some legibility.
