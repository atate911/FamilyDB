# Proposal: Gia

**The idea:** One lit green screen per page, where Vera speaks and the numbers live, set in a quiet dark app that reads like a modern one; the phosphor is the voice, not the wallpaper.

## The look

- **Type.** IBM Plex Sans for everything people read and tap; IBM Plex Mono for what the screen says (inside the monitor, times, counts, drive times, "was due 6 days ago").
- **Colour.** Near-black, green-tinted ground; panels one step lighter. One phosphor green for the screen, primary actions, focus and the active tab (a green underline, never a blue-grey fill or corner dot). Amber for late and "needs a look". Everything else text grey. No cyan, lilac or coral: the screen is near-monochrome, so is the app.
- **Structure.** A left rail with the page's monitor on top and the page's controls under it; content on the right (D28/D29). On the phone: monitor, then content, bottom tab bar.
- **Rules.**
  1. A monitor only where it shows what a list cannot (time, space, a scale).
  2. Glow, scanlines and cursor only inside the monitor.
  3. A teletext page number in each header (`P100 Home`, `P200 Vera`, D15): the one bit of phosphor outside the screen.
  4. Screen text never below 13px.
  5. Nothing said twice: no ring repeating the strip, no status block on every page.

## The pages

- **Home.** The monitor is the conversation: `VERA · SAT 3 OCT`, "What's on your mind?" with the cursor, and the message field inside the screen with From, mic and Send on its lower edge in one bezel (fixing D28's split). Under it NEXT UP, with a line running out of the monitor into the Coming up timeline (D33). Right: Coming up (NOW node), To do (late as amber rings), Lately added, Wishes. Finish setting up is one amber line of three steps, not section 1.
- **Chat.** Rail: Vera's glyph screen and the conversations (Family, Maya, Theo). Right: the thread, times in mono, composer in Home's bezel.
- **Ideas.** Rail: the radar with numbered blips and Nearest / Furthest (D28), then search and three selects (kind, for, status), never a wall of buttons. Right: the list, drive time in mono.
- **To do.** Rail: the text-mode bars (`Late ######## 3`, D28/D29), Open / Done / All. Right: the table with the NOW line (D27).
- **Plans.** Rail: a countdown ("NEXT IN 1 DAY", D26), List / Month. Right: the month, events as legible chips, time first.
- **Status.** Rail: the needle gauge to the daily limit (D28), an "On this page" index. Right: numbered sections, green / amber / grey dots, and the one "Ready" block.
- **Settings.** No monitor. One list with dotted leaders to each part's state (D27), amber rule on what needs a look.

## Taken from

D28: rail and monitor, needle gauge, timelines. D29: the mono prompt, text-mode meters. D27: Settings list, To do NOW line, footer colour legend. D33: the line from Next up into the timeline. D15: teletext page numbers. D26: the countdown.

## Refused

A colour per section; a dial on every page head; glow outside the screen; off-palette active states; button-wall filters; unreadable screen text.

## Why it wins

The green screen stays special on the seventh page because it appears only where it means something, leaving everything around it free to be the clearest app in the set. Each fault the reviews found (stray colours, repeated motifs, a split composer, tiny screen text, busy filters) is answered by a rule.
