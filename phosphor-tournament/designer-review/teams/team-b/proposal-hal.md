# Proposal: Hal

**The idea:** Phosphor green is the machine's voice. Whatever Vera says or works out glows on a green screen. Everything the family reads, types and presses is calm, modern and plain.

## The look
- **Type:** a clean sans for everything people read, write and press. A green monospace with scanlines and a soft glow, only for what the machine says: Vera's tag, readouts, counts, monitors. Titles are a tall condensed sans followed by a green count ("Ideas 12").
- **Colour:** near-black green, with panels one step lighter. Phosphor green is the only accent. Amber means only "late" or "needs a look". Nothing else: no slate blue, no rainbow of sections.
- **Structure:** one navigation, a top bar on the desktop (brand plus a blinking cursor) and bottom tabs on the phone. Each page has a title with its count, one instrument, then plain rows.
- **Rules:** (1) One instrument per page, showing real data, readable in two seconds. (2) No pixel or monospace body text. (3) No line or rail that does not mean time or distance. (4) Nothing clipped on a monitor. (5) The same 2px glowing green focus ring everywhere.

## The pages
- **Home:** "Tell Vera what to plan." over the box, with the ways to start under it. Beside it, the Next up monitor: rings at 1, 2 and 4 weeks, "Oaks Park roller rink · Sun 13:00 [tomorrow]". Below, a readout strip: Today · Plans 4 · To do 4 · Late 3 · Ideas 12. Setup as a one-line stepper. Then Coming up, To do and Lately added in three columns.
- **Chat:** a centred column about 680px wide. Vera's messages sit on a faintly lit panel under her mono tag; the family's are plain sans on the right. The box is pinned at the bottom; the kids' conversations are tabs above the thread.
- **Ideas:** "The line from home", a green scale from home to 3 h, with each idea as a numbered dot matching the "#" in the table. One row per idea. Filters take one row, with the rest behind "More".
- **To do:** a tally screen (Late 3, This week 0, Later 0, No date 1). One row per task, the due date in amber when late, and a green NOW rule between what is late and what is not.
- **Plans:** the Next up monitor, then the month with today's border lit, each plan a green-edged chip with its time in mono. List or Month.
- **Status:** a spending gauge, an arc and needle reading "$0.00 today · limit $2.00". Then Who answers, Keys and Connected to, with lit dots and "3 of 4" meters.
- **Settings:** a 3x3 grid of cards, each with an icon, a name and a one-line state. "Needs a look" in amber.

## Taken from / refused
- **Taken from:**
  - D35: the gauge, the tally, and numbered dots that match the list.
  - D30: the line from home.
  - D36: titles with counts, the stepper.
  - D17: the readout strip.
  - D34: the Next up monitor's week rings.
  - D37: the bezel's power light.
- **Refused:** two navigations (D34), pixel fonts for reading (D37), off-palette selected states (D36), rails that mark nothing, charts you have to study, in-jokes ("Sommaire"), walls of chips, and pages that are green and monospace all over.

## Why it wins
Other designs glow everywhere, or play safe and lose the character. Here the green screen has one meaning, the machine talking. It is on every page and always shows something true, while everything the family touches stays easy.
