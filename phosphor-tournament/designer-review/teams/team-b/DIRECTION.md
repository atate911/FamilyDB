# Team B direction: "The lit screen"

**The idea.** A calm, plain family app with one green phosphor screen per page. The screen is the machine's voice: what Vera worked out, what is next, what it costs. Glow means *now*.

## Rules

1. **One screen per page, or none.** It holds real data, readable in two seconds, never clipped. Settings has none.
2. **Glow and green mono only on the screen,** except what is *now*: Vera's newest line, the next plan and the focus ring.
3. **Four colours:** near-black green ground, warm off-white ink, phosphor green (live, actionable, focus) and amber (late, needs a look). Kinds of idea go by icon and word, never by hue.
4. **Type:** IBM Plex Sans to read and press; IBM Plex Mono for screens, times and counts; VT323 for one big figure per screen at most. Screen text is at least 13px.
5. **One level of frame:** rows on hairlines, no box in a box.
6. **Titles carry their count.**
7. **Usable first:** bordered fields, a labelled Send, a 2px green focus ring, words in the nav, and reduced motion respected.

## Pages

- **Home:** Vera's tag, "What's on your mind?" and the box with starters. Beside it, the screen: Next up over the radar of the coming weeks. Then a readout strip (Today, Plans, To do, Late, Ideas), one amber setup line, and Coming up, To do and Lately added.
- **Chat:** one centred column. Vera's lines sit on lit slips and her newest glows. The kids' conversations are tabs above.
- **Ideas:** the screen is the map radar. Below it, one filter row and rows: kind, name, distance, who for, number.
- **To do:** the screen is a tally (open, dated, undated, reminded). Below it, the folded add form, filters and ticked rows.
- **Plans:** the screen shows the month's busy days in mono. The grid stays quiet, with today outlined.
- **Status:** the screen is a needle gauge of today's spend against the limit. Lamp lists and tables below.
- **Settings:** rows, each with its state under it and an amber edge on what needs a look.
- **Phone:** bottom tabs, with the screen after the box.

## Taken from, and left out

- **From Eve:** one screen per page, the readout strip.
- **From Finn:** glow means now, kinds without hue, four colours.
- **From Gia:** the screen-text floor, Settings as a list.
- **From Hal:** the screen as the machine's voice, titles with counts, one focus ring.
- **Left out:**
  - Gia's left rail (it narrows content) and her teletext numbers (they read as code);
  - Finn's three-step glow decay (the page only knows "newest");
  - Eve's composer inside the glass (the family's words are not the machine's);
  - Hal's condensed titles.

**Why:** all four agreed on scarce phosphor, plain type and one warning colour. This keeps what they shared and settles each split towards the easier page.
