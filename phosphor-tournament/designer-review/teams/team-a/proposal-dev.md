# Proposal: Dev

**The idea:** A calm modern app on a dark desk, with one lit phosphor screen per page, and that screen always shows the page's live answer.

## The look
- **Colour.** An ink-green near-black desk, with surfaces one step lighter and warm off-white text. Phosphor green, with a soft glow, means only Vera and live data. Amber means late or needs a look; red means off. Each kind of idea gets a muted tint. The selected state is a dim green fill with a green edge, never blue.
- **Type.** Big condensed grotesque titles (D09's scale, mixed case) and a humanist sans for the body. Mono is for figures, times and the screens only, at 13px or more.
- **The screen.** One CRT per page: a rounded bezel, faint scanlines, green on black, always carrying data. A green trace may run from it into what it summarises (D33).
- **Rules.**
  - Glow only on green.
  - Lines only where they mean time or distance.
  - Relative dates first.
  - Each page's left column opens with one big numeral answer.

## The pages
- **Home.** "Tell Vera what to **plan.**", the box, then three starter rows. The screen shows Next up plus a bar per coming plan, and its trace drops into the NOW node of Coming up. Then To do, with a NOW line after the late items, Lately added, and setup as a 1-2-3 strip. On a phone the screen becomes a one-line ticker (D30).
- **Chat.** The conversation is the screen's job here, so there is no CRT. It uses a central spine with the times on it, family on the right and Vera on the left with a green edge, ending in a glowing "your turn" node above the box, whose prompt is a green `>`.
- **Ideas.** The screen is "the line from home": places plotted by drive time, nearest and furthest named. Below it, a kind-facet grid, "For" toggles, and a table of name, kind, drive, way and for.
- **Plans.** The screen is a countdown dial ("Next in 1 day, then 3 more"), beside a light month grid where plans are text lines with a kind edge, not filled blocks. The list view uses date tiles (D32).
- **To do.** "4 open · 3 late", green-selected filter chips, and the list with its NOW line, the tick, Edit and snooze.
- **Status.** The screen is the spending dial. Then plain-language lamps (Who answers, Keys, Connected to). The per-model tables sit in a fold.
- **Settings.** No screen; settings are paperwork. Each row is the label, its summary right beside it, a lamp, and amber "Needs a look".

## Taken from
- D33: screens that carry data, the trace into NOW, the left numerals, the facets, the dials.
- D30: the Chat spine and "your turn", the NOW line, the line from home, the phone ticker, the focus rings.
- D09: the title scale and hatched kind swatches.
- D31: the numbered setup steps.
- D32: the date tiles.
- D34: the bezel's finish.

## Refused
- Wood, rivets and brass.
- Blue selected states.
- Decorative rails and brackets.
- Green paragraphs.
- Tracked all-caps navigation.
- Scanlines over text a family must read.
- More than one CRT on a page.
- Raw timestamps.

## Why it wins
It does exactly what the concept says: modern and easy everywhere, with old-screen character where it earns its keep. Each page's one screen answers its question before you read a word; the rest stays quiet and readable.
