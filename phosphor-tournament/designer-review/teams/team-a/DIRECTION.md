# Team A direction: Signal

**The idea.** A calm, modern family app on a dark desk, where phosphor green is a signal, not a costume. Green means Vera, now, or live, and each page has at most one lit screen, which shows what its list cannot.

All four proposals said this in different words (Ben: "phosphor is light, not costume"; Cleo: "the glow always means something"; Dev and Ada: one lit screen per page), so this is a synthesis, not a pick.

## Rules

1. **Colour.** Green means Vera, now, live or "act here". Amber means late or needs a look. Everything else is graphite and bone-white. No blue, purple or gold, and selected states are a dim green fill with a green edge (Dev, Ada).
2. **Type.** IBM Plex throughout: Sans for reading, Sans Condensed for big titles, Mono for times, counts and screens. Nothing smaller than 13px.
3. **Screens.** At most one screen a page, always data, always captioned in words. Scanlines and bezels appear only inside it (Ben, Cleo). Glow goes on nothing else except focus and the now node.
4. **Titles.** Each title carries its one number ("Ideas 12", "To do 4 · 3 late"), with one sentence under it (Ben, Cleo, Dev, Ada).
5. **Time.** One now trace runs through every list that has time in it: Coming up, To do and Chat ("your turn") (all four).
6. **Lamps.** A lamp always comes with words (Cleo).
7. **Places.** Workings stay on Status.

## Pages

- **Home.** "Give Vera something to plan." sits above the box. The starters say where each one lands (Ben, Cleo). The screen is Next up, with the coming plans as ticks. Setup is one amber line, then Coming up and To do on the now trace, then Lately added and wishes.
- **Chat.** No screen; the conversation is the page (Dev). One column on a time spine. Vera's lines carry a green edge and the line ends at a lit "your turn".
- **Ideas.** The screen is the line from home: each idea is a numbered stop by drive time, matching the list's numbers (Ben, Cleo, Dev). Under it go one row of filters and a list sorted by its numbers.
- **To do.** The title reads "4 open · 3 late". There is no screen: the now line itself splits late from coming.
- **Plans.** The month grid has no screen (Cleo). Today is a lit disc, days with a plan are ringed and empty days are dim (Ben).
- **Status.** The screen is a spend gauge against the limit (all four). Then one list of lamps with words.
- **Settings.** No screen (Ben, Dev, Cleo). The parts are listed with their state in words, needs-a-look first in amber.
- **Phone.** A labelled tab bar, a Send button that keeps its word, and screens that never clip their text.

## Left out, and why

- **The D05 console column (Ada) and the radar.** Three of four chose the line from home, which is easier to read than a radar, and a single column is easier on a phone.
- **A Plans dial (Ada, Dev).** It repeats what the calendar shows.
- **Kind hues (Ben, Dev).** Kinds are told apart by their icons, which keeps colour for meaning.
- **The ledger strip, the FIG. 1 wiring diagram and the hop-arc.** Each is good alone, but together they would bring back the density we faulted.
- **Teletext numbers, grids, bezels on everything, and glowing panels.** Refused by everyone.

## Why

The rival designs either wear phosphor everywhere or forget it. Here a family learns in a day that green means Vera, now or live, and every screen answers its page before a word is read.
