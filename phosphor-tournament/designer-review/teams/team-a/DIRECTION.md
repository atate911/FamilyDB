# Team A direction: Signal

**The idea.** A calm, modern family app on a dark desk where phosphor green is a signal, not a costume. Green means Vera, now, or live, and each page has at most one lit screen, which shows what its list cannot. A synthesis: all four proposals said this in their own words.

## Rules

1. **Colour.** Green means Vera, now, live or "act here". Amber means late or needs a look. All else is graphite and bone; no blue, purple or gold (Dev, Ada).
2. **Type.** IBM Plex throughout: Sans to read, Sans Condensed for titles, Mono for times, counts and screens. Nothing is smaller than 12px.
3. **Screens.** At most one a page, always data, always captioned in words. Scanlines, bezel and glow stay inside it, apart from focus and the now node (Ben, Cleo).
4. **Titles.** Every title carries its number ("Ideas 12", "Status 6 of 13 on") (all four).
5. **Time.** One now trace runs through lists with time in them: Coming up, To do, and Chat's "your turn" (all four).
6. **Lamps.** A lamp always has words beside it (Cleo).
7. **Workings.** How the app works is shown on Status only.

## Pages

- **Home.** The headline is "Give Vera something to plan." Starters say where they land (Ben, Cleo). The screen is Next up, with the coming plans marked across four weeks. Setup is one amber stepper line. Coming up and To do sit on the now trace, then Lately added and the kids' wishes.
- **Chat.** No screen (Dev). One column on a time spine, with Vera's lines edged green, ending at a lit "your turn".
- **Ideas.** The screen is the line from home: numbered stops by drive time that match the list (Ben, Cleo, Dev). One row of filters.
- **To do.** No screen. Rows sit on a trace with date tiles, a tick and an Edit fold.
- **Plans.** No screen (Cleo). Today is a lit disc, days with a plan are ringed, and weekends are shaded (Ben).
- **Status.** The screen is a moving-coil spend gauge (all four). Then one list of lamps.
- **Settings.** No screen (Ben, Dev, Cleo). Needs-a-look comes first with an amber edge; every row states its condition in words.
- **Phone.** A labelled tab bar; screens scale without clipping.

## Left out, and why

- **The console column and radar (Ada).** Three of four chose the line, which is easier to read, and one column suits a phone.
- **A Plans dial (Ada, Dev).** It repeats the calendar.
- **Kind hues (Ben, Dev).** Icons tell kinds apart, so colour keeps its meaning.
- **The ledger, FIG. 1 and the hop-arc.** Each is good alone; together they bring back the density we faulted.
- **Teletext numbers, grids, glowing panels.** Refused by all.

## Why

A family learns in a day that green means Vera, now or live, and every screen answers its page before a word is read.
