# Proposal: Ada

**The idea.** One lit screen per page and a quiet desk around it. Each page has exactly one phosphor monitor, and it answers that page's first question; everything else is calm, modern and plain to read.

## The look

- **Type.** Two families, no more. A clear humanist sans (Plex Sans or similar) for everything you read or touch. A mono is used only inside the monitors and for times and figures. Page titles are large and condensed, with the page's count beside them in the same green ("Ideas 12 · last added Sun 27 Sep"). No serif, and no glow on reading text.
- **Colour.** Near-black green-grey ground. Phosphor green means lit, live, chosen or "do this". Amber means late or needs a look, and nothing else. Neutrals do everything else. There is no blue, purple or gold anywhere: active tabs, the open select's highlight and kind tags are all green or neutral, and kinds are told apart by their icons.
- **Structure.** On wide screens a left console holds the page's monitor and its controls, and the right side holds the content. This keeps D05's layout because you always know where the summary is. On the phone the console becomes the top of the page, and a bottom tab bar holds the six family pages.
- **Rules.**
  1. One monitor a page, and it shows data in space (a radar, a dial, a bar list), never decoration. Every mark on it is labelled.
  2. A single NOW line runs through time everywhere (Home, To do, Chat). Past is above or left, it is lit at now, and future is dim.
  3. Lit means active: green underline and fill for the current page, and a thick green double ring for focus.
  4. One message, one size. One row, one height.
  5. Workings stay on Status: no "Ready · gpt-6-luna" footer on family pages.

## The pages

- **Home.** Above the fold sit the ask and the next plan. The console holds the monitor ("NEXT UP Oaks Park roller rink, Sunday 13:00, tomorrow" with a small dial "next in 1 day, then 3 more"), then the From select, the mic and the one filled Send. The three ways to start sit beneath as plain rows. On the right are Coming up, as a horizontal NOW line with nodes per plan over a short list; setup as a one-line stepper ("1 2 3 · Next: choose your own password"); and To do with its NOW rule. Below the fold: Lately added, then the kids' wishes on one line.
- **Chat.** The console has Family / Maya / Theo as conversations. The thread hangs on a vertical line: each message is a node with its time, Vera's messages are slightly lit, and the line ends in a lit "your turn" node at the composer.
- **Ideas.** The monitor is the radar: every idea is a numbered blip by direction and drive time from "home", with rings at 15 min, 45 min and 2 h, plus "Nearest #7 · Furthest #4". Under it go search, one Kind select and a "For" chip row; Status sits behind "More". The list rows read kind icon, name, drive and heading in figures, then who it's for.
- **To do.** The monitor is a bar list (Late 3, This week, Later, No date 1). Tabs are Open / Late / Done / All. The list is grouped on the NOW rule, overdue dates are in amber, and the tick sits on the left.
- **Plans.** The monitor is a dial: "NEXT IN 1 DAY, then 3 more". The month calendar has large figures, today's cell outlined in green, and events as small dark chips. List and Month sit as two tabs.
- **Status.** The monitor is the spend gauge (needle, 0 to $2.00, red-line hatch, "average day $0.04"). After it comes one full-width list of lamps grouped as Who answers, Keys and Connected to, with each row on one line. Cost over 30 days goes in an odometer row.
- **Settings.** The monitor reads "9 parts · 1 needs a look". The list puts needs-a-look first with an amber edge, then a legend (on, waiting, off).

## Taken from and refused

**Taken from:** D05 (console layout, radar plotted by drive time, plans dial, to-do bar list, settings legend); D03 (title with count and a "last added" note); D10, D18 and D20 (the horizontal NOW line on Home, the chat thread line with "your turn"); D02 (the spend gauge and odometer); D04 (big calendar figures, Open / Late / Done tabs); D01 (focus rings); D20 and D05 (the setup stepper).

**Refused:** blue, purple or gold accents; visible page or keyboard numbers; more than one monitor a page; glow on headlines or body; walls of filter buttons; drafting grids, wood and brass bezels, pixel fonts on reading text; repeated system footers; unlabelled charts; a message set larger because it is newest.

## Why it wins

The character sits where it carries meaning. Each monitor tells the family something at a glance that a table could not, and the rest of the page is quiet enough that a parent, or a kid, never has to decode it. One green, one amber, two typefaces and one layout make every page familiar after the first.
