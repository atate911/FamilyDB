# Proposal: Ada

**The idea.** One lit screen per page and a quiet desk around it. Each page has one phosphor monitor that answers that page's first question; the rest is calm, modern and plain to read.

## The look

- **Type.** Two families. A humanist sans for everything you read or touch. A mono only inside monitors and for times and figures. Large condensed titles carry the page's count ("Ideas 12"). No glow on reading text.
- **Colour.** Near-black green-grey ground. Green means lit, chosen or "do this". Amber means late or needs a look, and nothing else. All else is neutral: no blue, purple or gold anywhere; icons tell kinds apart.
- **Structure.** On wide screens a left console holds the monitor and the page's controls, and content sits on the right (D05). On the phone the console tops the page, over a bottom tab bar.
- **Rules.**
  1. One monitor a page. It shows data in space, and every mark on it is labelled.
  2. One NOW line through time everywhere: past above, lit at now, future dim.
  3. Lit means active (green underline); focus is a thick green double ring.
  4. One message, one size; one row, one height.
  5. Workings stay on Status: no model footer on family pages.

## The pages

- **Home.** Above the fold sit the ask and the next plan. The monitor shows "NEXT UP Oaks Park roller rink, Sunday 13:00" with a "next in 1 day, then 3 more" dial. Then From, mic, Send and the three ways to start. On the right: Coming up on a horizontal NOW line, setup as a one-line stepper, and To do on its NOW rule. Below: Lately added, then wishes.
- **Chat.** The console lists Family, Maya and Theo. The thread hangs on a vertical line, a node and time per message, ending in a lit "your turn" node at the composer.
- **Ideas.** Radar monitor: each idea is a numbered blip by direction and drive time, with rings at 15 min, 45 min and 2 h. Below it: search, one Kind select and a For chip row.
- **To do.** The monitor is a bar list. Tabs: Open, Late, Done, All. Rows are grouped on the NOW rule with overdue dates in amber.
- **Plans.** The monitor is a dial ("NEXT IN 1 DAY"). The month calendar has large figures, today outlined green and events as dark chips.
- **Status.** The monitor is a spend gauge, 0 to $2.00. Then one full-width list of lamps (Who answers, Keys, Connected to), each row on one line, and a 30-day odometer.
- **Settings.** The monitor reads "9 parts · 1 needs a look". The list puts needs-a-look first with an amber edge, then a legend.

## Taken from and refused

**Taken from:** D05 (console layout, radar by drive time, dial, to-do bars, legend); D03 (title with count); D10, D18 and D20 (the NOW line on Home, the chat thread with "your turn", the setup stepper); D02 (gauge, odometer); D04 (calendar figures, Open/Late/Done tabs); D01 (focus rings).

**Refused:** blue, purple or gold accents; visible page or keyboard numbers; two monitors on a page; glowing headlines; walls of filter buttons; repeated system footers; unlabelled charts; the newest message set larger.

## Why it wins

The character sits where it carries meaning: each monitor tells at a glance what a table could not, and the rest is quiet enough that a parent or a kid never has to decode it. One green, one amber, two typefaces, one layout.
