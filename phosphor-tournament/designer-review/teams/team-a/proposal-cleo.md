# Proposal: Cleo

**The idea** A calm, modern dark app where green phosphor glows only where something is alive (Vera's voice, "now", and live readings), so the glow always means something.

## The look

- **Type.** Plex Sans for everything the family reads and writes. Plex Mono only for what the machine says: Vera's lines, times, counts and readings. No condensed or extended display faces.
- **Colour.** The background is warm near-black (#0d100e) with one raised surface, and text is off-white. Green (#6cf29a) does three jobs only: Vera, now, and working. Amber (#f2b35c) means late or needs a look.
- **Structure.** A title with its one number ("Ideas 12"), one sentence under it, then the content. A single "now" trace runs through every list that has time in it, with a lit node at this moment.
- **Rules.**
  1. No glow on card borders or background grids.
  2. At most one CRT screen per page, and only for a reading the list can't give.
  3. Every instrument has a caption in plain words.
  4. Lamps also say Working, Off or Needs a look.
  5. Dates are human ("was due 2 days ago").

## The pages

- **Home.** Vera's line in mono with a block cursor, then "Give Vera something to plan." and the box. Under it, starters that say where they land ("Remind me to… → To do"). Beside it, the one screen: Next up. Coming up and To do sit below on the now trace. Finish setting up is one amber line above the fold. A footer strip reads "Ready · gpt-6-luna · $0.00 of $2.00 today".
- **Chat.** A full-width thread on a vertical trace, with time stops, ending at "your turn" by the box. Vera's messages are mono on a faint green tint; the family's are sans, with initials. The kids' conversations go in a slim strip above the thread.
- **Ideas.** The screen is "the line from home": places plotted by drive time, with numbered stops staggered so labels never collide, and the same numbers in the table below. Filters are one row of three selects plus search.
- **To do.** Late items sit above the lit now line in amber, no-date items below it. The title reads "4 open · 3 late".
- **Plans.** A full-width calendar with no side screen. Today carries the now node. Chips show the title first and wrap rather than truncate. Empty weeks say "nothing on".
- **Status.** A lamp board with readable labels beside a spending gauge captioned "The limit starts again at midnight". Then three columns of lamps with words, and a captioned FIG. 1 wiring diagram.
- **Settings.** One list on a lamp line, with anything needing a look sorted to the top.

**Phone.** A tab bar with labels under the icons. Send keeps its word, and the starters wrap.

## Taken from / refused

- **Taken.**
  - D23: restraint, FIG. 1, and lamps with words.
  - D24: the line, the lamp board, the footer strip and "nothing on".
  - D25: numbers shared by the picture and the table, and settings sorted by need.
  - D15/D25: starters that say where they land.
  - D21/D08: the monitor's bezel and scanlines, used once.
- **Refused.**
  - Background grids, and glow on every card (D21, D37).
  - Costumes that replace the concept, blueprint or teletext (D22, D34).
  - A screen repeating its neighbour (D23's Plans dial).
  - Walls of filter chips.
  - ISO dates and half-bold titles.

## Why it wins

Most designs either wear phosphor everywhere and lose "easy to use", or forget the character. Here the glow is a signal: green means Vera, now, or working.  The family learns that in a day. The old screen's character becomes the app's grammar rather than its decoration.
