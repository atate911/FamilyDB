FamilyDB · Phosphor · from a blank slate

Eight designers, one line of brief

Eight designers were told only that FamilyDB is a family planning app with an assistant called Vera, and that the direction is "a modern, easy-to-use family app that carries the character of an old green phosphor computer screen". They saw the earlier designs as unlabelled pictures and could take or leave anything. They split into two teams. Each team's lead built the team's entry; the other six each built a quick sketch of their own proposal.

These are real renders of the app with the demo family's data. Each plate shows two cards: Home, Chat and Ideas, then Status, Plans, To do and Settings. Today's page is last, for reference. Nothing in the app itself has changed.

The two team entries

Signal Team A · built by its lead

"A calm dark desk where phosphor green means only Vera, now or live, and each page has one lit screen that shows what its list cannot."

The ideaGreen is kept for three things: Vera, now, and anything live. Late is amber. Each page gets one captioned green screen that draws what its list can't. Home's screen has the next plan and a four-week trace. Ideas gets a "line from home" with each place pinned at its drive time. Status gets a needle gauge.

Worth a lookThe chat hangs on a time spine that ends in a lit "your turn" at the box. Home's question: "Give Vera something to plan." Coming up and To do share one NOW line. Settings puts the part that needs a look first.

UnfinishedOn the Ideas line, places at about the same drive time pile up (stops 6, 7, 8 and 10). The fix is to merge them into one stop. The less-used pages (Family, Memory, Wishes, sign-in) weren't restyled.

The lit screen Team B · built by its lead

"A calm, plain family app with one green phosphor screen per page, where the machine speaks and what is now glows."

The ideaPlain, quiet pages with one green screen each, which is where the machine speaks. There are no section colours. Home has a readout strip (today, plans, to do, late, ideas) and three columns below it. Ideas keeps a sticky radar screen beside the list.

Worth a lookThe chat is a centred column with the kids' conversations as tabs. To do tallies with a text bar. Plans is a month list set in mono. Status has a needle gauge with a second needle for the 30-day average. One face (VT323) appears only on one Status figure.

UnfinishedOnly Home was checked on a phone. At $0 spent, the needle lies flat and says little. The less-used pages weren't restyled.

The six sketches

One Glow Team A · sketch

"A calm modern page where green light only means now, live or act here, and each page has one instrument."

The ideaUses Geist type. Home asks "Tell Vera. Let's plan." beside a tall radar screen. A figure strip comes next, then three ledger columns. The lists use who-has-what bars.

One lit trace Team A · sketch

"A calm dark app where phosphor glows only where something is alive: Vera, now, and working."

The ideaOne trace runs through the app, with a lit node at NOW. Coming up and To do hang on it. Vera's own lines are set in mono, so the machine's voice looks different from the family's.

One Lit Screen, the desk Team A · sketch

"A calm dark desk with one phosphor screen per page, and the screen always holds the page's live answer."

The ideaUses Barlow Condensed and Figtree. A glowing line drops from Home's screen into the Coming up timeline, so the screen and the list read as one thing. Home leaves the Home tab off the bar.

Afterglow Team B · sketch

"A calm, plain family app where only what matters now glows, and fades as it ages."

The ideaThings glow when they're new and fade as they age, like phosphor after the beam has passed. Home asks "What needs a plan?". Its screen lists the next plans as a dotted ledger. Everything else is very plain.

One Lit Screen, the monitor Team B · sketch

"One green screen per page, where Vera speaks and the numbers live, in a quiet dark app."

The ideaOn Home, the box and Next up both sit inside one sticky monitor, so you write to Vera on her screen. Pages are numbered like teletext (P100 Home, P200 Vera, P300 Ideas), and a small key at the foot explains the two colours.

Machine Voice Team B · sketch

"Phosphor green only where the machine speaks; everything the family touches is plain."

The ideaHome asks "Tell Vera what to plan." inside one framed console. A green readout strip of today's counts follows. To do is a table. Green only marks what the machine says.

For reference

Today's page what the family sees now

All eight share one gap the design can't fix by itself. The To do page isn't told today's date or which tasks are late. It can't mark late rows in amber or draw a NOW line, the way Home can. That would need a small change to the app if one of these goes forward.
