# The competition

Two teams of four designers each design the app's interface from a blank slate. The best design
wins. You know the app from your reviews, and the one-line concept: "a modern, easy-to-use family
app that carries the character of an old green phosphor computer screen". Nothing else is given or
required. Take any ideas you like from any of the 37 designs, disregard the rest, or start from
nothing.

All 37 designs, for ideas: CARDS=/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-FamilyDB/3d9e6f71-716a-5f05-abd6-33b8701ac8dd/scratchpad/blank-cards
(`D01-home.png` ... `D37-close.png`, four images per design).

Stage 1, each designer alone: write a proposal (at most 600 words) to your team's folder as
`proposal-<your name>.md`, with:
- **The idea** in one line;
- **The look**: type, colour, structure, the few rules it keeps;
- **The pages**: how Home, Chat, Ideas, To do, Plans, Status and Settings work in it;
- **Taken from** (design codes and what) and **refused** (what you would never do);
- **Why it wins.**
Stage 2: your team's lead reads the four proposals, chooses the direction (one of them, or a
synthesis), and builds it. Stage 3: the others critique the render, and the lead revises.
Do not read the other team's folder.
