# Afterglow

**Idea:** a warm family app that glows like an old green phosphor screen. The terminal is in the light, the type and the `>` prompt; the reading and the words are modern and friendly.

## Look

- **Type:** VT323 (CRT pixels) only for headings and big numbers. JetBrains Mono for data such as times, drive times and money. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for everything people read; it was designed for low-vision readers.
- **Colour:** near-black green glass with phosphor green (#72ffa8) and a soft glow. **Amber**, the other classic monitor colour, means only *needs you*: overdue, setup, settings to check. Each person has a colour of their own (Sam green, Alex blue, Maya pink, Theo violet).
- **Layout:** a sidebar on desktop, with Status and Settings kept "behind the scenes". On a phone, a sticky header with five tabs. Anywhere you can talk to Vera starts with a glowing `>`. Targets are 44 px or more, focus shows as a thick amber ring, and nothing needs JavaScript.

## What each page puts first

- **Home:** the Vera prompt, then tomorrow's plan and what comes after. On the right are setup steps and overdue to-dos in amber. On a phone the order is ask, next, to-dos, setup.
- **Chat:** the conversation, with "Writing as" and "Send where I am" by the box. The kids' chats are listed, readable by parents.
- **Ideas:** "save a thought", then a filterable list (kind, who it's for, drive time, status). An **Around home** radar plots each idea by drive time and direction.
- **Plans:** the month, with today and tomorrow lit up. On the side: Coming up, and "How was it?" with one-tap answers.
- **To do:** quick add, then the overdue to-dos grouped in amber, each with a big tick.
- **Status:** a plain verdict first, then spend against the limit, 30 days of cost, which model answers what (the missing fallback is flagged), and connection lights.
- **Settings:** how many sections need a look, then nine one-line rows, the ones needing a look in amber.

## Why it suits this family

They *talk* to Vera, so her prompt is the hero everywhere. The phosphor direction becomes mood, not a gimmick: calm for the parents, and a friendly glowing face, their own colour and big buttons for the kids. Sparing amber makes what needs a person stand out.
