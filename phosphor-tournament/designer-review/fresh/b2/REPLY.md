**Kitchen Terminal**: the family's shared kitchen-counter screen, drawn in the light of an old green phosphor monitor, with Vera as the friendly face on it.

Unfinished or known gaps:
- Only the Family thread is drawn in Chat. The kids' threads are tabs, but their (empty) conversations aren't shown.
- Plans, To do and Ideas are designed for phone width and don't scroll sideways, but the kit only shoots Home and Chat at 390 px. On a phone the calendar falls back to dots instead of titles, and I haven't looked at that in a screenshot.
- The kid's view (Maya signed in, no grown-up pages) is described in the design, not drawn.
- The daily breakdown of the 30-day chart on Status is invented. It adds up to the given 6 calls, but there was no per-day data.
- The Google Fonts stylesheet sometimes fails to load in the render browser (proxy retries). I re-ran the kit until every shot was taken with the fonts loaded. The pages fall back to system fonts if it fails.
