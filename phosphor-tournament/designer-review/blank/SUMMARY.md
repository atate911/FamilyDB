# Blank-slate review of 37 designs

They saw the images, nothing else: no names, no ids, no notes, no priorities, no earlier verdicts, not today's page. Designs were shuffled codes (D01 to D37). The reviews themselves are in `reviews-1.md` to `reviews-8.md`; the code key is `../blank-key.json`.

Each reviewer marked four or five designs and none saw the others, so a half point is within the noise.

## With the concept

Afterwards each reviewer was told only this (`CONCEPT.md`): "The design direction behind all of these is called the Phosphor Interface. The idea is a modern, easy-to-use family app that carries the character of an old green phosphor computer screen." They re-read their own reviews and said whether it changed their reading. Their answers are at the end of each `reviews-N.md`.

Eleven of 37 marks moved, none by more than one point: five up, six down.
