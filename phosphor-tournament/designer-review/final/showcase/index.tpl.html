The brand

Paper is the family's. Glass is FamilyDB's and Vera's. Green light appears only where FamilyDB, Vera or something live is, and every touch of it says something true.

The mark

FamilyDB's own smiling monitor, back from before the redesign: one phosphor stroke on charcoal. The favicon, the home-screen icon, the bar. It is never Vera's face.

Vera's screen

Vera is never drawn. Where she speaks there is a small screen with a lit prompt, its lines typing while she answers and going dark when she can't. Square, so she is never mistaken for one of the family's round faces, and it works whatever the family calls her.

For the family to decide

Kids ticking their own to-dos, and rating how a plan went. Today a kid can't change anything; the kid, parent and usability reviewers all asked for it. The design shows "Tell Vera I did it" for now.

A private chat for parents, so a present can be planned with Vera without it appearing in the family chat.

A kid sharing where they are with a message: hidden on kids' pages for now.

A twelve-hour clock setting: the design shows "1 pm"; the app needs a setting for it.

Building it: this is a set of drawn pages with a written standard (tokens, components, words, phone rules, states, accessibility numbers, notes for the engineer). Nothing in the app has changed yet; turning it into the app's templates is the next piece of work.

How it got here: thirty-seven earlier designs and a committee tournament; twenty designs judged blind by three judges, where Kitchen Table (green) came second, third and first; the family's choice; thirteen specialist reviews; five rounds of work by its designer; and blind checks after each step. Every design and judgement is in the comparison viewer.

Every designer and judge in this process is an AI model; the family's own eye is the verdict that counts.
