# Kitchen Table, final

The look chosen for FamilyDB's web page, finished. It is a set of drawn pages, not yet the app: nothing under `src/` has changed.

- `design/`: twenty-four standalone pages, `style.css`, the icon sprite, self-hosted fonts and `brand/` (the mark as favicon and home-screen icons). `STANDARD.md` is the written standard to build from: colour, type and spacing values (day and night), every component, the word table, what each role sees, the phone rules, the states, the brand, the accessibility numbers, and notes for porting into the Jinja templates under the content security policy. `type.html` is the type specimen. `CHANGES.md` records every change and why; the stage briefs (`FINAL.md`, `POLISH.md`, `BRAND.md`, `BRAND-2.md`, `LAST.md`) and the designer's replies (`reply-stage*.md`) record how it was made.
- `shots/`: every page at 1280 px and on a phone, by day and (in `dark/`) at night.
- `showcase/`: the showcase page (published as an artifact): every page, day and night, desktop and phone, with a before-branding switch, the checks and the open decisions.
- `checks/`: the blind and lens checks at each step.
  - `blind/` and `lenses/`: original Kitchen Table against the final (4 of 4 chose the final, 8s against 6–6.5); the thirteen lenses before and after (mean 6.7 → 7.4).
  - `brand-1/`: the first branded version against the plain final (3 of 4 chose plain, though all four said the brand was the stronger identity), with the style and accessibility lenses.
  - `brand-2/`: the refined brand against the plain final (4 of 4 chose the brand: 8, 7.5, 8, 7.5 against 7 each).

## How it was made

From the description-only Kitchen Table (green), which three blind judges ranked second, third and first of twenty, and which the family chose. Thirteen specialist lenses reviewed it; its own designer then worked in seven stages: the system and the seven pages with a real phone layout; the missing pages (kids, wishes, sign-in, forms); every state, dark mode and the standard; a polish pass from the checks; the brand (FamilyDB's smiling-monitor mark, Vera's small screen, one map of ideas by drive time, phosphor at night) with type held to the version the family chose; a refinement that kept the brand and removed its costs; and the last touches the final judges asked for.

## Left for the family
