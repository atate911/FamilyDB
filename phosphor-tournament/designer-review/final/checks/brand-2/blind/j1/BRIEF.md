# Brief

You are an expert creative graphic and interface designer.

The work: two designs for the web page of a family planning app. A family (two parents, two kids aged 9 and 11) talks to an assistant called Vera, by text message or on this website, and the app keeps their ideas (places to go, things to do), plans, to-dos, the kids' wish lists and settings. The family uses it on phones and laptops; the kids must be able to use it. Each design shows the same seven pages with the same content. The designs are known only by a code, `P` and `Q`. They come from different designers. Treat them alike.

Each design has a folder, `P/` and `Q/`, with:
- `overview-desktop.jpg`: its seven pages on a desktop (Home, Chat, Ideas, Plans, To do, Status, Settings), the top of each at half size. Read this first.
- `overview-phone.jpg`: the same seven pages on a phone, top part.
- `desktop/<page>.jpg` and `phone/<page>.jpg`: each page full length (home, chat, ideas, plans, todo, status, settings).
- `overview-dark.jpg` and `dark/<page>.jpg` (and `dark/<page>-phone.jpg`): the same pages with the phone or computer set to dark mode.

Look at all the overviews, light and dark, then open full pages wherever you need to, desktop and phone, at least Home, To do and Plans for both. Be specific; do not invent anything you cannot see.

Write `review.md` with:

## P
**What it is**, **What works**, **What doesn't**, **Mark** (out of 10, as graphic and interface design, one sentence why)

## Q
The same headings.

## Between them
1. Which would you build for this family, and the three reasons that decided it.
2. Which has the stronger identity, the one a family would recognise as its own app and remember, and does that identity cost anything in use?
3. Where the other one is better, if anywhere (page and element).
4. What is still wrong with your choice, most important first (up to five).

Reply with your two marks and your choice.
