# Review: a parent's view (Sam or Alex)

## Verdict
**8/10.** It talks to me like a person: "Roller rink tomorrow, and three to-dos have slipped past their dates" (Home), "$0.00 of your $2.00 daily limit" with "a usual day is about 4¢" (Status), and "Sam and Alex can read your chat with Vera" on Maya's chat. I'd trust it with the household. What holds it back on my phone is that Home is very long and puts the plan and the late to-dos below a big chat box, and I can't tell how a surprise stays secret once someone mentions it in the family chat.

**Optimise before building?** No. Everything below is about order, wording or small additions. Nothing needs a redesign, so it can all be fixed while it's being built.

## Protect
1. Home, the opening line ("Roller rink tomorrow, and three to-dos have slipped past their dates") with links to both. I understand my day from one sentence.
2. Status, the "Spent today $0.00 of your $2.00 daily limit" bar with the "usual day, about 4¢" marker, plus "If Vera reaches the limit, she takes a break until midnight". It answers "is this costing us money?" in plain dollars.
3. Chat (parent and kid), the eye note "Parents can read Maya's and Theo's chats. The kids see a note saying so" and, on the kid's side, "Sam and Alex can read your chat with Vera." It's honest with the kids and keeps me in the loop.
4. Wishes, the Yes / Thinking about it / Not this time buttons with "A word for Maya", and the "Planning a surprise? … add a gift idea instead" card. It's kind, and it keeps presents off the kids' pages.
5. States sheet, kids get "Vera is resting until tomorrow" and "You've sent all 20 of today's messages" with no money or company names, while I get "Change the limit." That's the right split.
6. Settings, the yellow "1 thing needs a look" banner and the "Needs a look / Could be better / Off" tags, explained in "Reading this list." I know what to do first without understanding any of it.

## Fix, ranked

**1. Phone Home is about eight screens long, and what I need today is below the fold** (phone/home: Ask Vera block, Next up, To do)
- Problem: On my phone the first screen is the greeting, the setup strip and a huge green Ask Vera block (box, yellow Send, three suggestion chips). "Oaks Park roller rink, Leave by 12:30 pm" starts under the tab bar, and the three late to-dos are on about the third screen. When I'm tired I open it to see "what's next and what's late", not to type. It hurts parents on the go.
- Fix: On phone, shrink Ask Vera to a one-line box with a send icon (chips collapsed under "Ideas to ask…", or only on Chat). Put Next up (just the first plan) and To do (overdue only, "All 4" link) straight under the greeting. Move "Just added to Ideas", "How did it go?" and "Wish lists" lower.
- Severity: major. Effort: M.

**2. Surprises can leak through the family chat** (Chat, Family conversation; To do "Buy Maya's birthday present")
- Problem: The Family chat says "Everyone in the family can read it", and Maya can open it (chat-kid shows a "Family" tab). If Alex types "remind me to buy Maya's birthday present" there, which is exactly what the To do page tells us to do, Maya reads it. The surprise tag protects the to-do but not the message that created it. This is the thing that would make me stop using it.
- Fix: Show it on the page. When Vera spots a surprise in the family chat, she replies "I'll keep this from Maya" and the message shows a "Hidden from Maya" pill, like the to-do has. Add a line under the Family chat header: "Planning a surprise? Write to Vera in your own chat or by Telegram." Better still, give each parent a private "You and Vera" chat like the kids have.
- Severity: major. Effort: M.

**3. A kid can tick "Share where I am" by themselves** (chat-kid, the "Share where I am (ask a grown-up first)" checkbox)
- Problem: An 11-year-old will just tick it. "Ask a grown-up first" is only a note, not a control, and I can't see a setting that turns it off for the kids.
- Fix: For kids, hide the checkbox unless a parent has turned on "Kids can share where they are" (in Family or Settings › Sign-in and security), and say so on the kid's chat when it's off.
- Severity: major. Effort: S.

**4. During the "one family password" stage, anyone can pick Sam** (signin, "Brand-new install: one family password" and the "Who's writing?" chooser)
- Problem: Until I set my own password, Theo can type the family password, tap "Sam (Admin)" and reach Settings and Spending. The bubble says "Only until Sam picks a password", but nothing pushes me to do it on day one.
- Fix: Make "Choose your own password" step 1 of setup (it already is on the empty Home) and block Settings › Spending and AI model until it's done. On sign-in, show the family-password box only for Sam. Kids and Alex wait for their own.
- Severity: major. Effort: S.

**5. Reminders depend on Telegram, but that's buried** (Home phone, "No reminders are set. Reminders go by Telegram, which isn't connected yet"; To do footnote)
- Problem: I only learn at the bottom of a card that "remind me on Tuesday" won't reach me. I'd assume it works and miss the dentist. Telegram is also only the second setup step, after passwords.
- Fix: In the "Who, when, reminder" quick-add and on any to-do with a reminder, show a clear "Won't be sent: Telegram isn't connected. Connect" pill. In the Setup card, say what you lose: "Without this, reminders don't go out."
- Severity: major. Effort: S.

**6. Too many little badges in the menu** (sidebar and tabs: "2 to rate", "3 late", "1 to decide", "1 to check")
- Problem: Four counters at once feels like homework, and "1 to check" next to Settings worries me as if something's broken. Only "3 late" really matters today.
- Fix: Keep red counts for late to-dos only. Make "to rate" and "to decide" plain grey numbers, and drop the Settings badge once the yellow banner shows the same thing on the Settings page.
- Severity: minor. Effort: S.

**7. "AI model: Could be better" and "Backup: Add a key" read like an upsell** (Settings › AI model; Status › Backup)
- Problem: As a non-technical parent, "Add a key" sounds like paying for a second service. It doesn't say whether that costs more or what "key" means.
- Fix: Reword it to "Optional: if OpenAI is down, Vera can use a second AI company. You'd need an account there; it only costs when used." Show "Off" (grey) rather than "Could be better" (yellow) for a choice we made on purpose.
- Severity: minor. Effort: S.

**8. Ideas search has an extra "Show ideas" button** (Ideas, next to "Newest first")
- Problem: I type in search and the "Show ideas" button next to it confuses me. I'd expect Enter to just work.
- Fix: Rename it "Search", or keep it but make Enter submit and label the group "Search and sort".
- Severity: minor. Effort: S.

**9. The kid can't tick their own to-do** (todo-kid, "Sam or Alex tick these off. Done? Tell Vera…")
- Problem: Maya packing her swim bag and then having to text Vera to tell me is a step she'll skip, so it stays "late" and nags me.
- Fix: Let a kid mark "I did it" (shown to parents as "Maya says done — confirm?"). This counts as asking, not changing.
- Severity: minor. Effort: M.

**10. Status lists things that sound broken when they're simply not set up** (Status, "How each part is doing": Telegram and Google Calendar "Not connected")
- Problem: In the first week the Status page looks like half the app is failing, even though the top says "well under budget".
- Fix: Group these under "Not set up yet" with one "Continue setup" button, separate from "Working" and "Not working".
- Severity: minor. Effort: S.

## Missing
- A private "You and Vera" chat for each parent (for presents and grown-up things). Today only the kids have one.
- A way for parents to choose what each kid can do: share location, daily message count, see Ideas or Plans. I only see the "20 messages left today" result.
- The "What Vera knows" page isn't drawn. I'd want to see and delete anything about the kids (allergies, school) and know who can read it.
- Reminders without Telegram, such as an email or a phone notification, for the parent who won't install Telegram.
- A monthly cost line in plain words ("This month: $1.26, about the price of…") and an alert to me when the limit is hit, not just a note in the chat.
- What happens with a lost phone or a kid who knows a parent's password: "Sign out everywhere" and "Reset Maya's password" shown on a Family page.
- Undo or a warning before a kid's message goes to the Family chat that everyone reads (kids can switch to the "Family" tab).
- A short "What is Vera?" explainer for the first visit, including that Vera is an AI from a paid company and what it sends there.

## One sentence
Make a surprise impossible to leak: give parents a private chat with Vera and show "Hidden from Maya" on any family message or item that mentions a present.
