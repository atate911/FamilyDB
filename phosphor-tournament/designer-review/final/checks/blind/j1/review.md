# Review: FamilyDB, designs P and Q

Pages looked at: both overviews; desktop Home, To do and Plans for both; phone Home, To do, Plans and Chat for both; phone Ideas for P. The other pages were checked from the overviews only.

## P

**What it is**
A warm, quiet design on a cream background. The desktop has a left sidebar (Home, Chat with Vera, Ideas, Plans, To do, Wishes, What Vera knows, then "Behind the scenes": Status, Settings). Page titles are in a serif, and the body text is in a rounded sans. There is a deep-green "Ask Vera" panel with a yellow Send button, and cards with small coloured category pills (Day trip, Show, Trip, Activity). On the phone, the sidebar becomes a top tab bar (Home, Chat, Ideas, Plans, To do, More) under the FamilyDB header, with a "Vera is on" check and an avatar.

**What works**
- The visual identity is settled and friendly. Green, cream and a yellow accent are used consistently, and the type hierarchy is clear (big serif titles, bold sans item names).
- The desktop Home is well ordered. Ask Vera comes first, then "Next up" with a large date tile for the roller rink, then To do, then "Just added to Ideas". Setup, Wish lists and "Vera today" sit in the right column.
- The desktop To do is a clean table. Each row has name, owner avatar, due date with "6 days late" in red, reminder state with an "Add one" link, and Edit. A red left border and an "OVERDUE · 3" header mark the late items.
- The desktop Plans month grid is readable. Events have a time and a title, colour follows the kind (day trip blue, show pink, outing grey), past plans carry a "Rate it" prompt, and today is outlined with a "TODAY" label. A "Coming up" list and a "How did it go?" card follow below.
- Ideas cards are rich: kind pill, status pill ("Planned · Sat 17 Oct", "Went Thu 1 Oct"), who it's for, and drive time with a direction arrow.

**What doesn't**
- **The content contradicts itself about the hike.** In Chat, Alex says "Put the hike on Saturday at 10" and Vera answers "Done: Silver Falls hike, Saturday 10:00 to 14:00". But Plans shows the hike on Thursday 1 Oct, and Ideas says "Went Thu 1 Oct".
- **Times are hard to read.** The time numerals use slashed zeros, so "13:00", "09:00" and "$2.00" read as "13:ØØ", "Ø9:ØØ" and "$2.ØØ" everywhere. The 24-hour clock also doesn't suit a 9- and 11-year-old.
- **The phone layouts are a stacked copy of the desktop, not a phone design:**
  - Ideas puts Search, Kind, Status, For and a Show button above the first idea. The list starts about two screens down.
  - To do opens with a full add form (Who, Due, Remind, Add) plus a Telegram banner. The first to-do is below the fold, and the Open/Done/Cancelled/All control wraps "All" onto a second line.
  - The Plans month grid shows only coloured bars with no titles. Today's cell is clipped to "3 TOD".
- **The phone Home hides the main action.** The Ask Vera suggestion chips run off the edge ("Remi…"), and "Finish setting up" falls to the bottom of a very long page.
- **Chat's "Writing as" picker** lets the person at the keyboard pick Sam, Alex, Maya or Theo. A child can post as a parent, and the reverse.
- **The rating faces on Plans have no labels** (three smiley icons, no text). The Cannon Beach weekend shows as two separate chips (Sat 08:00, Sun "all day") rather than one span.
- **Nothing hides gifts from the kids.** "Buy Maya's birthday present" is shown plainly in a to-do list that Maya can open.

**Mark: 6.5 / 10.** It is a handsome, coherent desktop system, but the phone pages are long form-first stacks. Slashed-zero times and a date contradiction undercut the trust and legibility a family app needs.

## Q

**What it is**
The same family of styling as P: cream ground, left sidebar, serif titles, a green Ask Vera panel with a yellow Send button. It is more restrained in colour: event chips are mostly neutral and pick up the person's colour (Nutcracker in Maya's pink). It adds a "Vera is answering" status pill under the logo, count badges in the sidebar ("2 to rate", "3 late", "1 to decide", "1 to check") and a Family page. On the phone, navigation is a bottom tab bar (Home, Chat, Plans, To do, More) with a red "3" on To do.

**What works**
- **Phone layouts are actually designed for the phone:**
  - To do has a one-line "Add a to-do… / + Add" with a collapsed "Who, when, reminder · Sam · No date · No reminder" row, so the list starts on the first screen.
  - Ideas collapses its filters into one "Filter · Any kind · Anyone · Any status" row.
  - Plans opens with a "Coming up" list and "How did it go?", with the month as a small dot calendar at the end.
  - Ask Vera chips wrap instead of running off the edge. Setup shrinks to one "3 setup steps left / Continue" strip at the top of Home.
- **Times are written for people:** "1 pm", "Tomorrow, 1 pm", "Leave by 12:30 pm", "Sat 8 am to Sun". This is easy for a 9-year-old.
- **Kid-aware details:**
  - "Surprise · hidden from Maya" on the birthday-present to-do, and "Surprise" on the Lego idea.
  - A note in Chat: "Parents can read Maya's and Theo's chats. The kids see a note saying so."
  - "Writing as Sam · Not you? Sign out" instead of a free choice of who to post as.
- **Ratings are labelled:** "Loved it / OK / Not great" buttons with faces, on Home and on Plans, with who went ("Sam and Alex").
- **The content is consistent:** Chat says "Put the hike on Thursday at 10" and "Thursday 1 October, 10 am to 2 pm", which matches Plans.
- **To do rows read in one line** on desktop: avatar + name, clock + date, "6 days late" in red, reminder state, Edit. "No reminders are set. Reminders go by Telegram, which isn't connected yet." is said once, under the list.
- **The Status page** turns problems into a "How each part is doing" list, each row with a tag (Working / Needs a look / Could be better / Not connected) and a direct action ("Set up sign-ins", "Add a key", "Connect Telegram").

**What doesn't**
- **The desktop month grid is drab.** Most events are the same grey-brown chip, so the calendar is harder to scan by kind than P's. Today is only a filled circle on "3", with no word.
- **Ideas is not in the phone bottom bar** (it goes under More), although it is the family's main store of places and things to do. Wishes, which the kids own, is in More too.
- **The rating card weighs heavily on Home.** The "How did Silver Falls hike go?" card sits high in the desktop right column, above Wish lists, and adds yet another card to an already long phone Home.
- **Badge and tag noise.** The sidebar carries a chip on four items, and Settings rows repeat "Could be better" five times. The tags lose force.
- **Kids get no simpler view.** Every page is the parent view, and nothing visible is aimed at a 9-year-old beyond the wording.

**Mark: 8 / 10.** It is the same visual quality as P but genuinely designed for phones and for a family with children. The weak spots are calendar colour and some badge clutter.

## Between them

### 1. Which I would build: **Q**

Three things decided it:

1. **It works on a phone.** On To do, Ideas and Plans, Q gets to the content on the first screen; P buries it under stacked forms and filters. P's phone month grid has untitled bars and a clipped "3 TOD".
2. **It is safe and legible for the kids.**
   - Surprises are hidden from the child they are for ("hidden from Maya").
   - "Writing as Sam · Not you? Sign out" replaces P's picker, which lets anyone post as anyone.
   - "1 pm" replaces P's slashed-zero "13:ØØ".
   - Ratings are labelled "Loved it / OK / Not great".
3. **It can be trusted.** Q's chat, calendar and ideas agree on when the hike happened, where P's Saturday/Thursday contradiction would make a parent doubt Vera. Q's Status and Settings rows each carry a status tag and a fix button.

### 2. Where P is better

- **Plans, desktop month grid:** P's event chips are coloured by kind (blue day trip, pink show, dark-red roller rink), and today is labelled "TODAY". This is easier to scan than Q's mostly grey chips.
- **To do, desktop:** P's rows have a separate "Add one" reminder link on each to-do, which makes adding a reminder a one-click job.
- **Home, desktop:** P's right column is lighter. With no rating card wedged in, Wish lists and "Vera today" sit closer to the top.

### 3. What is still wrong with Q, most important first

1. **There is no kid view.** Maya and Theo see a parent dashboard (spending, setup, admin badges). They need a simpler Home with their wish list, their plans and Ask Vera.
2. **Ideas and Wishes are hidden under "More" on the phone.** One of them should take a bottom-bar slot (e.g. Ideas instead of Chat, since Ask Vera sits on Home).
3. **The desktop calendar needs colour coding** by kind or by person on every event, and a "Today" label on the current day.
4. **Cut the badge and tag noise.** Keep the counts that need action ("3 late", "2 to rate") and drop "1 to check" and repeated "Could be better" tags, or merge them into one Status line.
5. **The phone Home is too long.** Next up, To do, Just added to Ideas, the rating card, Wish lists and Vera today all stack up, so the rating card and Vera today should drop off the phone Home.
