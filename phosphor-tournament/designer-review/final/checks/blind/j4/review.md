# Review: P and Q

Both designs share a lot: a cream background, a deep green brand colour, a serif for headings and a sans-serif for body text, a left sidebar on desktop, a green "Ask Vera" card on Home, and cards on the right for setup and wish lists. So the differences are in the details, and in how each one handles the phone.

## P

**What it is**
A warm, calm, card-based layout. The sidebar shows a count next to each item ("Plans 2 to rate", "To do 3 late", "Wishes 1 to decide", "Settings 1 to check") and includes a Family link. Times use a 12-hour clock ("1 pm", "Leave by 12:30 pm"). On the phone there is a top bar with a "Vera is answering" pill and a five-tab bottom bar: Home, Chat, Plans, To do, More.

**What works**
- **Home (desktop):** the subtitle "Roller rink tomorrow, and three to-dos have slipped past their dates" is underlined and links to both. "Next up" pairs a calendar-tile date with "Tomorrow" and "Leave by 12:30 pm" chips, a drive time, and avatars for who's going. Rating a past plan ("How did Silver Falls hike go?" with Loved it / OK / Not great) sits right on Home.
- **Kids and secrets:** "Surprise · hidden from Maya" on Maya's birthday present and "Surprise · hidden from Theo" on the Lego idea. The Wish lists card shows "Maya 4 wishes · 1 to decide" and "Maya added 'A pet hamster' today." A family with kids needs exactly this, and Q has none of it.
- **To do (desktop and phone):** one quick-add field with a "Who, when, reminder · Sam · No date · No reminder" row under it, instead of a full form. Every row reads person · date · "6 days late" in red · "No reminder", with a red left rule on overdue items. On the phone this stays compact: four tasks and the add box fit in about 2,400 px.
- **Plans:** on desktop, Cannon Beach is drawn as one bar across Sat and Sun. Past plans are dashed with "How did it go?". On the phone the order is right: "Coming up" list first, then rating, then a small month grid with dots.
- **Phone Home:** the setup card shrinks to a one-line "3 setup steps left · Continue" banner, so Ask Vera stays near the top.
- **Consistency:** the chat says "Put the hike on Thursday at 10" and the calendar shows Silver Falls on Thursday 1. Settings has a "Colours in FamilyDB" key ("Each person keeps their colour on every page", "Red only means late, or broken").

**What doesn't**
- The phone bottom bar leaves out Ideas and Wishes. The kids' own page sits behind "More".
- The phone Home page is very long (about 6,600 px): Ask Vera, Next up, To do, Ideas, rating, Wish lists, Vera today.
- The sidebar has a lot of badges (four counters plus the "Vera is answering" pill). Badges in three styles (red, outline, amber) compete for attention.
- Calendar cells are cramped: "How did it go?" wraps inside a small dashed box. Most plans are the same greige, so only Nutcracker (Maya's pink) stands out.
- The phone Plans page has no Month/List toggle (desktop has one).
- On the full-page phone captures, the fixed bottom bar shows up partway down the page over the content. That is probably an artifact of how the page was captured, but I can't check it on a real device.

**Mark: 8/10.** Clear, consistent and family-aware (surprises, kids' wishes, rating, leave-by time), with a phone layout that is actually redesigned rather than just squeezed. It loses points for burying Ideas and Wishes on the phone and for a crowded sidebar.

## Q

**What it is**
The same structure and palette, with more colour coding: category pills (Day trip blue, Show purple, Trip blue, Activity pink) and coloured blocks for plans in the calendar. Times use a 24-hour clock in a font with slashed zeros ("13:Ø0", "Ø9:ØØ"). On the phone, navigation moves to a six-tab bar at the top (Home, Chat, Ideas, Plans, To do, More). The user block reads "Parent · runs the app" with "Not you? Switch", and the chat has a "Writing as Sam / Alex / Maya / Theo" picker.

**What works**
- **Phone navigation:** Ideas gets its own tab, and the To do tab carries a red "3" badge.
- **Plans (desktop):** category colours make the month easy to scan. Past plans are faded with a "Rate it" link, and "TODAY" is labelled in the Sat 3 cell.
- **To do (desktop):** a real columned table (task, person, date with "6 days late", reminder with "Add one", Edit) is easy to scan. Above the list, a green notice says "Vera sends reminders by Telegram, which isn't set up yet… Set up Telegram".
- **Chat:** the "Writing as" chips make it obvious who is posting on a shared laptop, which suits the kids.
- **Ideas (desktop):** a "Look it up" link on each card that hasn't been looked up, and a status pill ("Planned · Sat 17 Oct") on ideas that became plans.

**What doesn't**
- **The content contradicts itself:** in the chat Alex says "Put the hike on Saturday at 10" and Vera replies "Done: Silver Falls hike, Saturday 10:00 to 14:00". The calendar and "How did it go?" both put it on Thu 1 Oct.
- **No surprises and no kids' activity:** Maya's birthday present and the Lego set carry no "hidden from" marker. Both wish lists say "No wishes yet". Plans has no "to rate" count in the nav, and Home has no rating card.
- **Slashed-zero digits** ("13:ØØ", "$2.ØØ", "19:ØØ") look like code. They are hard for a 9-year-old to read and odd next to the serif headings.
- **Phone To do:** the desktop table collapses into tall stacked cards (person, then date, each on its own line) that take about 3,900 px. The Open / Done / Cancelled / All tabs wrap "All" onto a second line, and the search box doesn't fill the width. The add form (text, Who, Due "mm/dd/yyyy", Remind, Add) and the Telegram notice push the first task below the fold. The raw browser date field "mm/dd/yyyy" also shows on desktop.
- **Phone Plans:** the month grid comes before the list and shows only coloured bars, no titles. The Sat 3 cell clips to "3 TOD". The rating faces have no labels, unlike P's "Loved it / OK / Not great".
- **Phone Home:** the Ask Vera chips run off the edge ("Remi…"), and the setup card drops to the bottom.
- **Settings:** the sign-in warning is a pink/red alarm banner. For a setup step, that is more alarming than it needs to be.

**Mark: 6/10.** It has a good eye for colour coding and a tidy desktop To do table. But the phone layouts mostly stack the desktop parts with clipping and wrapping, the slashed-zero time font hurts readability, and the content contradicts itself.

## Between them

### 1. Which I would build: P

1. **It works on the phone, where this family mostly is.** P's phone To do, Plans and Home are compact and in the right order: the list comes before the grid and setup collapses to one line. Q's phone pages are stacked desktop parts with wrapped tabs, a clipped "TODAY" and off-screen chips.
2. **It serves the kids and the family's secrets.** P has "Surprise · hidden from Maya / Theo", wish counts with "1 to decide", "Maya added 'A pet hamster' today", and labelled rating buttons. Q has none of these.
3. **It is more readable and its content is consistent.** P uses a plain 12-hour clock ("1 pm", "Leave by 12:30 pm") with normal digits, and its chat and calendar agree on Thursday. Q has "13:ØØ", and its chat says Saturday while its calendar says Thursday.

### 2. Where Q is better

- **Phone navigation:** Q's top tab bar gives Ideas its own tab. P hides Ideas and Wishes under More.
- **Desktop Plans calendar:** Q's category colours (Day trip, Show, Trip, Activity) and the "TODAY" label make the month easier to scan than P's mostly greige blocks.
- **Desktop To do:** Q's columned rows, the "Add one" link for reminders, and the Telegram notice with a "Set up Telegram" button sitting above the list.
- **Chat:** the "Writing as Sam / Alex / Maya / Theo" chips, which suit a shared family laptop.
- **Ideas (desktop):** the "Look it up" link on each card that hasn't been looked up.

### 3. What is still wrong with P (most important first)

1. **The phone bottom bar hides Ideas and Wishes** behind "More". Wishes is the kids' main page, so it should be a tab, or the tabs should change for a child who is signed in.
2. **The phone Home page is too long** (about 6,600 px with seven cards). It should show Next up, To do and one secondary card, and link out to the rest.
3. **The calendar doesn't use colour well enough:** most plans are the same greige, and "How did it go?" wraps inside tiny dashed cells. It should use the person colours or Q-style category colours consistently, and shorten the past-plan cue to an icon or "Rate".
4. **The sidebar is noisy:** four badges in three styles plus the status pill. Badges should be kept for things that need action (late, to rate), and "1 to decide" and "1 to check" should be made quieter.
5. **Phone Plans has no Month/List toggle,** and the month grid shows only dots, with no way to see an event without scrolling back to the list.
