Decided

  • Vera’s box stays a plain card in the paper looks; no colour-blind floor; Kitchen Table is the default when it ships.
  • Each person’s look is stored with them and follows them to every device, kids included.
  • Status is for every grown-up; a parent opening Settings gets the friendly “for an admin” page that names who to ask.
  • Presents say whom they’re hidden from; to-dos get their own Edit page; “Set by” is recorded on kids’ to-dos; “installer” is gone.
  • Phosphor is the design language; Afterglow is one look drawn from it, under its own name, one fixed look with no day or night; the Phosphor look stays on the Look page.
  • Afterglow’s pixel face is for page titles and the wordmark only; the five small motions stay and the old ones stay retired.
