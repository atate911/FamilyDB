Who she is

Who answers, and in what voice. With none, the answers are plain and brief, from the bot itself.

Leave it empty and she is Vera.

How she speaks, not what she does: the rules for ideas, plans and reminders stay as they are, and win where the two meet. {name} is what she is called, Vera, wherever it is written.

changed

A few sentences of your own, such as no emoji, call Mia “Captain”, or less chat in the mornings. They are kept after her description, so they last when hers is improved or rewritten.

About the family

Ages, tastes, what to avoid, how far you will go. The family list on the Family page says who may message her; this says who they are.

What she says unasked

Reminders, “how was it?”, notes after a lookup, and notices when she cannot answer are written, not thought up, so they cost nothing and still arrive when the model is down. Each box shows her line; type over it to use your own. Under each is how it reads now, with made-up details filled in. A reminder or a follow-up that comes due while you are talking to her is mentioned in her reply instead.
