# Stage 7: the last touches

Four new blind judges compared your stage 6 version with the plain final. **All four chose yours**
(8, 7.5, 8 and 7.5 against 7, 7, 7 and 7), and all four said its identity is clearly stronger and
now costs little. Their reviews are in `reviews-brand2/`. They named the same few small things;
do these, render everything, look, fix, and stop. Change nothing else.

1. **The surprise chip always says who it's hidden from** (all four). Never just "Surprise". In a
   narrow tile, use the lock icon and "Hidden from Maya"; elsewhere "Surprise · hidden from Maya".
2. **The radar stops interrupting the idea cards** (all four). Give it its own place: a third view
   beside "All ideas" and "Restaurants" (for example "Map"), or a band after the cards on desktop and
   behind "Show the map" on the phone. Drop the duplicate numbered list (the cards are the list);
   label the dots with something a kid can match to a card, at a size they can read; ring and
   compass labels in Atkinson, not mono; fix dot 10 sitting on the "W".
3. **The status pill is quiet when all is well** (three judges). "Vera is ready" becomes a light,
   calm pill in the family's type. The dark glass pill with the mono voice appears only when there
   is something to notice: she's writing back, resting at the limit, or can't answer.
4. **To do's Add is a full, obvious button again** (all four). On the phone, put a one-row "Add a
   to-do" at the top of the list (box and button), with who, when and reminder folded beneath it;
   keep the rule that nobody is picked by default.
5. **Small craft**: month names on the phone calendar's spill-over days ("28 Sep", "1 Nov"); the
   tight letter spacing in the "Add a to-do" heading; the Status cost figures on one row on phones
   from 360 px, stacking only below that.

Update `CHANGES.md` and `STANDARD.md` where these touch them, then stop and reply.
