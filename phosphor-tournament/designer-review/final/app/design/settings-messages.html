What Vera sends unasked

  • Weekend ideasOff

    Nowhere chosen, so none is sent. Costs: one model call a week. None sent in 30 days. Change Weekend ideas

  • RemindersOn

    When a reminder somebody asked for is due. Costs: free. 4 sent in 30 days, the last Thu 1 Oct, 8:00 am.

  • How did it go?On

    The day after a plan, at 10:00. Costs: free. 2 sent in 30 days, the last Fri 2 Oct, 10:00 am. Change How did it go?

  • Tomorrow’s plans, checkedOn

    The evening before a plan, at 18:00, only when something is off. Costs: free. 1 sent in 30 days, the last Sat 3 Oct, 6:00 pm. Change Tomorrow’s plans, checked

  • A task brought upOn

    When the part of the week a task was kept for comes round, and the calendar is free. Costs: free. None sent in 30 days. Change A task brought up

  • What a lookup foundOff

    After ideas are looked up, together each evening at 21:00. Costs: free to send; each lookup is a small model call. 3 sent in 30 days, the last Mon 28 Sep, 9:02 pm. Change What a lookup found

  • What needs fixing, to adminsOn

    When something only an admin can fix goes wrong, to each admin with a Telegram id. Costs: free. None sent in 30 days. Change What needs fixing, to admins

  • A kid’s ask, to the parentsOn

    When a kid asks for something that isn’t OK, or asks for a parent, to each parent with a Telegram id. Costs: free. 1 sent in 30 days, the last Fri 2 Oct, 4:10 pm.

  • A parent’s answer, to a kidOn

    When a parent answers a wish, in the kid’s own chat. Costs: free. 2 sent in 30 days, the last Sat 3 Oct, 11:20 am.
