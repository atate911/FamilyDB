Today’s limit reached

When spending reaches the daily limit, Vera stops until midnight and says so in the chat. Every message box closes with the reason, and admins get the one action that fixes it.

Header pill · calm, then the three that need noticing

Vera is ready Vera is writing back Vera is resting until midnight Vera can’t answer right now

Kids never see the pill. Its words come from the one health state for “Vera” and are the same on Status, Settings and Home. When all is well it is quiet: soft green, the family’s type, a still dot. Only when there is something to notice does it become the dark glass pill with Vera’s mono voice: writing back (its dot breathes until the reply lands), resting, or can’t answer. On the phone it shortens to “Ready”, “Writing”, “Resting” or “Can’t answer”; the full sentence is its spoken name.

A message on its way, and one that didn’t get through

While a reply is pending, the box is closed. The page refreshes itself with a meta refresh that is only present in that state, and “Check for her answer” lets anyone reload it themselves. With scripting on, it checks quietly instead. After 60 seconds without a reply, the message is marked as failed.
