Five bolder palettes

The first six were all one idea: a light grey page, a navy-ish panel and one signal colour. These five each commit to something different: a dark-first theme, a plum panel with brass, an 80s home computer, almost no colour at all, and an evening sky. Layout and type are the same in all five; Vera’s green screen is the one lit thing in every one. At the top, each palette sheet sits above its Home. Press “Whole page” to see a shot in full.

Telling them apart

Midnight changes the most: the app lives in the dark, and the green screen feels most at home.

Home Computer is the most playful and the closest to FamilyDB’s story.

Ink is the most striking: all the colour belongs to the family.

Aubergine & Brass and Dusk are the warm, grown-up pair: one rich and indoor, one soft and evening.

All five passed their contrast and colour-blind checks by script, use no hot pink, and keep red for late only.
