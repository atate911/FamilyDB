The idea: two terminals and a page

Three things, each with one job. Amber on navy is the family’s own terminal: it lights the next thing to act on. Green on charcoal glass is Vera’s. The page is paper and ink, where the people write in their colours.

For colour-blind eyes

The eight avatars, simulated (Machado 2009). Neighbours differ in lightness as well as hue, so they stay apart. The figures are the closest pair under each simulation (CIEDE2000; under 5 is hard to tell apart, over 10 is clearly different). Names and initials always say who, too.

Signals

Each signal has one colour, one form and one job, and always words beside it.

  • Saturday 3 October

    Today: amber lit on the navy screen. Home’s date, the calendar’s day number under an amber rule.

  • Leave by

    Leave by and Tomorrow: the same lit label over the biggest figure on Home, with an amber rule beside it.

  • Plans

    You are here: an amber plate on the navy panel, and the navy screen with an amber label on the phone’s tab bar.

  • Add a plan

    The one primary button: the navy screen with an amber label by day, an amber plate at night.

  • 3 late

    Late: the only red. “6 days late” in words, the red rule in the margin, the red plate on the panel.

  • 3 steps left

    Set this up, needs a look: copper, printed on paper with the warning sign. Never lit, never on navy, so it is never the amber signal.

  • Working

    Done and working: green, the done tick and the “Yes!” stamp.

  • A link

    Links: navy-black ink with a grey underline that thickens on hover.

  • Focus

    Focus: a 3 px ring in ink on paper, amber on the panel and at night, phosphor on Vera’s glass.
