Specimen

Type

Two faces plus Atkinson. Atkinson Hyperlegible for every word the family reads. Barlow (Semi Condensed for titles, Condensed for labels, the panel and big figures), the sign lettering that makes the page crisp. Martian Mono for the margin, where the book keeps its dates, times and numbers. Archivo has left.

The faces

  • Barlow Semi CondensedTitles and the wordmark. 700, set tight: page titles, section heads, the greeting, Ask Vera’s head. Barlow is drawn from California’s highway and rail signs. Its flat terminals and square-cut shapes are what make the page feel crisp, the way a station sign does. From The Board (OFL, 21 KB).
  • Barlow CondensedLabels, the panel and every big figure. 600 for the panel’s page names and 700 for capitals: running heads, group heads, column heads. Also 700 for Leave by, drive times, money, ranks, the calendar’s day numbers and the time on Next up. Its tabular figures are narrow and line up. Two weights, 40 KB.
  • Atkinson HyperlegibleEverything the family reads and writes. 400 and 700, 17 px body. Kept from every round: it was chosen for the kids’ reading.
  • Martian Mono 0123456789The margin. Dates, times and entry numbers in the margin column, chat times, and the lines on Vera’s glass. Narrow (75 %), with every figure the same width, so a column of dates lines up. It is what makes this the Log (OFL, 38 KB).
