# Brief 3: House Log, with The Board's panel and crispness

The family has chosen. Their words: **"Let's mix both. I like the left navy panel from The Board
but the tightness and breathability of the House Log. Overall I'd go with House Log with the left
panel and the tightness and crispness of The Board. Colors still need to be fixed."**

So **House Log is the base**, and two things come in from The Board (its latest version, with its
stylesheet, standard, fonts and shots, is in `borrow-board/`):

1. **The left navy panel.** The Board's sidebar: the navy fascia down the left, white type on it,
   the current page as an inverted plate, counts on the right, the account at the foot, Vera's
   ready pill at the top, and on the phone its navy top bar. Bring it in whole, and make it sit
   with the Log: keep what was good about the Log's numbered contents if it still earns its place
   on navy, or drop it. Vera's box must still read as hers next to it (her glass, her glow, her
   rounded corners), and nothing else on the page should turn into navy panels: the Log's
   breathing room stays.
2. **The Board's tightness and crispness.** Work out what makes The Board feel tight and crisp
   (its condensed sign lettering for figures and headings, square corners, firm rules and rails,
   inverted plates, even spacing, figures that line up) and bring the parts that sharpen the Log
   without losing its air. If that means The Board's Barlow for headings and figures, keep the
   total to two faces plus Atkinson, and say which face left and why.
3. **Keep the Log's breathability:** ruled sections instead of boxes, the date margin, the light
   page, generous line spacing in lists.
4. **Colours: leave for the next round**, which three colour designers will do on your result. Use
   the navy panel's colours as they are and keep everything else as it is now; don't tune the
   palette.

Keep everything from your earlier briefs: Ask Vera as Home's hero, the one-line greeting, the
departure-board Next up with the leave-by figure, route stripes, the floors, the brand facts, no
