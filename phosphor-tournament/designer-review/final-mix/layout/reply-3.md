House Log is now the base with The Board's navy panel and crisper type. Every page is re-rendered light and dark, desktop and phone, and I looked at all of them. The final render had no warnings, and there are still no inline styles or scripts. `DIRECTION.md` has a "Round 6" section and `STANDARD.md` is updated.

- **The navy panel is in whole.** It uses The Board's colours unchanged, with white sign lettering and icons, counts lined up on the right, and the late count as a solid red plate. The current page is an inverted white plate with a green edge (Maya's colour on her pages). Vera's ready pill is at the top and the account sits at the foot. On the phone the top bar is navy too.
  - I dropped the Log's 01–10 page numbers. On navy they competed with the icons and counts and didn't help anyone find anything.
  - Nothing else on the page turned navy. Sections are still ruled lines on the light page.
- **Vera's box still reads as hers.** It stays charcoal glass with 14 px rounded corners (everything else is square), a phosphor ring and glow. So next to the navy panel it doesn't look like another panel.
- **Crispness:**
  - **Fonts:** Barlow, The Board's sign lettering, replaces Archivo. Semi Condensed sets titles, the greeting and the wordmark. Condensed sets labels, the panel, tabs and every big figure (Leave by, drive times, money, ranks). Archivo left because Barlow does both of its jobs more crisply. That keeps it at two faces plus Atkinson: Barlow, and Martian Mono for the date margin, which is what keeps it the Log. The new fonts total 99 KB.
  - **Lines:** section lines over headings are firmer, 3 px instead of 2.
  - **Tabs:** every "you are here" is now an inverted plate: the panel, the phone tab bar, and the Open/Done and Month/List switches.
  - **Labels:** small capitals are 14 px condensed instead of 13 px wide, so they're tighter and still easy to read.
- **Kept:** Ask Vera as Home's hero, the one-line greeting, the departure board with Leave by, route stripes, the due column, drive figures, the date margin and the airy list spacing.
- **Colours:** nothing changed except adding the panel's colours. Every new text pair on the panel passes AA in both themes; the lowest is 6.6:1.
