# House Log

**The voice:** the family's logbook. Vera keeps the ruling (dates, times and numbers in a mono margin) and everybody writes the lines, in plain words and their own colour.

## Type

- **Archivo** (OFL, 90 KB) has two voices in one file. Condensed and heavy for titles, section heads and big dates, fitting a strong title on a short line. Wide capitals for running heads, group heads and column heads.
- **Martian Mono** (OFL, 38 KB), narrow, is the margin: every date, time and entry number. It has equal-width figures, so dates line up. It is also Vera's voice on the glass.
- **Atkinson Hyperlegible** stays for every word people read.

## Layout changes

1. **A margin on every list.** Date and time on the left edge, then the tick or a plan's glyph, then the words. *When* scans down one edge and *what* down the other, and plan and to-do titles line up.
2. **Sections, not cards.** A 2 px ink rule and a condensed head instead of a box. More fits on each screen.
3. **Home:** Ask, then Next up and To do as one log. Setup, rating, wishes, ideas and spending are side notes. Today is a green stamp in the margin.
4. **Plans:** the weekend columns are 45 % wider, where the plans are, so fewer titles are cut short. Events are text lines with the person's rule.
5. **To do:** the due date moves into the margin. Ruled group heads ("OVERDUE · 3" in red). The add box is the next line.
6. **Chat is a transcript:** who and when in the margin, the words beside the person's colour rule.
7. **Ideas is a numbered index** with Kind, For, Drive from home and Status columns, so drive times can be compared down one column.
8. **The sidebar is a numbered contents**, with counts aligned. A running head says which page you're on.
9. **Wishes:** big rank numbers in the margin, number 1 in the kid's colour, and parents' answers as margin notes.

**Colour:** cool stone-white paper (not cream), near-black ink and hairline rules. Green means today and Vera, red means late. People keep their pens, and a kid's pages are ruled in her colour. Dark mode is graphite.

## Where it is weaker

- On a 390 px phone the margin takes 68 px, so titles wrap more.
- Without boxes it relies on rules, and hairlines fade on dim screens.
- The Ideas table needs about 1100 px. Narrower, it drops columns, and below 1000 px it becomes rows.
- On the phone's Home, the late to-dos start just below the first screen. The lede links to them.

## Round 5: the Log, taken further

**Ask Vera leads Home.** A full-width pane of her glass sits right under the greeting, with her 56 px screen, her line in the mono after a lit `>`, a two-line box, a big phosphor Send and the starters. It is the only rounded, lit block on a ruled page, so it reads as Vera's, not as a section. Maya's Home has her version, with her line in Atkinson. Chat's box sits on the same glass with the same Send.

**The greeting earns its space.** Home opens with one line: the date in green capitals and "Good morning, Sam." at 26 px. Under it, "Roller rink tomorrow, and three to-dos are late" is the bigger type (22 px, ink, with its links). Every other page title drops from 64 to 40 px. On the first screen, desktop and phone, you get the greeting, Ask Vera and the next plan with its Leave by time.

**Borrowed from The Board, and made the Log's:**
- **Next up as a departure board.** When in the margin, the route, the plan, then **Leave by 12:30 pm** (72 px), the biggest figure on Home. It is set in the Log's own Archivo and rules, not the Board's navy panel. On the phone, Leave by comes straight under the date line.
- **Route stripes.** Every plan (Home, Coming up, the calendar's events) has a 6 px bar in the colours of the people going, with Everyone in grey. It replaces the person's rule and the calendar glyph.
- **A due column.** The margin now holds the due date and the lateness ("Sun 27 Sep / 6 days late" in red), so one column says when and how late.
- **Drive time as a figure** on the right of every plan row and in the Ideas index, with the direction in capitals beside an arrow turned to point that way.

**Not borrowed:** the navy fascia and panels, the platform-number plates, Barlow, Coming up before the month on Plans, and the wish-list line diagram. The Log keeps paper, ink rules and its margin.

**Colour:** Maya's pink (`--p3`) is now plum (#B884B0, with matching soft, ink and mark colours): dusky, not sugary, and still distinct from violet and periwinkle. It is a stopgap until the colour round.

**Weak spots, improved:**
- Hairlines are about 30 % darker in both themes.
- On the phone, the margin now sits on a line above the title, so titles keep the full width.
- Ideas keeps its drive column at every width. Under 1180 px the kind becomes a label over the title, and the phone shows number, title and drive figure.

**Still weaker:** a drive direction like "north-north-east" wraps on the phone. The calendar's phone dots don't have route stripes.

## Round 6: House Log, with The Board's panel and crispness

House Log stays the base: ruled sections, the date margin, the light page and airy lists. Two things come in from The Board.

**The navy panel.** The Board's sidebar is in whole: the navy fascia (#111A2B, and #1F2B40 at night, its colours unchanged), white sign lettering, icons, counts lined up on the right, the late count as a solid red plate, "you are here" as an inverted white plate with a green edge, Vera's ready pill at the top and the account at the foot. On the phone the top bar is navy. **The Log's numbered contents (01–10) are gone.** On navy the numbers competed with the icons and counts and added nothing you'd look for, so the icons do that job. A kid's panel keeps her colour on the current plate's edge and the account rail. Nothing else turned navy: the page is still paper and rules.

**Vera stays Vera next to it.** Her Ask box is charcoal glass, not navy. It has rounded 14 px corners (everything else is square-cut), a 4 px phosphor ring and a glow, so the two dark blocks never read as one family.

**Crispness.**
- **Barlow** (OFL, The Board's three files, 60 KB) replaces Archivo. Semi Condensed 700 sets titles, the greeting and the wordmark. Condensed sets labels, the panel and every big figure (Leave by, drive times, money, ranks, day numbers).
- **Archivo left** because Barlow does both of its jobs. Its flat, sign-cut shapes are what made The Board feel crisp, and its narrow tabular figures line up. That keeps the total at two faces plus Atkinson: Barlow, Martian Mono (the margin, what makes it the Log) and Atkinson.
- **Firmer rules:** section rails went from 2 to 3 px of ink.
- **Inverted plates** for "you are here": the panel, the phone tab bar and every tab switch (Open/Done, Month/List).
- Labels are 14 px Condensed capitals, not 13 px wide ones, so they're tighter and still easy to read.

**Kept:** Ask Vera as Home's hero, the one-line greeting, the departure board with Leave by, route stripes, the due column, drive figures, the margin, generous row spacing.

**Colours:** untouched apart from adding the panel's colours, ready for the colour round.

**Weaker:** the navy panel adds a second dark block beside Vera's glass on every desktop page, so the colour round should check the two side by side. The red late plate on navy is only 2.6:1 against the panel itself (its text is 6.6:1).

**No starters (brief 4).** The suggested-message buttons are gone from Ask Vera everywhere: Home, Maya's Home, her empty chat (Vera's own line there is enough) and the empty-state sheet. Vera works out what's meant on her own. The hero is tighter: her head and typed line share a row and the box and Send are 80 px. That brings the whole Next up board and the start of To do into the first desktop screen, and on the phone Leave by sits about 150 px higher.
