Specimen

Palette

Enamel: the colours of old enamel signs and good painted interiors. A warm grey page, petrol-black ink, a deep petrol panel and eight earthy colours for the people, with Vera’s phosphor glass still the one lit thing.

The eight people

Each person has a colour (avatars under dark letters, the kid’s tab), a soft wash, an ink for their name and a mark for route stripes and rules. Light and dark colours alternate round the wheel, so neighbours differ in lightness as well as hue: the closest pair under any simulated colour-blindness is still clearly apart. The name is always in the words too.

Vera’s glass and the phosphor

Unchanged on purpose: the shadow of the first green CRT design. The charcoal glass is green-black, never petrol, so it never reads as the panel; the phosphor is the only bright green anywhere.
