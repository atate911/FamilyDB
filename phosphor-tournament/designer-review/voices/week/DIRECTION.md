# Week at a Glance

**The voice:** the family's week, printed big: seven days across, today in solid ink, everyone in their own pen.

## Type

**Archivo** at three widths, with **Atkinson Hyperlegible** for every word people read.

- **62 %** for numbers that stand alone: day numbers, dates, money, ranks, drive times. They're tall and narrow like a desk planner's, so a 72 px "3" fits a seventh of the page.
- **72–90 %** for headings: heavy, tight and short, like a poster's.
- **125 %** for 13 px printed capitals: weekdays, labels, group heads. At that width they stay legible at our smallest size.

It's all one 86 KB file. Atkinson stays because it's still the clearest face for the kids.

## Layout changes, and what each makes easier

- **A masthead replaces the sidebar,** so the week gets the full width. The current page is a tab printed in ink.
- **Home leads with the next seven days,** today first as a solid ink column and the weekend wider. The next plan is written out on its own day. You can see what's on and which days are free at a glance.
- **Greeting and Ask Vera sit side by side,** so the week starts higher.
- **The kid's Home is her week,** with her to-do on the day it's due.
- **On the phone, the week turns on its side.** Each day is a row, and a free day takes one line, so tomorrow's plan shows above the tab bar.
- **To-do rows lead with their date** (red when late), so you can scan by day. The list comes first, with the adder beside it.
- **Ideas show drive time as a big figure,** so distance compares at a glance.
- **Plans:** today is an ink cell, and weekend columns are 1.4× wide.
- **Chat:** each day starts with its number.
- **Wishes:** ranks are big numbers, and rank 1 is solid in the kid's colour.

## Colour

Paper and ink. Because today is ink, green now means only Vera, pens only people, and red only late. Sheets are square under a 3 px rule. Instead of soft shadows, the next plan gets a printed offset.

## Where it's weaker

- An empty Saturday is a loud black column. At night it softens to grey.
- Weekday columns on Home are narrow, so long titles wrap.
- A rolling seven days breaks with the fridge's Mon–Sun week. Plans keeps Mon–Sun.
- Undated kids' to-dos only show on My to-dos.
- Between 820 and 1180 px the masthead takes three rows.
- It's calmer than Felt Tip; some may miss the stickers.
