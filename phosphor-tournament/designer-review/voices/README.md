# Four voices

Round 4. The family asked for "another round of creative ideas for making it more distinctive and usable, focusing on layout and typography, and a distinctive visual voice for the app". Four designers each started from Felt Tip (`../joy/wall`) with a different idea of what FamilyDB is, and were free to change layout and type (not the fonts of earlier rounds):

- **The Board** (`board/`): the day as a station departure board. Barlow Condensed and Semi Condensed. Home leads with the next plan and its leave-by time as the biggest figure.
- **Week at a Glance** (`week/`): the week on one page. Archivo at three widths. Home leads with the next seven days; a masthead replaces the sidebar.
- **House Log** (`log/`): the household logbook. Archivo and Martian Mono. A date margin on every list; ruled sections instead of cards.
- **Thread** (`thread/`): talk first. Familjen Grotesk and Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono. Home is one timeline from "Now"; Chat is the phone's middle tab.

Each folder has `BRIEF.md`, `DIRECTION.md`, `reply.md` and its `STANDARD.md`. `compare/index.html` shows all four. None has been judged blind.
