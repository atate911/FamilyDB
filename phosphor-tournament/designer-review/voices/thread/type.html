Specimen

Type

Three voices in one conversation. The family speaks in Atkinson Hyperlegible, Vera types in Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono, and the names of things (plans, places, wishes, page titles) are set in Familjen Grotesk.

The faces

  • Familjen GroteskNames of things. Page titles, group heads on the thread, a plan’s title, an idea’s name, big dates and money, the wordmark. 600 for titles, 700 for the page title and figures, tracked in a little. Its cut “a”, hooked “g” and “y” are the voice: plainly modern, and nobody’s default. One variable file, 19 KB.
  • “A pet hamster”Wishes, in the kid’s own words. Familjen italic, 500, inside curly quotes in her colour. The only italic in the app, so a wish always looks like something someone said. 21 KB.
  • Atkinson HyperlegibleEverything the family reads and writes. 400 and 700, 17 px body. Unchanged, for the kids: its letters are drawn so they can’t be mistaken for one another.
  • Atkinson Hyperlegible MonoVera’s voice, and the thread’s clock. Her messages, the times and dates in the margin, labels in capitals, and the lines on the glass. From the same legibility research as Atkinson, so a kid reads Vera as easily as her parents; typed, so Vera never sounds like a person. Weight 400–600, 18 KB. It replaces JetBrains Mono.
