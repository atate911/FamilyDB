# Thread

**The voice:** FamilyDB reads like the household's own running conversation: one line down the page with the clock in its margin, Vera typing in a steady mono, the family speaking in Atkinson, and every plan, to-do and wish dropping in as a well-made object.

## Type

- **Familjen Grotesk** (OFL) for the *names of things*: page titles, plans, places, ideas, big dates and money. Its cut "a" and hooked "g" give it a voice without Bricolage's busyness; its italic does one job: **wishes, quoted in the kid's own words**.
- **Atkinson Hyperlegible** (unchanged) for everything the family writes and reads, to-do titles included (a task is a sentence).
- **Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono** (OFL, replaces JetBrains Mono) for **Vera and the clock**: her messages, margin times, label capitals. Same legibility research, so kids read Vera easily; typed, so she never sounds like a person.

New font files total 57 KB.

## Layout changes

1. **The thread.** Home, To do, the kid's Home and To do, and Plans' "Coming up" are one vertical line with dates in the margin. *When* scans down one column; late dates turn red.
2. **Talk first.** Home's composer is "Now", the top of the thread. Other desktop pages have a one-line **Write to Vera** box in the rail. On the phone, **Chat is the middle tab**, a small pane of Vera's glass.
3. **Objects with their own form.** A plan is a ticket with a **people strip** (its people's pens down its edge). A to-do is a line with a tick, a wish is a quote, and an idea is a card with its kind in mono capitals. You know what it is before reading.
4. **Chat as a transcript.** Everyone's words line up on one edge, with the time in the margin. With five people, a name says who spoke faster than left-versus-right.
5. **Wishes as replies:** a parent's answer is threaded under its wish.
6. **Plans:** "Coming up" leads and the month follows,.
7. **To do:** the list is the page. On the desktop, Add sits beside it, not above it.
8. **Calmer surface:** a plain porcelain page, with no dot grid, tape or tilts. Today is a filled green node, the same mark as "Now".

## Where it is weaker

- The mono is wide, so Vera's long replies run to more lines.
- On a phone the margin takes about 75 px, so to-do titles wrap sooner.
- Your own messages no longer sit on the right.
- With the rail box, some pages offer two places to write.
- The kid's Home now puts *Next up* before *My wishes*.
