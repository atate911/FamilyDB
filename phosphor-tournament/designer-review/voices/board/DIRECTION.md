# The Board

**Voice:** the family's day, set like a station departure board: what leaves next, when to go and whose it is, readable from across the kitchen.

## Type

**Barlow** (OFL), from California's highway and rail signs. **Condensed** 600/700 sets the board: times, dates, drive and due figures, money, ranks, column heads, navigation. Its narrow tabular figures line up in columns. **Semi Condensed** 700 sets headings and the wordmark. Everything is mixed case, as on road signs, because word shapes read faster; only small column heads and plate weekdays are capitals. **Atkinson Hyperlegible** stays for every sentence and every title a kid reads. The new fonts total 60 KB; Bricolage is gone.

## Layout, and what each change makes easier

1. **Home leads with a departure board.** Next up runs full width. The next plan is the big row, with **Leave by 12:30 pm** as the largest figure on the page. You see when to leave without reading a sentence.
2. **Route stripes.** Each plan row has a bar split into the colours of the people going (Everyone is grey), so you see whose plan it is before reading names.
3. **To-dos on a board.** The due date and "6 days late" sit in their own right-hand column, so the eye runs down one line of dates. Overdue gets its own board with a red block.
4. **Plans: Coming up first**, beside How did it go?, so the next four plans are read before the grid. The month follows as a timetable.
5. **Ideas as a destinations board**: the drive time as a figure, with an arrow turned to its direction. Picking something near is quick.
6. **Wishes as a line diagram**: ranks are platform-number plates joined in the kid's colour.
7. **Phone:** the first screen is the greeting, the next plan and its leave-by time; late to-dos come next. Ask Vera moved below them, since Chat has its own tab.

Everything hangs from ink. The sidebar and phone bar are a navy fascia, every panel has a 4 px top rail, and the page title sits on a heavy rule (in her colour on a kid's pages). "You are here" is always an inverted plate.

## Where it is weaker

- There is a lot of navy. The Ask card is navy too, so Vera's panel stands out less than the old charcoal glass; its green rail and phosphor Send carry it.
- The wish-list line only bridges the gaps between cards; it is not one continuous line.
- Chat changed least: it has a band head, line-coloured message edges and station-style day breaks, but the same structure.
