**Borrowed from House Log**
- **One "when" edge.** On every list the date is down the left edge and the one figure you act on is down the right. To-dos now start with their due date and how late, like a departure starts with its time; Maya's to-dos do too. On the phone the date sits on its own line above the title.

- **Chat as a transcript.** Name and time sit in a margin, and the words sit beside the person's colour rule, with no bubbles. Vera's receipts and suggestions stay as panels. On the phone her suggestions stack so names don't break.
- **Wider weekend columns** on the month (1.45×), where the plans are. Event headers now wrap so times don't run into the people's initials in the narrower weekday cells.

**Not borrowed:** the numbered sidebar, a mono face for dates (a third typeface, and Barlow Condensed already lines figures up), the numbered Ideas index, rank numbers in the margin, and the cool stone paper. `DIRECTION.md` says why for each. `STANDARD.md` is updated with the day head, the one-edge rule, ruled sections, the transcript, the wider weekend columns and the new title sizes.
