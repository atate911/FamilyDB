# Brief 2: House Log, taken further

The family looked hard at round 4. The Board came first at first; now: **"Actually, reviewing more,
I think I might like House Log even more."** So House Log goes forward, and The Board's best ideas
may come into it. The family's notes on both, which all apply to you:

1. **Ask Vera is the hero of Home.** They love the big box for writing to Vera: at the top of Home,
   big enough to invite writing, with its lit Send and the ways to start, on the first screen on
   desktop and phone. It must read as Vera's (her dark glass, her phosphor, her sign `.vs`), never
   as one more ruled section. The kid's Home gets her version. Chat's box is the same Vera.
2. **The greeting must earn its space.** "The big 'Good morning' header looks good, but takes a lot
   of space that doesn't do any work." Make it small (one line with the date, or folded into the
   page head or into Ask Vera's card). The useful line ("Roller rink tomorrow, and three to-dos are
   late", with its links) stays prominent, set as information. Ask the same of every page's big
   title. Words stay as they are; only their setting changes.
3. **Borrow from The Board.** Its direction, stylesheet and shots are in `borrow-board/`. What the
   family liked there: the departure-board Next up, with **"Leave by 12:30 pm" as the biggest
   figure** on Home; **route stripes** in the colours of the people going; a due column you can run
   your eye down; ideas showing drive time as a figure. Bring in what makes House Log better and
   leave the rest; whatever you borrow should look as if it was always the Log's. Say in
   `DIRECTION.md` what you borrowed and what you didn't.
4. **Colour:** "I'm not sure about the colours, especially the pink." A colour round comes next,
   so don't redesign the palette now, but replace the hot-pink person slot (`--p3`, Maya's) with a
   colour that isn't sugary, and keep the eight slots distinct.
5. **Your own weak spots:** hairlines that fade on dim screens, the phone margin squeezing titles,
   the Ideas table needing a wide screen. Improve them where you can.
