# Dossier: folio (main camp)

The line's idea: a videotex service as a folio: numbered pages of one service, read like a teletext magazine.

Every judge's words on this line, oldest first. Scores compare best within their round.

### r12-wmut-1 Videotex Switchboard: main round 12, #8 of 17, mean 7.01 (today's page 4.84; in 2 judges' top fours, 7 promise votes)
What it set out to be: The family's videotex service as a control panel: Home is the map of every page in equal cells, every page is the same rack of cells with its own green screen under its plate, and only the machine gives off green light. Home is the service's sommaire, laid out as a graphite switchboard. Every page is a cell, and each cell's head is a contents line: lamp, page number, name, dotted leader, count. The line is the key into the page, and the cell below it holds what the page contains now. Every other page opens the same four-column rack. Column 1 holds the plate, then the page's own green screen (the machine's summary of the page), then its tools. The three columns beside it hold one thing to a cell. Because both sides share the same rows, seams meet as modules do in a rack. It should feel like a calm, solid panel where the only light comes from the machine: the tubes, the lamps, the mark and the primary keys.
Its designer's own weaknesses: Where a page has little data, column 1 now runs longer than the main column: Plans list (6 half-unit plans beside plate, screen, tabs and add), To do (4 tasks beside a tall tools cell) and Family (the long 'Who can get in' cell). The rack leaves honest empty space there. | Some cells stretch to their row and end up mostly empty: the Chat and Status plates, Home's Memory and Wishes cells, the Wishes kid lines in their half rows, and the Family roles row. | Home's view passes only restaurant_count, so 310 shows a count, not the restaurants and their drive times. Status on Home shows Answering, Telegram and Calendar, derived from the setup steps and the agenda source, but not the day's spend. The To do view has no today, so its screen cannot say 'late'. None of these would work without a Python change, so I left them out rather than fake them. | Home's words now differ by 32.7%, up from 30.5%, because the map names each page's contents (lamp rows, the Settings parts). New or changed words elsewhere: the plates' ledes for Plans, 'Who can get in', 'What we know', the screens' words (aria-hidden, so not counted). | The screens repeat what the cells already say, as a summary. They are aria-hidden, so a screen reader hears nothing from them, and some judges may find them redundant. | One idea's title still breaks across lines ('Silver / Falls hike') at the 46px plate step in a 285px column. Setup, Your password and the activity pages are not rebuilt; they pick up the rack from CSS only.
- [feedback 7.3] The most CRT soul after Desk Terminal. The question is typed on a big green tube, each page has its own green screen summarising it, the power-on starts from a line, and the rest is grounded graphite with measured heather, cornflower and sea caps. It loses on empty cells (Memory, Wishes), text wrapping in narrow cells, tracked-caps buttons, brass warmth and heavy boxed item cards.
- [soul 7.8] You type on the glass: the question and a block-cursor prompt sit on the Home tube, and Chat's box is a lit '> Message Vera' prompt. Every page gets its own summary screen (DUE NEXT on To do, the month on Plans, 9 parts on Settings), and the tubes warm up from a line. The graphite rack with brass numbers is heavy, and some screens only repeat their cells.
- [style 7.4] A genuinely new structure. Home is the service's sommaire, a switchboard of page cells, each with its number, a leader and a count, plus pixel pictograms. The big tube has the question typed on the glass, and every page opens the same rack with its own green screen. It is coherent and memorable. It loses points for heavy boxing (caps, borders and rules on every cell) and for cells that stretch half-empty (Memory, the Status plate), which read as a widget dashboard.
- [system 7] A real grid system. Every page is the same four-column rack of equal cells, each headed by one contents line (lamp, page number, name, leader, count), and the prompt is typed on the glass: the best 'map of the service' Home yet. It loses points for tracked caps on controls (ADD AN IDEA, SEND, TELL VERA SOMETHING), cells stretched empty to their row (Memory, the Wishes kids), Ideas and To do set as cards instead of rows, and no page keys on Home itself.
- [interaction 6.8] The Home switchboard is the round's best answer to 'find what you came for'. Every page is a cell whose head is a 44px key with a count, and ticks, lamps and the settings index sit right on Home. But the cells carry equal weight, so the squint loses priority, and the question sits at the foot of a tall tube below Next up. Controls are tracked caps. To do's Show/Search are below the fold under the tube. Home lacks the page row the other pages have, and 8 to 20 targets per page are under 44px.
- [type 6.5] The sommaire contents line (lamp, page number, name, dotted leader, count) is a true pagination device, and Settings list 991-999 as a numbered contents. But narrow cells wrap titles ('Call the dentist about / Theo', 'Silver / Falls hike'), the tracked caps buttons ('+ ADD AN IDEA') shout, and the page numbers are tiny beside 46px plate titles.
- [usability 6.2] Home as a map of every page, each with a count and a lamp, is a strong way to see what is where, and the To do boxes are 44px. But Home has no page-tab row while every other page does. On Ideas the search and kind filters sit below the radar and off the first screen, so 'show only restaurants' means hunting. Idea cards are tall and half empty (9 to a screen). The message is typed in green VT323 on the glass, so the box reads as display text. To do does not mark late, and ADD AN IDEA is in tracked caps.
- [skeptic 6.7] The boldest structural change that still works. Home is a graphite switchboard of every page, the question is typed on a big lit tube, and section colour is only a 2px cap and a number, which is truly grounded. Faults: page numbers (200, 310, 991) are costume the family never uses. Several cells stand half empty (Memory, Restaurants). The keys are tracked caps ('ADD AN IDEA'). To do's three card columns wrap every title. The nav sits in two places.

Set the keys in sentence case. Let empty cells collapse (span Memory and Restaurants into their neighbours). Make To do a one-line list, not three cramped cards. ## What the main tournament's lessons.md recorded about this family

- FAILED REACHES. (1) Videotex Switchboard (7.01, 7 promise, the wild mutant) made Home a sommaire. It failed on tracked-caps controls ('ADD AN IDEA'), half-empty stretched cells (Memory, Wishes), 46px plate titles wrapping in 285px cells, card lists, brass warmth, no page-key row on Home, and screens that repeat their cells. It failed on a plum-graphite cast (hue 304), 53 of 59 small Home targets, a second ALT access-key bar row, inline tracked mono caps in rows, a near-title first To do row, a one-sided Chat and no late marking. - CANDIDATE PRINCIPLES. (a) The machine and the family are laid out apart: one cased, lit terminal per page holds the tube, the deck, the contents and the status, and a square, matte desk holds the family's rows. The terminal is sticky, sized to its content, and folds to its foot where nothing is live. (b) Each page has one place for its controls, and every filter there is a one-tap latched key showing its state. There is never a select beside a list that does the same job. (c) Every head states its figure, as a numeral over a label or the title followed by its count, and late appears in that figure in amber words as a link to the filtered list.
