### r13-wild-3 Dessau Phosphor: main round 13, #12 of 17, mean 6.47 (today's page 4.72; in 1 judges' top fours, 4 promise votes)

## What the main tournament's lessons.md recorded about this family

- FAILED REACHES. Backlit Plate (7.8, 5 promise votes, the most) has the clearest law for scarce light: light shows only through a cut in a matte plate, and the cut's shape says what it shows (a slot for time, a round window for place, 4:3 for the screen). On screen it read as plain: "mind" barely blooms, Status is one bare column, Settings and Family are only restyled, the 4:3 windows have spare glass, and one long slate sheet has no seams. Dessau Phosphor (6.47, 4 promise votes; the style lens ranked it first at 8.2): a round phosphor tube holding each page's one live figure, four shapes with four jobs and a legend in the footer, and a huge scanlined glowing "mind". It failed on a matte Bauhaus poster below the fold, navy cards (hue 259), ochre, terracotta and pink, 31 small targets, and To do reverting to cards with ISO dates. Keep the round screen and the shape legend on graphite. Signal Catalogue (5.71): its scope pulse per plan and its LED countdown meters are lovely lit data, sunk by the walnut ground (neutral hue 73). Graft the meters onto slate and make the pulse less like an ECG. Grid Console (6.16): its light-trail spine with section-coloured nodes and the only visible afterglow are worth keeping, without the Tron floor, the extended caps or the outlined cells. Blueprint (6.16): keep the date column with dimension-line "IN 2 DAYS" marks and the 24px baseline grid, with the grid confined to the margins, and drop the violet revision clouds. Folio Switchboard (6.88): keep its figure-setting.

- CANDIDATE PRINCIPLES. (a) Every page opens on one head row: the title, the page's figure (with a stacked unit and change line), and its one green primary action. (b) State is coded by shape as well as colour, one meaning per signal, with a legend wherever lamps mean something, and red is reserved for something that has stopped. (c) A lit window's shape says what it shows, and it draws that page's own data, with marks numbered to the rows they stand for.
