# Dossier: quote (main camp)

The line's idea: a count board: each section led by one big quote or figure, read at a glance.

Every judge's words on this line, oldest first. Scores compare best within their round.

### r12-wild-4 Single Quote: main round 12, #11 of 17, mean 6.59 (today's page 4.84; in 2 judges' top fours, 6 promise votes)
What it set out to be: A trading terminal that quotes one thing at a time: the thing that matters now, set large, with every other screen a numbered key away. FamilyDB as a calm trading terminal. Each screen quotes one thing large, the way a quote screen shows one security: a condensed title, its figure in the section's colour, and under the unit a change line saying what moved or what comes next. Under each quote runs a short tape of the next few items, and everything else sits one numbered key away. A command line (FamilyDB > SETTINGS > GENERAL▌) and a function-key legend frame every page. Only live things give off phosphor light, and that light has the original tube's bloom, blur, scanlines and afterglow. The feel is exact and quiet, with a hint of the trading floor: a figure that has just arrived flashes, then settles.
Its designer's own weaknesses: To do still cannot show lateness on its own page. The tasks view gives the template no clock and no late flag, so the quote's change line reads 'first due Sun 27 Sep 19:56' rather than '2 late', and the overdue #4 row is not amber. The template already reads row.late (amber due, a 'N late' change line), which needs a one-line addition to views.task_row. Home does show lateness in amber. | Chat is still 94% green by share of colour, and Status is now 100%. The coloured area on both is tiny and what is lit there is green by design. With red off-lamps gone, Status has no companion colour beyond the violet legends. | The page's words differ by 8.5% on Home. Tape rows shorten 'was due yesterday' to 'yesterday' and 'due in 2 days, 19:56' to 'in 2 days', and the setup strip shows its first step both large and in its list. On To do the OPEN badge is hidden while only open tasks are shown. | The in-flow panels rely on ::details-content (current Chromium). Other browsers get the older overlay panel, which covers the list. | The ALT⇧ legend is right for Chrome and Firefox on Windows and Linux, not for a Mac (Ctrl⌥). Access keys 0-9 can also collide with a screen reader's or browser's own shortcuts. | Folded past weeks show a past plan only as a small slip without its time. Chat stamps lack the weekday (27 SEP 19:47), because the chat view hands the template a pre-formatted string. | For about 200ms during Next up's inverse flash the title is dim on a half-lit bar, and the tube's text colour is hard-coded (#bdffd2) to avoid a Chromium colour-mix interpolation bug. | The 3rem tile figures rise above their legends and leave dark space at the top of each board cell. The tape's 12-14px type is small on a large monitor.
- [feedback 5.3] A title with its figure in the section colour is a neat typographic device, and the one-sided quote chat with the newest line set large is fresh. The plum-violet housings are the off-period purple the owner pushes back on, it is 88% green by colour share, and 53 of 59 targets are small.
- [soul 7.4] Real terminal behaviour: a command path in the bar ending in a blinking cursor, an LED on the current key, a row of glowing lamps on Status, and Next up flashing in inverse as it arrives, with the tubes at full bloom. The plum-graphite housing and big section-coloured figures lean toward a trading floor, and Chat as a flat transcript loses her lit marks.
- [style 7.6] A confident editorial device that works on every page: a condensed title followed by its figure in the section's colour ('Ideas 12', 'Things to do 4', 'Settings 1'), a command path in the bar ('> STATUS▌'), and a lamp row with big counts on Status. The plum-graphite housing is a subtle, fresh warmth. It loses points for the busy two-row numbered bar with its ALT legend, the odd near-title size given to the first To do item and to Chat's last line, and tape type that is too small.
- [system 6.6] The 'quote one thing large, tape the rest' hierarchy is a genuine new rule that could hold on every page. The quoted figures in section colour, the compact 1300px Home and Chat's last line set large all work. Against it: a plum-graphite tint (hue 304) over every surface, tracked mono caps legends everywhere (even inline 'FOR ANYONE'), a double bar with number keys and an ALT legend, and tape rows with no aligned facts column.
- [interaction 6.3] The strongest title-to-figure hierarchy of the round ('Ideas 12', 'Things to do 4 open'). It adds a one-line filter summary chip that states the filter's state, numbered access keys for keyboard users, and a Chat whose last line is set large. Failings: 53 of 59 Home targets and 28 of 29 on Status are under 44px, the tape rows are tiny tracked mono caps, it has two header rows of chrome, the To do page shows no late state, and the To do quote sets whichever task is first due huge.
- [type 7.3] The boldest typographic idea that works. Every head is a quote: a condensed Archivo title, then the section's figure in its colour, then a stacked mono legend ('Ideas 12 IN ALL / LAST ADDED SUN 27 SEP'). The Status lamp row reads '6 working 7 off', and Settings are numbered 0·1 to 0·9. What lets it down: mono caps set inline in rows ('Pho Oregon FOR ANYONE', 'SAM', 'YESTERDAY'), a first To do row set at about 36px so row sizes are unequal, the chat's last line upsized, and tape text at 12-14px.
- [usability 5.8] The bar names every page in words, the Ideas filter pill shows its current state, and each title's figure plus change line ('Ideas 12, last added Sun 27 Sep') is a good glance. But Home has 53 of 59 targets under 44px, the worst here. The first task on To do is set as a headline as if it were special, and late is not marked. Show is folded behind a disclosure, the numbered access keys may clash with a screen reader, and the tape's 12-14px caps are small.

## What the main tournament's lessons.md recorded about this family

- FAILED REACHES. (1) Videotex Switchboard (7.01, 7 promise, the wild mutant) made Home a sommaire. It was a rack of equal page cells, each headed by a 44px key with lamp, page number, name, leader and count, with pixel pictograms and a per-page green summary screen. The judges called it the best 'where is everything' device yet. It failed on tracked-caps controls ('ADD AN IDEA'), half-empty stretched cells (Memory, Wishes), 46px plate titles wrapping in 285px cells, card lists, brass warmth, no page-key row on Home, and screens that repeat their cells. To try again: grid-auto-rows:min-content with spans, sentence-case keys, one hairline border with the 2px cap on Home cells only, one-line rows to a facts column, 32px plate titles, and setup in the first and widest cell. Also a screen should say what its cells do not (late count, next due, spend). Or graft it into the leader as Home's lower half. (2) Single Quote (6.59, 6 promise) had the round's best typographic device: title, then the section's figure in its colour, then a stacked mono legend ('Ideas 12 / LAST ADDED SUN 27 SEP'). It failed on a plum-graphite cast (hue 304), 53 of 59 small Home targets, a second ALT access-key bar row, inline tracked mono caps in rows, a near-title first To do row, a one-sided Chat and no late marking. To try again: neutral graphite, one bar row, a 15px tape step at 44px, and quote the next-due item in the head, not a big row. (3) Metered's needle meter (below) is the third reach. (4) Zenith Desk (5.91, style's #2 at 7.8) set Vera at the zenith with plans as stars and a meridian thread through Chat. It lost on unlabelled stars, a teal wash, a parchment serif, maroon bubbles and 40 small targets. - CANDIDATE PRINCIPLES. (a) The machine and the family are laid out apart: one cased, lit terminal per page holds the tube, the deck, the contents and the status, and a square, matte desk holds the family's rows.
