### r12-idea-2 Desk Terminal: main round 12, #1 of 17, mean 8.34 (today's page 4.84; in 8 judges' top fours, 7 promise votes)
What it set out to be: One real terminal stands at the left of the family's desk on every page, the only lit, cased and rounded thing there: the machine states how the page stands and holds its controls; the matte desk beside it holds only the family's rows. On every page one real terminal stands at the left of the family's desk. It is a graphite body holding the page's one lit tube, its console deck and, where a page has three bands or more, a numbered Contents plate, and it ends in a foot that says how the machine stands. The machine states, the desk holds: the square, matte slate desk carries only the family's one-line rows, so the tube's light (bloom, blur, scanlines, a warm-up from a bright line, glowing lamps) is back at full strength where attention belongs, and the rest of the page is the calmest yet.
Its designer's own weaknesses: Pages with nothing live or to tune (the new-idea form, Wishes, Family, Memory, an idea's page) keep a terminal that is only the status foot: a small graphite box at the top left with an empty column under it. | The status foot shows today's spend only on Status. Elsewhere it shows the limit a day, because the harness gives templates no spend; the shipped page would need the every_page context processor to hand it over. | Home's terminal is about 930px tall, so its foot sits below the fold at 1280×900. The Ideas terminal fills the 900px viewport exactly, so a family with more kinds or people would scroll inside it. | The month view's trace draws only the days its grid still shows from today (6 in the demo), so it reads thin; the full four-week trace is on the Plans list, which the render does not shoot. | Her last line from the chat is on Home's glass only while the view counts it as recent. In today's demo it is too old, so today's Home shows it and this one does not. | On the phone the tube and foot are moved visually after the list with CSS order, so for those two the Tab order no longer matches the drawing. On the desktop it matches. | The Settings overview lists the pages twice: as the bank on the deck, and as rows with their states on the desk. | Up to 17.7% of each page's words differ from today (the Plans controls moved, Ideas rows lost their meta line, the edit fold's words became a pencil key), and the family's wording would follow. | VT323's curly apostrophe in "What’s" draws high on the glass. On the phone, the drive column truncates long idea titles.
- [feedback 8.6] The biggest owner-shaped step this round. One real terminal on every page, in a thin graphite case, holds the question typed on the glass ('What's on your mind?' with a cursor), warms up from a bright line, and ends in a lit 'Ready' foot. A numbered Contents plate on a rail of rings sits beside a calm slate desk of one-line rows with rails and a NOW lamp. The light sits where the machine is and grounded colour everywhere else, and it changes every page (Home, Status, Plans, To do, Chat), not a corner.
- [soul 8.6] The most complete return of the tube on this panel. Every page has one real terminal, and its glass draws that page's own data. The question is typed on the glass under a block cursor, To do shows ASCII late bars with amber on the phosphor, Status shows the spend, Plans a trace and Ideas the radar. It warms up from a bright line, the latched key has a lit LED, and the desk beside it stays matte. Light is where attention belongs on Home, To do, Status and Plans alike.
- [style 8] The clearest new identity of the round, and it holds as a system on every page: one cased, lit terminal stands at the left and the matte desk beside it holds only the family's rows. The question is typed on the glass with a cursor, To do's tube draws Late, This week and Later as phosphor bars, Status's tube holds the spend, and the status foot gives the machine a presence on every page. Numbered band rings and a Contents plate with leaders are good typesetting. The light is back exactly where the machine is.
- [system 8.5] The first entrant that is a page template, not a palette. On every page the machine column holds the tube, the latched-key deck, a numbered 'On this page' plate and a status foot. The matte, square desk holds the family's one-line rows, with leaders, column heads and ringed band numbers. 'Lit versus matte' becomes layout, and the tube's warm-up from a bright line brings the original's light back exactly where attention belongs. Held back by a cramped Home composer (chips wrap in the ~400px column), an inner scrollbar in the Ideas terminal, an almost empty terminal on the form page, and the Settings pages listed twice.
- [interaction 8.6] Changes how every page works, not only how it looks. Filters on Ideas, To do and Plans are one-tap latched keys with a visible LED state, one control per filter. A status foot on every page says whether Vera works and what she costs. The Contents plate and the To do tube (Late / This week / Later / No date) read at a glance. All 12 ideas are above the fold, there are 0 small targets on every page, and Home is about 1450px. What it loses: on inner pages the lit tube and its controls come before the h1 in the sight line and in the DOM, so title-then-action is weaker, and the Contents plate repeats the band heads.
- [type 7.5] A disciplined listing: dotted leaders run to fixed Time and 'in N days' sub-columns, WHO/DUE and DRIVE/FOR/# heads sit over tabular figures, all 12 ideas show on one screen, and VT323 stays on the glass. It loses some hierarchy because page titles are only about 2rem next to their icon, the months in 'Thu 1 Oct' are in denim link colour on text that is not a link, and drive and direction share one column.
- [usability 9] Every filter works the same way: one-tap latched keys for Kind, For and Status on Ideas, Open, Done, Cancelled and All on To do, and List/Month and Earlier/Later on Plans. So Restaurants, overdue and a month are one click each. The To do tube counts Late 2, This week, Later and No date; 'was due' is in amber words; the numbered bands and the Contents plate give counts, with '2 late' as a link. The bar has words, no target is under 44px, row text is about 17px, and Home fits in about 1.5 screens. Costs: Home's message box is narrow in the terminal column, the Ideas terminal scrolls inside itself at 900px, the phone's Tab order no longer follows the layout (CSS order), and a pencil alone hides 'complete, or snooze'.
- [skeptic 7.8] This is the only newcomer whose change shows on every page: one cased terminal at the left holds the single lit tube at full bloom, the controls and a status foot, and the matte slate desk beside it holds the family's rows. Faults: the greeting's big question has become a small VT323 line on the glass (with a stray high apostrophe), so Home's largest heading is now 'Finish setting up'. The Contents plate repeats the band heads. 'Oct' in denim reads as a link. Status links to itself from its own foot. The Ideas filter bank fills the whole viewport.
