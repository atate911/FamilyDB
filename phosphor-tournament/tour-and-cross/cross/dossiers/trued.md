# Dossier: the track series (Track Diagram)

The idea: the family's week drawn as a signal box's illuminated track diagram: steel lines on slate boards with a lamp at each stop, drawn only where there is an order (Coming up, To do, the setup steps, the chat); the line language the owner called 'high style' in Signal Box (round 7).

Every judge's words on every design of this lineage, in every round it was judged, oldest first (main tournament rounds, then the tour's). Scores compare best within their round; 'today's page' is the yardstick.

### r7-idea-2 Signal Box: main round 7, #4 of 12, mean 6.99 (today's page 3.9; in 6 judges' top fours, 0 promise votes)
What it set out to be: The control room's mimic line: every sequence in the family's week hangs from one blue-pewter track, each stop a slate plate with its lamp and its time in a timetable margin, and the phosphor is the lamp that means now. A signal box or power-station desk, where green VDUs sat beside a mimic panel. Everything FamilyDB shows in time (the next plans, what is due, the conversation, setup, activity) hangs from one 2px blue-pewter track. Each stop is a flat slate plate with a lamp on the track, and its time hangs in a right-aligned timetable margin. The page's own Status lamps become one legend for the whole app: hollow ring for to come, lit sage for done, amber for needs a look, red for off, accent for you are here, and #6dff9c only for now and live. It should feel calm, legible and mechanical, like a desk that tells you at a glance what is running and what comes next, with the phosphor as the one lamp that is on.
Its designer's own weaknesses: The colour change is modest. Only 12.5% of Status pixels, 20.3% of Home below the fold and 14.8% of Chat differ from today by more than 5 dE, well under the lead's 35% aim. The slate plates are about 4.5 dE from today's charcoal panels, and the black between them is today's black. | Slate at this lightness is within 1.5 dE of GitHub Dark's #161b22, like every slate entrant, so a judge may still read the plates as a borrowed theme. | The CSS is at 14,923 of 15,000 characters, so the brief's Wishes (rank inside the node), Memory log, the 'What has changed' log and month-view event plates with kind rings were not built. Wishes and Memory look as today, and month events keep today's cyan-edged chips. | Lines everywhere can read as a 'timeline component' or a git graph to a skeptic, especially Status's three vertical rails. | Where a line ends without a way-on station (Status's 'Who answers' and 'Connected to', the Plans list), the track runs about 15 to 20px past the last lamp instead of ending on it. | On Home's amber setup plate, the 1px siding before each step link reads a little like a dash in front of the words. | The Ideas monitor still wraps '#7 Pumpkin patch at Bi-Mart / farm'. It is the brightest thing on the page, and Ideas stays 88% green. | Home is 1940px at 1280, 90px over the brief's 1850 target, though shorter than today's 2111. | The To do page still has 22 of 97 targets under 44px, inside the Edit folds' forms. A done task's box inside its plate is today's square while its lamp lights; that state is not in the demo. | The 'today' lamp at the start of the fortnight line has no label, because no new words may be added, so its meaning rests on the lamp legend. | The phone keeps a narrower margin rather than folding the times into the plates as the brief asked, which costs width on a 390px screen (Chat's bubbles are about 260px).
