# The feature ledger: index

270 features from 104 designs of the main tournament's thirteen rounds, found by scouts who looked at every design's pages and read every judge. GEMS come from designs that finished in the lower half of their round (173): the small good ideas lost with their package. Each lineage's graft takes at least one gem every round. A design's files and screenshots are in tour/gallery/index.json. One line per feature; the full entry (files, shot, praise, caveats) is in ledger.md under its number, and each lineage's shortlist in ledger-for-<lineage>.md.
