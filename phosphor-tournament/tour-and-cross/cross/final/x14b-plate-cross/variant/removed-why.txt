Plate, Engraved (x14a-plate-cross): what this design removes, and why. (removed.txt is written by
the lint on every check and lists the mechanics; this says the reasons.) Everything Backlit Plate
removed stays removed, for its reasons (monitor cases, the Ideas Kind select and tabs, the filter
selects, the cards' secondary meta on the Ideas list, the cobalt wash).

- The per-page READY chip in every page head: folded into one status foot engraved into the
  plate's bottom edge on every page (pinhole, Ready, who answers, today's spend of the limit, and
  the way to Status), so the machine's state is said once, in one place, with more in it.
- The 4:3 aspect of the screen windows: each window is sized to its words.
- The settings index's rounded icon tiles (section cards) and its "Needs a look" pill: each part
  is an engraved row under a rule head (The family, The machine, Sign-in), and needing a look is
  a half-amber pinhole with amber words.
- Home's "Next up" window no longer carries the radar inside it: the radar is its own round
  window cut over the screen's corner. Its words say "3 more on the slot" (the Coming up slot
  shows them) instead of "on the radar".
- Home's Coming up DAY / AT / IN column heads: each row now leads with its engraved day numeral,
  so the column needs no head; the first plan is a row too (with a lit "Next"), so the count in
  the head matches the rows.
- The Plans list's DAY / AT / IN heads and its rule count: the count is the page head's figure.
- The To do list's "4 shown" on the Open rule: the count is the page head's figure ("4 open ·
  3 late"), said once.
- Status's long lede about today's spend: the spend window says it; the lede is the question.
- The month's today pinhole: today is a small lit window round its numeral instead.
- base.html's DM Sans preload: the page sets no DM Sans.

Plate, Channelled (x14b-plate-cross), the cross of Plate, Engraved, gives up further:
- The stencil's leak cut under 'plan': it read as an underline or a typo to every judge. E1's
  halo carries the word, and its light falls as an edgeless scanlined pool on the box's lip.
- The placards 'Plans · rings 1·2·4 wk' and 'Plans · 1 wk/div': the radar's rings are labelled
  on the glass ('1 wk', '2 wk', '4 wk') and the slot's axis already says 'Now', '1 wk' ...;
  the others are now plain words ('Due dates, two weeks either side of today').
- Status's bar meter and its two placards: the spend is a round dial (round = range), and one
  plain line under the bevel says what the trace and the dashes show.
- The violet, ochre and cyan starter notches: one steel edge; the destination is 'lands in ...'.
- The cyan bar on the month's plan chips: a 2px edge in the plans ink mixed half toward steel.
- The Ideas 'For anyone · Any but dropped' disclosure and its keys: For and Status are two
  visible selects beside Search, applied by its Search key, so one control per filter.
- Coming up's '4 plans' count: 'Next, then 3 more'.
