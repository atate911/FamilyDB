## Departures (Terminus)

The page is a station at night. Vera's line is a 1.5px steel rail down Home's left. It runs from her
screen to the box (the stop you are at, a lit ring). Then come the ways to start (hollow steel
stops, each key edged and washed 6% in the colour of the page it usually lands on, its pictogram in
that colour), Continue (a lit stop) and Finish setting up (an amber ring). The line ends on a T
buffer: a 22px steel bar with two bolts.

Every rail ends on a T buffer, and one mark always means one thing. Vera's line ends on a plain
buffer. To do's rail runs down to the foot of the left column and ends on the buffer that carries
the machine's lamp, the Ready foot. That foot is an 8px breathing phosphor lamp (ringed amber or
red while status_light says so), "Ready", who answers and the day's limit in Plex Mono, and Status
at the right. It stands level with the interchange across the page. Settings' directory ends on
the same buffer at Setup.

One lit wire, 2px phosphor with a two-layer halo, leaves the box's ring for the departures board.
When the page loads, one afterglow runs along it (static under reduced motion). The right column
hangs from a steel branch through each section's roundel (its enamel at 80%), with each kid as a 9px
rose stop. It ends at a "Change here for" interchange: a steel rail with 30px enamel roundels for
Memory (rose), Family (clay) and Settings (bone). Each roundel is a whole link with its name and a
Plex Mono figure.

One light. Phosphor is only on the mark and caret, the title's lit word, the box's ring, the wire,
the boards' glass, the NOW and Ready lamps, Send / Add it, focus, and the Status contents' "you are
here" lamp. Amber is only words, the setup ring and a warn lamp, never a ring on a list. A page
head's action is a plate in its section's enamel. Row hover everywhere is a steel wash (13%) with a
2px sage edge. On the glass it is an 11% steel wash with the destination underlined. Nothing on a
board is underlined at rest.

Board rows are 70% phosphor without halo. Only NEXT UP, the next time (VT323 3rem, hot core) and the
first row's time run hot. What the board stands on (the calendar note) is engraved in its case's
chin. The trace is 6.4rem tall and its ticks fill about 70% of it, with the next tick 3px and hot.
It has VT323 day numerals at 1.3rem, Plex Mono week labels at 13px and weekends shaded as station
gaps, and nothing else moves on it. On a phone each row is a one-line name over its time and date in
Plex Mono, and the radar sits beside the clock, so the clock and three rows are above the fold.

Ideas has the radar (10rem) as a full-width header board, as Plans has its departures. "Save a
thought" is a link at the right of the tabs. Kind words are ink, and a kind's colour lives only in
its ring (1.5px and a 12% fill).

Dated lists (To do, Home's To do, Plans' list) lead each row with the engraved day numeral. It is
Plex Mono 500 1.5rem in bone, tabular, with the month over the weekday in Archivo condensed 600
0.75rem in ink-2. It sits in a 4.4rem column with the rail to its right, and a row with no date
shows an en dash. Each To do row has one mark, the square tick, and late is said in amber words
only. Forms carry a quiet steel rule (45%) down the left and one lit stop at Add it, joined to the
button by a 12px phosphor stub.

Type: Archivo condensed 600 (75%) for the title ("Tell Vera where we're *headed.*", 2.25rem on one
line, or 1.875rem on a phone), page names and section signs (1.5rem, each with its figure in Plex
Mono capitals). Archivo 100% is for reading, Plex Mono for figures and legends, and VT323 only on
glass (with Plex Mono for board rows on a phone).
