## What the judges said of the titles

- t1 heat a, feedback: Best: "What's the plan?" (t1-track-refine, t1-timetable-refine, t1-pocket-refine). It is the family's own kitchen-table phrase, it asks for exactly what FamilyDB keeps, and the lit word is the job itself. It is short enough to hold one line on any phone. Its only cost is a slight echo of the placeholder's "Plans for the weekend". Close second: "What's next for us?" (t1-track-graft, t1-timetable-graft, t1-pocket-graft). It speaks as a household ("us"), leans forward in time and is a warm invitation, but "next" says schedule more than ideas or wishes, and the lit word is less the job than "plan". "What shall we line up?" (t1-track-revival) is clever and set beautifully large. But "shall" is a little stiff, and the lit word names the lineage's motif, not the family's job, so it reads as a pun. The fix is "What shall we plan?" with "plan" lit, which keeps the "we" and the job word at Revival's display size. Fails: "What's on your mind?", kept by r10-idea-3, r13-mut-4, r10-wild-3, r11-wild-1, the three dice mutants and both yardsticks (lit inside Desk Terminal's tube). It is a generic chat opener that says nothing of weekends, plans, reminders or wishes.
