Each round is its own page, with every design's screenshots (by page, or by design), the large view, and what each judge said, including their suggested fixes. The round notes tell what the round taught.

Scores are out of 10: the mean of the judges' marks that round. Today's page is the yardstick every design is measured against, and its own score moves between rounds because the judges and the bar moved. The pictures shown here are each round's winner on Home.
