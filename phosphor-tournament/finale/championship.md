## What each lens said of the four finalists

### Grid Console Keyed (`r20-mut-3`)

- **feedback** 8: Fine rails and keyed section heads run through every page (Signal Box lineage), cyan, violet and amber stay low-chroma and bounce off the green. The bloom on the lit key word, the monitors and the floor-grid lamp bring the tube's light back where it belongs. The condensed headings and numbered tabs change the page's type, not only its colour. Risk: many lines and glows in places.
  - fix asked: Keep the rails, keyed section heads and the glow on the key word, monitors and floor lamp. Thin the extra lines on Chat and Ideas and soften the grid floor so only the lit parts glow.
- **soul** 7.7: Keeps the monitors and a bloomed MIND, and adds lit rails, a NOW lamp, a perspective grid floor under the glass and an UP lamp in the foot. Condensed type with keyed numerals gives a clear system. The grid lines are slightly busy, and it reads less as a green screen than as a console.
  - fix asked: Keep the lamp rails, the NOW marker and the keyed nav. Thin or fade the background grid so it does not wash the page, and keep the glow on MIND and the screens.
- **style** 8.3: Condensed grotesque headings, a lit green MIND, a glowing rail with keyed section nodes, a perspective grid floor and a gauge with real bloom. It brings back the tube's light where attention belongs, and it carries across Home, Status, Plans, To do and Settings. It reads as one considered system with a clear console mood. It is a little busy in places.
  - fix asked: Keep the condensed headings, the lit rail and the gauge bloom. Thin the grid floor and the bottom UP node so they do not compete, and give the Ideas head a bit more air before the tube.
- **system** 8.4: The most complete system. Numbered keyed nav, a condensed display face with one lit word, a lit vertical rail with nodes, one card grid on Home, Status, Settings and Ideas, and a dial gauge on Status. Glow stays on the key word, the mark, the dial and the rail. Rows, rails and cards change on every page, not just the hero. Minor cost: lots of thin rail lines and some native-looking select styling.
  - fix asked: Keep the keyed nav, the rail with NOW node, the dial, and the lit key word. Thin the rail lines where they cross dense cards. Style the Ideas Kind/Status selects like the other controls. Keep the Add button's glow at the Home Send level.
- **interaction** 7.4: The keyed, numbered nav with icons, the rails and the section markers help orientation. The condensed headline is a good first stop and the buttons are clearly labelled. Rails, grid and markers run on every page, which makes it busy, and the phone's chips and hero are cramped.
  - fix asked: Keep the keyed nav numbers and the condensed headline. Remove some of the rail ornament, so each section gets one marker. Give the phone chips room, and balance the card grid on Ideas.
- **type** 8: A condensed uppercase headline paired with tracked mono numerals and designators (00 HOME, 02, 12 IN ALL). A convincing system that holds on every page. Body and list text stay legible and the figures line up. The display type is more restrained than mut-2, so the hierarchy is a little flatter, and some small labels are tiny.
  - fix asked: Keep the system of numerals and tracked mono designators. Raise the 10-11px labels by a step and enlarge the page titles slightly for more contrast with body text. Keep tabular figures in the To do dues.
- **usability** 7.7: Home is readable: a moderate headline, a full-width SEND, labelled From select and a clear checkbox. To do uses a dated rail with a 'now' marker and a green Add button, and the pills and search are obvious. Status and Settings are consistent card grids. Costs: tiny mono numbered nav ('03 PLANS') and a little grid noise.
  - fix asked: Keep the timeline To do and the Add button. Write the nav as 'Home, Vera' with the numbers dimmed, raise the tiny mono labels to at least 12px, and bring the Status section heads closer to the list.
- **skeptic** 7.3: Condensed headings, coloured numerals, a keyed accent per page and lit hairlines carry the change into content, and the tube light (halo, gauge, floor glow) stays on the glass. Weak spots: the faint grid and rail lines add clutter, and the teal tint on the ground edges toward an editor theme.
  - fix asked: Keep the keyed page accents and the condensed headings with coloured figures. Drop the grid and the perspective floor to a trace, neutralise the teal ground toward green-black, and keep bright green only on glass, mark and send.

### Desk Terminal, Even Dock (`r20-mut-1`)

- **feedback** 7.4: Grounded and coherent: a left dock of monitor, pager and status on every page, a typed prompt on the glass, a bar-scope readout and even rows. CRT soul is strong and the colours are restrained. It reads as a close cousin of today's structure, so less change in the content than the leaders.
  - fix asked: Keep the typed prompt on the glass, the scope readout and the even dock. Add the rail and key language of the leaders to the page body, and vary the section heads so the pages differ more from today's.
- **soul** 8.6: Puts the most of the page on glass. The question is typed as a lit line on a green screen with a LISTENING status. Next up is a plotted bar scope, the radar has numbered points, and Status has a limit-flagged gauge. The glow is concentrated on screens and the page around them is calm and grounded, so the phosphor feels real and changes whole pages.
  - fix asked: Keep the glass-typed question, the plotted scope and the gauge. Let the typed headline's glow be a little less wide, and make the bar plot's axes a touch brighter so it reads at a glance.
- **style** 6.8: A polished dock layout with a left glass column, a tube that holds real data, lit rows and good list rhythm. It is tidy and well made but predictable, with little new personality in the type and colour.
  - fix asked: Keep the dock and the data-bearing tube. Add one more distinctive typographic choice, a display face or numerals, and let the colour companions show on more of the page body.
- **system** 7.4: A calm, tidy refinement: side dock plus main column, a desk-terminal glass with a Listening strip, even type steps, hairline ledgers and good controls. It is more of the existing page with better structure than a new system, so it risks the 'nearly identical' verdict, though the layout change is visible across pages.
  - fix asked: Keep the Listening glass and the dock layout. Add one stronger typographic move (a bigger section numeral or a display weight for page titles) so it reads differently from today on Plans, To do and Settings.
- **interaction** 7.9: The 'What's on your mind?' glass line with a Listening status gives the page an obvious entry point. On the phone it stacks the field, a full-width Send, the mic and chips well, and the dropdown is clear. The Ideas table (drive time, who it's for, number) is easy to scan. The page differs from today's mostly in Home and the list rows, so it reads as refined more than new.
  - fix asked: Keep the glass question with the Listening line, the full-width Send and the Ideas table. Carry the table-rule and numbered-section treatment onto Plans and To do, so the change is not only on Home.
- **type** 6.6: A pixel-mono headline set on the glass and tidy tabular list columns. These are good, tasteful details but mostly an extension of today's type. The sans body and headings change little, so the family would see only a modest difference.
  - fix asked: Keep the glass headline and the tabular columns. Add a real display step for page titles beyond the existing sans, and set section heads with a distinct tracked label style.
- **usability** 8: Easiest to use. The Home order is question, textarea, From, mic, big Send, ways to start. Each list page has a clear primary action top right (Add a task, Add a plan). Late tasks carry an amber 'was due 6 days ago' chip. Desktop nav is labelled (Memory, Family, Status, Settings) and the Settings sidebar means you never lose your place. The select has a check and a chevron. The monitor sidebar repeats a Ready line on every page.
  - fix asked: Keep the layout (monitor aside plus content), the labelled bar and the primary button top right. Drop the repeated Ready/Status footer from every aside, and make the Settings aside and list the same width so they don't repeat.
- **skeptic** 7.5: The question is typed on a lit glass, a gauge, scope or chart dock repeats on every page, and the green is bright only on glass, send and live dots. Lists get dotted leaders, amber late marks and a rail. The system shows across Status, Plans, To do and Settings. Weak spots: the dock takes a third of the width everywhere, and the Home hero is heavy.
  - fix asked: Keep the glass dock, the typed headline and the leader rows. Narrow the dock or let it collapse on short pages. Tone down the Home headline halo one step. Give the 'NOW' rail a little more companion colour in the Plans list.

### Track Diagram Rail Keys (`r19-mut-2`)

- **feedback** 7.8: The rail and track language is the most legible: To do's lamps and 'was due' margin, Chat hanging from Vera, Status rails. The lit 'mind' has a good halo and the gauge and week dial are lovely. The serif headings and counts are a real typesetting gain. Faults: the full-width green Add button on To do is glare, and the serif sits a little off the CRT feel.
  - fix asked: Keep the rails, the lit 'mind', the gauge and the week dial. Replace the full-width green Add bar on To do with a compact primary button, and test a more machine-like heading face beside the serif.
- **soul** 7.2: The scan-lined, haloed word 'mind', the lit rail node, the serif heading with a glowing count and the lamp-keyed filters are tasteful and lively. The page is calm and clearly different from today. The glass is today's, and the scanline word reads a little noisy.
  - fix asked: Keep the lit rail, the serif figure and the lamp-keyed filters. Calm the scanlines on 'mind' so they read as bloom, and give the hero monitor more content.
- **style** 7.9: A warm serif display paired with the green gives the page real editorial taste. Creamy numerals, a track-diagram rail and the glowing dot-keys on filters are lovely. The halo on 'mind' stays, and the page is calm. The serif is less Phosphor than the condensed faces, and some pages are quieter.
  - fix asked: Keep the serif and cream numerals and the dot-keyed filters. Restore a bit more phosphor glow on the key words of Status and To do, and make the track-diagram rail more consistent across Plans and Settings.
- **system** 7.8: A rail and track-diagram idea carried through chat, To do and Status. A serif display face with counts, LED toggle chips and a styled open select are well judged. The serif, mono and sans mix is slightly eclectic, and the full-width neon Add button on To do and the pixelated MIND are loud.
  - fix asked: Keep the rail dots on To do and chat and the LED chips. Drop the full-width neon Add button to a standard primary button, and de-pixelate the Home MIND. Settle on two faces (serif display plus one sans) and let mono carry only data.
- **interaction** 7.1: The rail through the chat and To do turns the lists into a readable sequence. The serif page names with their counts give an honest glance read. On the phone the From select wraps onto two lines and the textarea is clipped to about two lines. That is a real comprehension flaw in the key control.
  - fix asked: Keep the rail in the chat and To do, and the serif page names with counts. On the phone, shorten the From select's label so it stays on one line, and let the textarea show at least three lines.
- **type** 7.8: A high-contrast serif for titles, with large figures (Ideas 12, Status 6) paired with a clean sans and tracked small caps. It is the most elegant pairing and it differs from today across the page. Serif on phosphor is a slight stretch, and the glitchy 'mind' word is fussy at small size.
  - fix asked: Keep the serif and big-figure pairing. Remove the glitch texture from 'mind' or limit it to one word. Make sure the serif's small sizes (the Who answers heads) keep enough weight.
- **usability** 7.4: Best state-at-a-glance. To do shows 'was due 6 days ago' on an amber rail, a full-width Add something to do button, and counts like '4 open, 3 late' and '9 parts, 1 needs a look'. Faults: on the phone Home the textarea is clipped (the placeholder is cut off) and the From select wraps onto two lines. The Plans clock dial adds nothing.
  - fix asked: Keep the due rail and the counts. On the phone, give the textarea a minimum height of about 4 lines and put the From select on one line (shorten its label or let it take the row). Drop the Plans clock dial or give it a job.
- **skeptic** 7: The serif display with counted figures, the track-diagram rail and the glowing key word make a fresh, calm pairing, and grounded companions sit well. The serif is not a CRT face, so some will say it loses the phosphor feel, and the green hero slab on To do is glare.
  - fix asked: Keep the figure-led titles and the rail with its lit now-node. Replace the full-width green Add button on To do with a normal-size primary. Consider a mono or slab alternative to the serif so it sits closer to the tube.

### Lit Index, Ledger Rail (`r20-mut-2`)

- **feedback** 7: The boldest typographic change: giant condensed numbered headings, a ledger rail and hatched swatches that alter the content of every page. But the huge white-and-green MIND and heavy headings are more contrasty than the owner asked for, and the tube's soft glow is mostly confined to the monitor.
  - fix asked: Keep the condensed numbered index, ledger rail and hatched swatches. Calm the headline size and brightness, drop the page-wide white, and extend a halo from the monitor to the lit key word.
- **soul** 6.6: Huge condensed type with a giant green MIND is a bold typographic move and shows across all pages. But the solid bloomed block is glare on the page, not light where attention belongs. The tube is only a sticker on an editorial layout.
  - fix asked: Keep the condensed type system and mono caps labels. Shrink MIND well below hero scale and let it glow only at its edges, so it is a lit word and not a green slab.
- **style** 8.1: The boldest typographic identity: a huge condensed MIND with a halo, a numbered ledger index, giant Settings entries and hatched kind swatches. It is memorable and clearly different from today on every page. The scale is slightly shouty on Home, and the tube is the only glass.
  - fix asked: Keep the ledger numbering, the hatched swatches and the glowing MIND. Drop the heading scale one step on Home and Settings so the type does not shout, and carry a little tube glow onto the other pages' key words.
- **system** 8: The boldest typographic system: a huge condensed grotesque with mono labels, hard rules with coloured tabs, indexed A/B/C/D nav, and numbered Settings. It changes the whole page and reads as one language on every sheet. The oversized lit MIND? is glare for daily use, and the phone hero eats the viewport.
  - fix asked: Keep the condensed display plus mono system and the coloured-tab rules. Cap the hero at about two thirds of its size, especially on the phone, and lower the bloom on MIND?. Keep the large-type page heads and the index numbering.
- **interaction** 8.4: The squint read is clearest of all: a huge lit 'MIND?', then the field, then the green SEND, then a numbered index of sections. The change runs through every page (numbered tabs, big condensed names, ledger rules, hatch icons on ideas). Targets are generous and the focus rings are strong. Costs: the phone hero eats a lot of height, and the underline field is a weaker affordance.
  - fix asked: Keep the huge lit question, the numbered index and the ledger rules. Cap the phone hero height so the Send button sits higher. Give the underline field a faint box or fill so it reads as a field.
- **type** 8.5: The most complete typographic overhaul. A condensed grotesque display face with a clear scale from giant MIND to page titles to tracked mono labels, numbered running heads and Ideas rows set as a ledger with big condensed titles. Changes run across Home, Chat and Ideas. The cost is that the MIND headline is large and glaring.
  - fix asked: Keep the condensed display scale, the numbered ledger and the tracked mono labels. Cap the MIND headline size (about 70%) and tone its bloom so the type does not glare. Use tabular figures for the dates and set the body text a little larger.
- **usability** 6.4: Strong hierarchy and a clear Send. But the A/B/C/D and 00-05 nav letters are cryptic for a family. The huge condensed headline pushes the composer down. List/Month are bare text with no tab affordance. The select field has no visible chevron, and the Settings page is shouty.
  - fix asked: Replace the A/B/C/D and 00-05 letters with plain words. Cut the headline height by roughly a third on the phone. Give List/Month a visible tab shape, and show a chevron on the select.
- **skeptic** 6.6: The most visible typographic overhaul, with huge condensed headings, ledger numbering and per-section colour bars, visible on every page. But the giant glowing MIND? is glare, the opposite of 'a little less bright and contrasty', and the headings shout over small mono captions.
  - fix asked: Keep the ledger index and colour bars. Cut the Home headline to about half its size and halve its glow, and keep the hero a size step below shouting. Lift the 10px caption sizes.
